## 一、科技与伦理

### 1. AI and Ethics（人工智能与伦理）

Not only has artificial intelligence transformed the way we work, but it has also raised profound ethical questions. Machines can now make decisions that affect people's lives, from hiring to medical diagnosis. The difficulty is that AI has no conscience; it simply follows the data it was given. Consequently, the responsibility for its consequences rests with us. It is precisely because the stakes are so high that we must approach AI with both excitement and caution. The future will be shaped less by the technology itself than by the choices we make today.

人工智能不仅改变了我们的工作方式，也引发了深刻的伦理问题。如今机器能做出影响人们生活的决定，从招聘到医疗诊断。困难在于，AI 没有良知，它只是遵循被输入的数据。因此，其后果的责任在我们。正因为风险如此之高，我们对待 AI 必须既兴奋又审慎。塑造未来的，与其说是技术本身，不如说是我们今天的抉择。

**词与短语**：raise ethical questions（引发伦理问题）、rests with us（在于我们）、the stakes are so high（风险如此之高）

### 2. The Digital Divide（数字鸿沟）

The digital divide refers to the gap between those who have access to technology and those who do not. While some enjoy the benefits of high-speed internet, others remain cut off from the digital world. This divide is not merely about convenience; it is about opportunity. Education, jobs and services increasingly require digital access. Consequently, closing this gap has become a matter of social justice. What was once a luxury is now a necessity, and those left behind will fall further behind.

数字鸿沟指的是能接触技术者与不能者之间的差距。一些人享受高速互联网的便利，另一些人则与数字世界隔绝。这一鸿沟不只是便利问题，而是机会问题。教育、就业和服务越来越依赖数字接入。因此，弥合这一差距已成为社会公正问题。曾经的奢侈品如今成了必需品，被落下的人只会落后得更远。

**词与短语**：refer to（指的是）、be cut off from（与……隔绝）、a matter of social justice（社会公正问题）

### 3. Automation and Jobs（自动化与就业）

Few forces have reshaped the economy as profoundly as automation. It has made production faster and cheaper, yet it has also displaced countless workers. The question is no longer whether jobs will change, but how we will adapt. History suggests that new technologies create new jobs even as they destroy old ones. Nevertheless, the transition can be painful for those without the right skills. What is needed, therefore, is not resistance to change but investment in education and retraining.

很少有力量能像自动化那样深刻地重塑经济。它让生产更快更廉价，却也取代了无数工人。问题已不再是工作是否会改变，而是我们将如何适应。历史表明，新技术在摧毁旧工作的同时也会创造新工作。然而，对缺乏相应技能的人来说，转型可能是痛苦的。因此，我们需要的不是抗拒变化，而是投资教育和再培训。

**词与短语**：as profoundly as（像……那样深刻地）、displace workers（取代工人）、investment in education（投资教育）

### 4. Data and Privacy（数据与隐私）

In the digital economy, data has been called the new oil. Companies collect vast amounts of information about us, often without our full awareness. This data powers services we enjoy, but it also creates risks. The line between convenience and surveillance is becoming dangerously blurred. If we are not careful, we may trade away our privacy for small benefits. Protecting personal data is not a technical issue alone; it is a matter of fundamental rights.

在数字经济中，数据被称为"新石油"。公司在往在我们不完全知情的情况下收集海量信息。这些数据支撑着我们享受的服务，却也带来风险。便利与监控之间的界限正变得危险地模糊。如果我们不小心，可能会为蝇头小利出卖隐私。保护个人数据不只是技术问题，更是基本权利问题。

**词与短语**：the new oil（新石油）、blur the line（模糊界限）、trade away（出卖）

### 5. Technology and Human Connection（科技与人的连接）

Technology promises to connect us, yet many feel lonelier than ever. We have hundreds of online friends but few people we can truly confide in. Screens have replaced face-to-face conversation in many homes. This paradox lies at the heart of modern life: the more connected we are, the more isolated we can become. The solution is not to reject technology but to use it deliberately. Real connection requires time, attention and presence, none of which can be replaced by an app.

科技承诺连接我们，然而许多人却比以往更孤独。我们有数百个网友，却鲜有可真正倾诉的人。在许多家庭，屏幕取代了面对面的交谈。这一悖论是现代生活的核心：连接得越多，我们反而可能越孤立。解决办法不是拒绝科技，而是有意识地使用它。真正的连接需要时间、专注和在场，这些都无法被一个应用取代。

**词与短语**：confide in（向……倾诉）、at the heart of（在……的核心）、use it deliberately（有意识地使用）

## 二、社会与政治

### 6. The Role of Government（政府的角色）

The proper role of government has been debated for centuries. Some argue that it should do no more than protect rights and enforce contracts. Others believe it must actively promote welfare and reduce inequality. In practice, most societies fall somewhere in between. The challenge is to strike a balance between freedom and security, between individual initiative and collective responsibility. A government that does too much can stifle innovation, while one that does too little can fail the vulnerable.

政府的恰当角色已被争论了几个世纪。有人认为它只需保护权利、执行契约，有人认为它必须积极促进福利、减少不平等。实际上，大多数社会介于两者之间。挑战在于在自由与安全、个人主动性与集体责任之间取得平衡。政府做得太多会扼杀创新，做得太少则会辜负弱势群体。

**词与短语**：be debated for centuries（被争论几个世纪）、strike a balance（取得平衡）、fail the vulnerable（辜负弱势群体）

### 7. Democracy（民主）

Democracy is more than the act of voting. It is a system that depends on informed citizens, free debate and respect for the rule of law. Elections alone do not guarantee freedom if people cannot think or speak freely. That is why a free press and an independent judiciary are essential. Democracy is fragile; it requires constant care. Those who take it for granted may one day find it gone.

民主不仅仅是投票行为。它是一种依赖知情公民、自由辩论和对法治尊重的制度。如果人们不能自由思考和言论，仅靠选举无法保证自由。这就是自由媒体和独立司法必不可少的原因。民主是脆弱的，需要不断呵护。那些视之为理所当然的人，或许有一天会发现它已不复存在。

**词与短语**：more than the act of voting（不仅仅是投票）、the rule of law（法治）、take it for granted（视之为理所当然）

### 8. Social Justice（社会正义）

Social justice is the idea that everyone deserves equal rights and opportunities. Yet achieving it is far from simple. Inequality is often inherited, passed down through families and communities. To create a fairer society, we must address not only outcomes but also the conditions that produce them. This means investing in education, healthcare and housing. Justice is not a single act but an ongoing effort that each generation must renew.

社会正义是人人应享有平等权利和机会的理念。然而实现它远非易事。不平等往往是代际传承的，通过家庭和社区延续。要建设更公平的社会，我们不仅要处理结果，还要处理产生结果的条件。这意味着投资教育、医疗和住房。正义不是一次性行动，而是每一代人都必须延续的持续努力。

**词与短语**：equal rights and opportunities（平等权利和机会）、pass down（代代相传）、an ongoing effort（持续的努力）

### 9. Freedom of Speech（言论自由）

Freedom of speech is a cornerstone of any open society. It allows ideas to be tested and truths to emerge. Yet it is not without limits. Speech that incites violence or spreads deliberate lies can harm real people. The challenge is to protect free expression while preventing its abuse. This is a delicate line, and reasonable people may disagree about where to draw it. But erring too far in either direction carries risks.

言论自由是任何开放社会的基石。它让思想得到检验、让真理浮现。但它并非没有限度。煽动暴力或散布蓄意谎言的言论会伤害真实的人。挑战在于保护自由表达的同时防止其被滥用。这是一条微妙的界限，理性的人可能在画界位置上存在分歧。但偏向任何一边太远都有风险。

**词与短语**：a cornerstone（基石）、incite violence（煽动暴力）、a delicate line（微妙的界限）

### 10. Immigration（移民）

Immigration is among the most divisive issues of our time. Immigrants bring skills, energy and cultural richness, yet they can also strain public services and provoke fears. Much of the debate is driven by emotion rather than evidence. A balanced view recognizes both the contributions and the challenges. The question is not whether to have immigration, but how to manage it fairly and humanely. How a society treats newcomers says much about its character.

移民是我们这个时代最具分歧的问题之一。移民带来技能、活力和文化丰富性，但也可能给公共服务带来压力、引发恐惧。许多争论由情绪而非证据驱动。平衡的观点既承认贡献也承认挑战。问题不是要不要移民，而是如何公平、人道地管理它。一个社会如何对待新来者，很能说明它的品格。

**词与短语**：among the most divisive（最具分歧的之一）、strain public services（给公共服务带来压力）、say much about（很能说明）

## 三、经济与全球化

### 11. Globalization Revisited（重思全球化）

Few forces have shaped the modern world as profoundly as globalization. It has lifted millions out of poverty and spread ideas across borders. Nevertheless, its benefits have been unevenly distributed. Many workers have seen their jobs move overseas, and local cultures have been eroded. To what extent globalization is a net good remains contested. What is beyond doubt is that it cannot simply be reversed. The task is to manage it so that its rewards are shared more widely.

很少有力量能像全球化那样深刻塑造现代世界。它使数百万人摆脱贫困、让思想跨越国界。然而其收益分配并不均衡。许多工人眼睁睁看着工作流向海外，本土文化受到侵蚀。全球化在多大程度上是净收益，仍存争议。但毫无疑问的是，它无法简单地逆转。任务在于管理它，使其回报更广泛地共享。

**词与短语**：lift millions out of poverty（使数百万人摆脱贫困）、be unevenly distributed（分配不均）、be beyond doubt（毫无疑问）

### 12. Inequality（不平等）

Inequality has risen sharply in recent decades. The wealthiest individuals now control a staggering share of resources, while many struggle to make ends meet. This concentration of wealth can undermine social cohesion and even democracy. Yet inequality is not inevitable; it results from policies and choices. Progressive taxation, education and social safety nets can all narrow the gap. A society that tolerates extreme inequality stores up trouble for the future.

近几十年来，不平等急剧加剧。最富有的个体如今掌控着惊人的资源份额，而许多人挣扎度日。这种财富集中会破坏社会凝聚力，甚至危及民主。然而不平等并非不可避免，它是政策和选择的结果。累进税制、教育和社会保障网都能缩小差距。容忍极端不平等的社会，是在为未来埋下隐患。

**词与短语**：a staggering share（惊人的份额）、make ends meet（勉强维持生计）、store up trouble（埋下隐患）

### 13. Sustainable Development（可持续发展）

Sustainable development seeks to meet present needs without compromising the future. It recognizes that economic growth, social well-being and environmental health are linked. Growth that destroys the environment is not progress but an illusion of it. The transition to sustainability requires changes in how we produce, consume and live. It is a long and difficult road, but there is no alternative. We borrow the earth from our children, not inherit it from our ancestors.

可持续发展旨在满足当下需求而不损害未来。它认识到经济增长、社会福祉和环境健康相互关联。破坏环境的增长不是进步，而是进步的假象。向可持续转型需要改变我们的生产、消费和生活方式。这是一条漫长艰难的路，但别无选择。我们是从子孙那里借用地球，而非从祖先那里继承。

**词与短语**：meet present needs（满足当下需求）、an illusion of progress（进步的假象）、borrow from our children（从子孙那里借用）

### 14. The Future of Work（工作的未来）

The nature of work is undergoing a profound transformation. Routine tasks are increasingly automated, while demand grows for creativity and interpersonal skills. Remote work has blurred the line between office and home. For workers, this brings both freedom and insecurity. The social contract around work is being rewritten, and not everyone will benefit equally. Preparing for this future requires lifelong learning and adaptable institutions.

工作的性质正经历深刻变革。常规任务日益自动化，而对创造力和人际技能的需求在增长。远程办公模糊了办公室与家的界限。对劳动者而言，这既带来自由，也带来不安。围绕工作的社会契约正在被改写，并非所有人都能平等受益。为未来做准备，需要终身学习和能灵活适应的制度。

**词与短语**：undergo a transformation（经历变革）、blur the line（模糊界限）、the social contract（社会契约）

### 15. Consumerism（消费主义）

Consumerism teaches that happiness can be bought, yet the evidence suggests otherwise. Beyond a certain point, more possessions bring little additional joy. In fact, the endless pursuit of things often leaves people feeling emptier. It also places a heavy burden on the planet. A growing number of people are turning to simpler, more meaningful ways of living. Perhaps the deepest freedom is wanting less rather than having more.

消费主义教导我们幸福可以买到，然而证据表明并非如此。超过某一点后，更多的物质带来的快乐微乎其微。事实上，无休止地追逐物质常常让人更加空虚，还给地球带来沉重负担。越来越多的人转向更简单、更有意义的生活方式。也许最深刻的自由，是想要得更少，而非拥有得更多。

**词与短语**：the evidence suggests otherwise（证据表明并非如此）、place a burden on（给……带来负担）、a simpler way of living（更简单的生活方式）

## 四、环境

### 16. Climate Policy（气候政策）

Addressing climate change demands collective action on an unprecedented scale. Individual choices matter, but they cannot substitute for policy. Governments must set clear targets, price carbon and invest in clean energy. The difficulty is that the costs are felt now, while the benefits are long-term. This makes climate policy politically hard but morally urgent. Delay is not a neutral option; it is a choice with consequences.

应对气候变化需要空前规模的集体行动。个人选择重要，但无法替代政策。政府必须设定明确目标、为碳定价、投资清洁能源。困难在于成本当下可见，收益却在长远。这使得气候政策在政治上艰难，在道德上紧迫。拖延并非中立选项，而是一种有后果的选择。

**词与短语**：on an unprecedented scale（以空前的规模）、substitute for（替代）、morally urgent（道德上紧迫）

### 17. Biodiversity（生物多样性）

Biodiversity is the web of life that sustains our planet. Yet species are disappearing at an alarming rate, driven by habitat loss, pollution and climate change. Each extinction weakens the ecosystems on which we all depend. The loss is not just ecological but economic and cultural. Protecting biodiversity is not a luxury for nature lovers; it is a necessity for survival. We are part of nature, not apart from it.

生物多样性是维系地球的生命之网。然而，由于栖息地丧失、污染和气候变化，物种正以惊人的速度消失。每一次灭绝都削弱了我们赖以生存的生态系统。这种损失不仅是生态的，也是经济和文化的。保护生物多样性不是自然爱好者的奢侈品，而是生存的必需品。我们是自然的一部分，而非置身其外。

**词与短语**：the web of life（生命之网）、at an alarming rate（以惊人的速度）、a necessity for survival（生存的必需品）

### 18. The Circular Economy（循环经济）

The linear economy—take, make, dispose—is reaching its limits. A circular economy, by contrast, designs waste out of the system. Products are made to be repaired, reused and recycled. This approach conserves resources and reduces pollution. Transitioning to it requires innovation in design and changes in consumer behavior. The circular economy is not just an environmental ideal; it is an economic opportunity.

线性经济——取用、制造、丢弃——正逼近极限。相比之下，循环经济从设计上消除浪费。产品被设计为可修复、可重复使用、可回收。这种方式节约资源、减少污染。向它转型需要设计创新和消费者行为的改变。循环经济不只是环境理想，也是经济机遇。

**词与短语**：reach its limits（逼近极限）、design waste out（从设计上消除浪费）、an economic opportunity（经济机遇）

### 19. The Energy Transition（能源转型）

The shift from fossil fuels to clean energy is the defining project of our age. It is technically feasible but politically and economically challenging. Renewable energy has become dramatically cheaper, yet storage and grids remain obstacles. The transition will create winners and losers, and a just transition must support those displaced. The pace of change is still too slow to meet climate goals. Urgency and fairness must go hand in hand.

从化石燃料向清洁能源的转变，是我们这个时代的决定性工程。它在技术上可行，但在政治和经济上充满挑战。可再生能源已大幅降价，但储能和电网仍是障碍。转型会造就赢家和输家，公正的转型必须支持受冲击者。变化的速度仍太慢，无法实现气候目标。紧迫与公平必须并行。

**词与短语**：the defining project（决定性工程）、feasible（可行的）、go hand in hand（并行）

### 20. Environmental Ethics（环境伦理）

Environmental ethics asks what we owe to nature and to future generations. For much of history, nature was seen as a resource to be exploited. A growing movement argues that nature has value beyond its use to us. This shift in thinking has profound implications for policy and daily life. We are not owners of the earth but stewards of it. The choices we make today echo through time.

环境伦理追问我们欠自然和后代什么。在历史的大部分时间里，自然被视为可开发的资源。一场日益壮大的运动主张，自然具有超越其使用价值的价值。这一思想转变对政策和日常生活有深远影响。我们不是地球的主人，而是它的守护者。我们今天的选择会在时间长河中回响。

**词与短语**：owe to（亏欠）、be exploited（被开发）、stewards of the earth（地球的守护者）

## 五、教育与知识

### 21. The Value of Knowledge（知识的价值）

Knowledge is often valued for its usefulness, but its worth runs deeper. It frees the mind from ignorance and equips us to question authority. A society that neglects knowledge becomes vulnerable to manipulation and error. Knowledge is also cumulative; each generation builds on the last. To dismiss learning as impractical is to misunderstand what makes civilization possible. We are the inheritors of a long tradition of inquiry.

知识常因其有用而受重视，但它的价值更为深刻。它把心灵从无知中解放，赋予我们质疑权威的能力。忽视知识的社会容易受到操纵和谬误的侵害。知识还是累积的，每一代都建立在前一代之上。把学习斥为不切实际，就是误解了文明得以可能的原因。我们是一长串求索传统的继承者。

**词与短语**：run deeper（更为深刻）、question authority（质疑权威）、dismiss as（斥之为）

### 22. Education Reform（教育改革）

Education systems everywhere are under pressure to change. The world students will enter is very different from the one their parents knew. Yet many schools still teach as they did a century ago. Reform should focus less on memorizing facts and more on learning how to learn. Creativity, collaboration and critical thinking matter more than ever. The goal of education is not to fill minds but to kindle them.

各地的教育体系都面临变革压力。学生将进入的世界与父母所知的截然不同。然而许多学校仍像一百年前那样教学。改革应少聚焦于死记硬背，多聚焦于学会学习。创造力、协作和批判性思维比以往更重要。教育的目标不是填满头脑，而是点燃头脑。

**词与短语**：be under pressure（面临压力）、memorize facts（死记硬背）、kindle minds（点燃头脑）

### 23. Lifelong Learning（终身学习）

The idea that education ends with graduation is obsolete. In a world of rapid change, skills can become outdated within years. Lifelong learning is no longer a luxury but a survival skill. It keeps the mind sharp and the career resilient. Fortunately, technology has made learning more accessible than ever. The question is not whether to keep learning, but what to learn next.

"教育随毕业而结束"的观念已经过时。在快速变化的世界里，技能可能在几年内过时。终身学习不再是奢侈品，而是生存技能。它让头脑敏锐、让职业有韧性。幸运的是，科技让学习比以往更易获得。问题不是是否继续学习，而是下一步学什么。

**词与短语**：become obsolete（变得过时）、a survival skill（生存技能）、accessible（易获得的）

### 24. Science and Society（科学与社会）

Science has transformed human life more than any other force. Yet its authority is increasingly questioned in some quarters. Part of the problem is that science is often poorly communicated. Scientists and the public can seem to inhabit different worlds. Bridging this gap is essential for addressing challenges like climate change and public health. A society that distrusts science is poorly equipped for the future.

科学对人类生活的改变超过任何其他力量。然而在某些地方，它的权威正日益受到质疑。部分问题在于科学常常被糟糕地传达。科学家与公众似乎生活在不同世界。弥合这一鸿沟对于应对气候变化、公共卫生等挑战至关重要。不信任科学的社会，为未来所做的准备是欠缺的。

**词与短语**：more than any other force（超过任何其他力量）、be poorly communicated（被糟糕地传达）、be poorly equipped（准备欠缺）

### 25. The Limits of Expertise（专业的局限）

Experts play a vital role in a complex society, yet expertise has its limits. Experts can be wrong, biased or captured by vested interests. The pandemic showed both the value and the limits of expert advice. This does not mean we should dismiss expertise, but that we should engage with it critically. The wisest approach is to respect knowledge while questioning authority. Humility and curiosity serve us better than blind faith.

专家在复杂社会中扮演重要角色，但专业也有局限。专家可能出错、有偏见或被既得利益俘获。疫情既展示了专业建议的价值，也展示了其局限。这并不意味着我们应摒弃专业，而是应批判性地对待它。最明智的做法是尊重知识、同时质疑权威。谦逊和好奇比盲信更能帮助我们。

**词与短语**：vested interests（既得利益）、engage with it critically（批判性地对待）、blind faith（盲信）

## 六、心理与哲学

### 26. The Nature of Happiness（幸福的本质）

Happiness is one of those words everyone understands until asked to define it. Some treat it as a destination to reach; others, as a journey to savor. Philosophers have long argued that happiness is not something we stumble upon but something we practice. It grows not from the abundance of what we possess, but from the depth of what we appreciate. To be happy, then, is less a matter of changing our circumstances than of changing our gaze.

幸福是那种人人都懂、直到被要求下定义时才说不清道不明的词。有人视之为待抵达的终点，有人视之为待品味的旅程。哲学家们早就主张，幸福不是偶然撞见的东西，而是修炼出来的东西。它的生长不源于我们拥有的丰盛，而源于我们感恩的深度。那么，要幸福，与其改变处境，不如改变目光。

**词与短语**：stumble upon（偶然撞见）、the abundance of what we possess（拥有的丰盛）、change our gaze（改变目光）

### 27. Free Will（自由意志）

Do we truly choose our actions, or are they determined by causes beyond our control? This ancient question still divides philosophers and scientists. Some argue that our decisions are the product of brain processes we do not control. Others insist that the experience of choosing is too fundamental to dismiss. Whatever the answer, the belief in free will shapes how we hold people responsible. The debate is not merely academic; it touches the foundations of ethics and law.

我们真的在选择自己的行为，还是被我们无法控制的原因所决定？这个古老的问题至今仍让哲学家和科学家分歧。有人认为我们的决定是我们无法控制的大脑过程的产物，有人坚持选择感太根本、无法否定。无论答案如何，对自由意志的信念塑造了我们追究责任的方式。这场辩论并非纯学术，它触及伦理与法律的根基。

**词与短语**：divide philosophers（使哲学家分歧）、too fundamental to dismiss（太根本而无法否定）、hold responsible（追究责任）

### 28. Identity（身份认同）

Who are we, really? Identity is partly given—by family, culture and history—and partly chosen. In the modern world, people are freer than ever to shape their own identities. Yet this freedom can also be disorienting. The search for identity is a search for a coherent story about oneself. It is never finished, and that is perhaps the point. We are always becoming who we are.

我们究竟是谁？身份一部分是既定的——由家庭、文化和历史赋予——一部分是选择的。在现代世界，人们比以往更自由地塑造自己的身份。然而这种自由也可能令人迷失。对身份的追寻，是对关于自己的连贯故事的追寻。它永无完结，而这或许正是意义所在。我们始终在成为自己。

**词与短语**：partly given, partly chosen（部分是既定，部分是选择）、disorienting（令人迷失的）、a coherent story（连贯的故事）

### 29. The Meaning of Life（生命的意义）

The question of life's meaning has occupied thinkers for millennia. Some find meaning in purpose, others in love or service. What matters is not so much the answer as the search itself. A meaningful life is often one lived for something larger than oneself. This can be family, craft, faith or a cause. Meaning is not discovered once and for all; it is created day by day. The unexamined life, as Socrates warned, is not worth living.

生命意义的问题困扰了思想家数千年。有人从目标中寻找意义，有人从爱或奉献中寻找。重要的与其说是答案，不如说是追寻本身。有意义的人生，往往是为比自身更大的事物而活——可以是家庭、事业、信仰或事业。意义不是一劳永逸地发现的，而是日复一日创造的。正如苏格拉底警告的，未经审视的人生不值得过。

**词与短语**：occupy thinkers（困扰思想家）、something larger than oneself（比自身更大的事物）、day by day（日复一日）

### 30. Mindfulness（正念）

Mindfulness is the practice of paying attention to the present moment without judgment. In a world of constant distraction, it is a rare and valuable skill. It teaches us to notice our thoughts without being swept away by them. Research suggests it can reduce stress and improve well-being. Yet mindfulness is not a quick fix; it requires patient practice. Its deepest gift is the ability to be fully present to our own lives.

正念是不加评判地关注当下的练习。在充满干扰的世界里，这是一项稀有而宝贵的技能。它教会我们觉察思绪而不被其裹挟。研究表明它能减轻压力、改善幸福感。然而正念不是速效药，它需要耐心的练习。它最深的馈赠，是能全然在场于自己生活的能力。

**词与短语**：the present moment（当下）、be swept away（被裹挟）、a quick fix（速效药）

## 七、文化与艺术

### 31. The Purpose of Art（艺术的目的）

What is art for? Some say it exists for beauty, others for self-expression. Art also serves deeper purposes: it can challenge, console and connect. Great art holds a mirror to society and lets us see ourselves more clearly. It gives form to emotions that words cannot capture. In times of crisis, people turn to art for meaning and comfort. Art is not a luxury; it is a fundamental human need.

艺术是为了什么？有人说它为了美，有人说为了自我表达。艺术还有更深的目的：它能挑战、慰藉和连接。伟大的艺术为社会竖起镜子，让我们更清楚地看见自己。它为言语无法捕捉的情感赋予形式。在危机时刻，人们向艺术寻求意义和慰藉。艺术不是奢侈品，而是人类的基本需求。

**词与短语**：hold a mirror to（为……竖起镜子）、give form to（赋予形式）、turn to art（向艺术寻求）

### 32. Cultural Identity（文化认同）

Cultural identity is the sense of belonging to a particular group and its traditions. It provides roots in a rapidly changing world. Yet identity is not fixed; it evolves as cultures meet and mix. The challenge is to honor one's heritage while remaining open to others. A confident culture can engage with the world without fear of losing itself. Identity is strengthened, not weakened, by genuine exchange.

文化认同是对特定群体及其传统的归属感。在快速变化的世界里，它提供根基。然而认同并非固定不变，它随着文化的相遇与交融而演变。挑战在于尊重自己的传承，同时对他人保持开放。一个自信的文化能与世界交流而不必担心失去自我。真正的交流只会加强而非削弱认同。

**词与短语**：a sense of belonging（归属感）、provide roots（提供根基）、honor one's heritage（尊重传承）

### 33. Language and Thought（语言与思维）

The relationship between language and thought has long fascinated scholars. Do we think in words, or merely clothe thoughts in words afterward? Some argue that language shapes how we perceive the world; others insist thought is largely universal. Wherever the truth lies, a richer vocabulary does not merely describe a richer inner life—it helps create it. To learn a word is to acquire a tool for noticing what previously escaped us.

语言与思维的关系长期吸引着学者。我们是用词语思考，还是仅在事后给思想披上词语的外衣？有人认为语言塑造我们感知世界的方式，有人坚持思维在很大程度上是普遍的。无论真相何在，更丰富的词汇不只是描述更丰富的内心——它本身就帮助创造这种内心。学会一个词，就是获得一件察觉从前被忽略之物的工具。

**词与短语**：fascinate scholars（吸引学者）、clothe thoughts in words（给思想披上词语外衣）、escape us（被我们忽略）

### 34. The Media（媒体）

The media is often called the fourth estate, reflecting its power to hold authority to account. Yet this power carries responsibility. A media driven by profit and attention may sensationalize rather than inform. The rise of social platforms has fragmented the public conversation. In this environment, the ability to evaluate sources has become essential. A free but irresponsible press can be as harmful as a censored one.

媒体常被称为"第四权力"，反映其监督权力的力量。然而这一力量伴随着责任。被利润和流量驱动的媒体可能耸人听闻而非如实报道。社交平台的兴起割裂了公共对话。在这种环境下，评估信息来源的能力变得至关重要。自由但不负责任的媒体，其危害不亚于被审查的媒体。

**词与短语**：the fourth estate（第四权力）、hold to account（监督/问责）、sensationalize（耸人听闻）

### 35. Literature and Empathy（文学与同理心）

Reading literature is often said to build empathy. By entering the minds of characters unlike ourselves, we learn to see the world through other eyes. A novel can make us feel the weight of choices we will never face. In this way, literature is a training ground for compassion. It reminds us that other people have inner lives as rich as our own. Few activities expand the moral imagination more powerfully.

人们常说阅读文学能培养同理心。进入与自己不同的人物的内心，我们学会透过他人的眼睛看世界。一部小说能让我们感受到我们永远不会面对的选择的分量。就这样，文学成了慈悲的训练场。它提醒我们，他人有着和我们一样丰富的内心。很少有活动能比文学更有力地拓展道德想象力。

**词与短语**：see through other eyes（透过他人的眼睛看）、a training ground for compassion（慈悲的训练场）、the moral imagination（道德想象力）

## 八、健康与医学

### 36. Public Health（公共卫生）

Public health is often invisible when it works. Clean water, vaccines and sanitation have saved more lives than any medicine. Yet because their success is quiet, they are easily taken for granted. The pandemic was a stark reminder of how much we depend on public health. Investment in prevention is far cheaper than treating disease. A society that neglects public health pays a heavy price in the end.

公共卫生在起作用时常常是隐形的。清洁的水、疫苗和卫生设施拯救的生命比任何药物都多。然而正因其成功悄无声息，它们容易被当作理所当然。疫情是对我们多么依赖公共卫生的严峻提醒。投资预防远比治病便宜。忽视公共卫生的社会最终要付出沉重代价。

**词与短语**：save more lives than（比……拯救更多生命）、be taken for granted（被当作理所当然）、a stark reminder（严峻的提醒）

### 37. Mental Health（心理健康）

For decades, mental health was a topic rarely discussed in public. Those who suffered often did so in silence. Fortunately, attitudes have shifted, and mental well-being is now recognized as no less important than physical health. Yet stigma persists, and treatment remains hard to reach for many. Addressing this requires not only better services but a cultural change. Seeking help is not a sign of weakness but an act of courage.

几十年来，心理健康是一个很少被公开讨论的话题。受困扰者往往默默承受。幸运的是，观念已经转变，心理健康如今被认识到与身体健康同等重要。然而污名依然存在，对许多人来说治疗仍难获得。解决这一问题不仅需要更好的服务，还需要文化转变。求助不是软弱的标志，而是勇气的表现。

**词与短语**：do so in silence（默默承受）、no less important than（不亚于）、an act of courage（勇气的表现）

### 38. Medical Ethics（医学伦理）

Medical ethics grapples with some of the hardest questions we face. How should scarce organs be allocated? How much risk is acceptable in treatment? Who decides when life support should end? These questions cannot be answered by science alone; they require values. Principles like autonomy, beneficence and justice guide the profession. As medicine advances, the ethical dilemmas multiply. Wisdom must keep pace with technology.

医学伦理面对的是我们所面临的一些最艰难的问题。稀缺的器官该如何分配？治疗中多大的风险可接受？谁来决定何时终止生命支持？这些问题不能仅靠科学回答，还需要价值观。自主、行善、公正等原则指导着这个行业。随着医学进步，伦理困境成倍增加。智慧必须与技术同步。

**词与短语**：grapple with（努力应对）、allocate organs（分配器官）、keep pace with（与……同步）

### 39. Aging（衰老）

Aging is not merely a biological process; it is a social and philosophical one. Modern medicine has extended life, but has it extended the quality of life? Many societies are unprepared for an aging population. Older people are often marginalized despite their experience and wisdom. A humane society values its elders and supports their dignity. How we treat the old reveals our deepest values. We will all, if fortunate, grow old.

衰老不仅是生物学过程，也是社会与哲学过程。现代医学延长了寿命，但它延长了生活质量吗？许多社会对老龄化人口准备不足。老年人尽管拥有经验和智慧，却常被边缘化。人道的社会尊重长者、维护他们的尊严。我们如何对待老人，揭示了最深的价值观。幸运的话，我们都会变老。

**词与短语**：the quality of life（生活质量）、be marginalized（被边缘化）、reveal our values（揭示我们的价值观）

### 40. Health Inequality（健康不平等）

Health is profoundly unequal. Where people are born, how much they earn and what they eat all shape how long they live. The wealthy enjoy longer, healthier lives than the poor in the same city. This gap is not primarily about medical care but about social conditions. Addressing health inequality means tackling poverty, housing and education. Health is a mirror that reflects a society's fairness.

健康是极不平等的。人们出生在哪里、收入多少、吃什么，都影响着他们的寿命。在同一座城市里，富人比穷人享有更长、更健康的生命。这一差距主要不在于医疗，而在于社会条件。解决健康不平等，意味着解决贫困、住房和教育。健康是一面镜子，映照出一个社会的公平程度。

**词与短语**：profoundly unequal（极不平等）、tackle poverty（解决贫困）、a mirror（一面镜子）

## 九、批判与思辨

### 41. Critical Thinking（批判性思维）

In an age of information overload, critical thinking has never been more valuable. We are bombarded with claims competing for our trust. Critical thinking lets us distinguish evidence from assertion and detect bias. It is not a habit of doubting everything, but a method of arriving at well-founded conclusions. What we need is not less information but better judgment. And judgment must be cultivated through practice and the courage to question even our own beliefs.

在信息过载的时代，批判性思维从未如此宝贵。我们被争夺信任的各种论断狂轰滥炸。批判性思维让我们区分证据与断言、察觉偏见。它不是凡事怀疑的习惯，而是得出有充分依据结论的方法。我们需要的不是更少的信息，而是更好的判断。而判断必须通过实践和勇于质疑自己信念的勇气来培养。

**词与短语**：be bombarded with（被……狂轰滥炸）、distinguish from（区分）、well-founded conclusions（有充分依据的结论）

### 42. Bias（偏见）

Bias is a subtle and pervasive force. We like to think we are objective, yet our judgments are shaped by hidden assumptions. Confirmation bias leads us to favor evidence that supports what we already believe. Awareness of bias is the first step toward overcoming it. This requires intellectual humility and a willingness to be wrong. The goal is not perfect objectivity but honest inquiry. We should hold our opinions lightly.

偏见是一股微妙而普遍的力量。我们喜欢自认为客观，然而我们的判断受隐藏假设的塑造。确认偏误让我们偏爱支持既有观点的证据。意识到偏见是克服它的第一步。这需要智识上的谦逊和愿意认错的胸怀。目标不是完美的客观，而是诚实的探究。我们应轻握自己的观点。

**词与短语**：a pervasive force（普遍的力量）、confirmation bias（确认偏误）、hold our opinions lightly（轻握观点）

### 43. The Post-truth Era（后真相时代）

We live, some say, in a post-truth era, where feelings trump facts and narratives trump evidence. Social media rewards outrage over accuracy. False stories spread faster than true ones. This poses a grave threat to democratic debate. Restoring a shared reality is among the great challenges of our time. It requires better media literacy and institutions worthy of trust. Truth is fragile, and once lost, it is hard to recover.

有人说，我们生活在一个后真相时代——情绪压倒事实，叙事压倒证据。社交媒体奖励愤怒而非准确。虚假故事比真实故事传播得更快。这对民主辩论构成严重威胁。重建共同的现实感是我们这个时代的重大挑战之一。这需要更好的媒体素养和值得信赖的制度。真相是脆弱的，一旦失去便难以恢复。

**词与短语**：trump facts（压倒事实）、reward outrage（奖励愤怒）、a shared reality（共同的现实）

### 44. Argumentation（论证）

A good argument is not a fight but a search for truth. It presents reasons, considers counterarguments and aims at understanding. Many disagreements fail because people argue to win rather than to learn. The strongest positions are those tested against the best objections. Listening well is as important as speaking well. The measure of an argument is not how loudly it is made but how well it holds up. We reason best when we reason together.

好的论证不是争斗，而是对真理的探寻。它提出理由、考虑反方、旨在理解。许多分歧失败，是因为人们为赢而辩，而非为学而辩。最强的立场，是经得起最有力反驳检验的立场。善于倾听与善于表达同样重要。衡量论证的标准，不在于它多响亮，而在于它多站得住脚。我们共同思考时，思考得最好。

**词与短语**：a search for truth（对真理的探寻）、argue to win（为赢而辩）、hold up（站得住脚）

### 45. Skepticism（怀疑精神）

Skepticism is often misunderstood as cynicism, but they are opposites. The skeptic asks for evidence, while the cynic assumes the worst. Healthy skepticism is the engine of science and good journalism. It protects us from fraud and folly. Yet skepticism must be balanced with openness, for the closed mind learns nothing. The goal is to doubt wisely, not to doubt everything. An examined belief is stronger than an inherited one.

怀疑精神常被误解为犬儒主义，但二者相反。怀疑者要求证据，犬儒者假定最坏。健康的怀疑是科学和好新闻的动力，它保护我们免受欺诈和愚蠢。然而怀疑必须与开放相平衡，因为封闭的心灵什么都学不到。目标在于明智地怀疑，而非怀疑一切。经过审视的信念，比承袭而来的信念更牢固。

**词与短语**：be misunderstood as（被误解为）、the engine of science（科学的动力）、doubt wisely（明智地怀疑）

## 十、个人与社会责任

### 46. Civic Duty（公民责任）

Democracy rests on the active participation of ordinary citizens. Voting is the most basic duty, but it is only the beginning. Civic life also means staying informed, serving on juries and speaking up for what is right. When citizens disengage, power flows to the few. Apathy is not neutrality; it is a choice that favors the status quo. A healthy democracy depends on people who care enough to act.

民主建立在普通公民积极参与之上。投票是最基本的责任，但这只是开始。公民生活还意味着保持知情、担任陪审员、为正义发声。当公民脱离参与时，权力便流向少数人。冷漠并非中立，而是一种偏袒现状的选择。健康的民主，依赖于足够在意、愿意行动的人。

**词与短语**：rest on（建立在……之上）、stay informed（保持知情）、favor the status quo（偏袒现状）

### 47. Leadership（领导力）

Leadership is the art of turning shared purpose into action. It is not the same as authority or rank; titles do not make leaders. True leaders inspire trust and empower those around them. They take responsibility when things go wrong and share credit when things go right. Leadership is exercised as much in quiet decisions as in public moments. In every field, the quality of leadership shapes the quality of results.

领导力是把共同目标转化为行动的艺术。它不同于权威或职位，头衔造就不了领导者。真正的领导者激发信任、赋能周围的人。出错时他们承担责任，顺利时分享功劳。领导力既体现在公开时刻，也体现在安静的决断中。在每个领域，领导力的质量塑造着成果的质量。

**词与短语**：turn purpose into action（把目标转化为行动）、empower those around（赋能周围的人）、share credit（分享功劳）

### 48. Ethics in Business（商业伦理）

Business and ethics are too often seen as opposites. Yet a business that cheats its customers or exploits its workers cannot thrive for long. Trust is the currency of commerce; once lost, it is hard to regain. Ethical companies attract loyal customers and committed employees. Doing the right thing is not a cost but an investment. In the long run, integrity and profit are not enemies but allies.

商业与伦理常被视为对立。然而欺骗顾客、剥削员工的生意难以长久兴旺。信任是商业的货币，一旦失去便难以挽回。有道德的公司吸引忠诚的顾客和尽职的员工。做正确的事不是成本，而是投资。从长远看，诚信与利润不是敌人，而是盟友。

**词与短语**：be seen as opposites（被视为对立）、the currency of commerce（商业的货币）、in the long run（从长远看）

### 49. Social Responsibility（社会责任）

We are not isolated individuals; our choices ripple through society. What we buy, how we treat others and whether we vote all shape the world around us. Social responsibility means recognizing this power and using it well. It is easy to blame institutions while ignoring our own role. Change begins when ordinary people take responsibility for the common good. The world becomes better through countless small acts of conscience.

我们不是孤立的个体，我们的选择会在社会中泛起涟漪。我们买什么、如何待人、是否投票，都塑造着周围的世界。社会责任意味着认识到这种力量并善用它。责备制度而忽视自身角色是容易的。当普通人开始为公共利益承担责任时，改变就开始了。世界因无数小小的良知之举而变得更美好。

**词与短语**：ripple through（泛起涟漪）、blame institutions（责备制度）、the common good（公共利益）

### 50. The Individual and Society（个人与社会）

The relationship between the individual and society is a perennial theme. We need society for security, education and meaning; society needs us for vitality and renewal. The tension lies in balancing personal freedom with social obligation. Too much conformity crushes the spirit; too little leaves chaos. A good society makes room for both the individual and the collective. The whole is built, after all, from the contributions of each.

个人与社会的关系是一个永恒的主题。我们需要社会提供安全、教育和意义；社会需要我们提供活力与更新。张力在于平衡个人自由与社会义务。过度的顺从压垮精神，过度的放任导致混乱。好的社会为个人与集体都留有空间。毕竟，整体是由每个人的贡献构建而成的。

**词与短语**：a perennial theme（永恒的主题）、balance freedom with obligation（平衡自由与义务）、make room for（为……留空间）
