# C1 分级阅读 · 高级

> 对应 CEFR C1（约 8,000 词以上）。短文涉及抽象、学术与社会议题，使用复杂从句、倒装、强调等高级结构，适合训练思辨与精准表达。

## 1. The Rise of Artificial Intelligence（人工智能的崛起）

**英文正文**

Not only has artificial intelligence transformed the way we work, but it has also reshaped the way we think about human ability. Machines can now write essays, diagnose diseases and even create art, tasks that were once considered uniquely human. Yet the rapid advance of AI raises profound questions. What happens to employment when machines outperform us in more and more fields? How do we ensure that such powerful technology is used for good? It is precisely because the stakes are so high that we must approach AI with both excitement and caution. The future, after all, will be shaped less by the technology itself than by the choices we make today.

**中文译文**

人工智能不仅改变了我们的工作方式，也重塑了我们对人类能力的看法。如今机器能写文章、诊断疾病，甚至创作艺术——这些任务曾被认为只有人类才能完成。然而，人工智能的飞速发展引发了深刻的问题：当机器在越来越多领域超越我们时，就业会发生什么？我们又该如何确保如此强大的技术被用于善举？正因为风险如此之高，我们对待人工智能必须既兴奋又谨慎。归根结底，塑造未来的，与其说是技术本身，不如说是我们今天所做的选择。

**重点词汇与短语**

- not only...but also...（不仅……而且……，倒装）
- outperform（超越；胜过）
- uniquely human（人类独有的）
- raise profound questions（引发深刻的问题）
- it is precisely because...that...（正是……才……，强调句）
- the stakes are high（风险很高）

## 2. Globalization: Promise and Peril（全球化：机遇与挑战）

**英文正文**

Few forces have shaped the modern world as profoundly as globalization. It has lifted millions out of poverty, spread ideas across borders and connected economies in ways that were previously inconceivable. Nevertheless, globalization is far from an unqualified success. Critics argue that it has widened inequality, eroded local cultures and left many workers vulnerable to economic shocks. To what extent these criticisms are justified remains a matter of intense debate. What is beyond doubt, however, is that globalization is here to stay. The challenge, therefore, is not whether to embrace it, but how to manage it in a way that distributes its benefits more fairly.

**中文译文**

很少有力量能像全球化那样深刻地塑造现代世界。它使数百万人摆脱了贫困，让思想跨越国界传播，并以从前无法想象的方式将各经济体连接起来。然而，全球化远非完全的成功。批评者认为，它加剧了不平等，侵蚀了本土文化，并使许多劳动者容易受到经济冲击的影响。这些批评在多大程度上是合理的，仍是一个激烈争论的话题。但毫无疑问的是，全球化已经不可逆转。因此，挑战不在于是否拥抱它，而在于如何以更公平地分配其利益的方式来管理它。

**重点词汇与短语**

- lift...out of poverty（使……摆脱贫困）
- far from an unqualified success（远非完全成功）
- widen inequality（加剧不平等）
- remain a matter of intense debate（仍是激烈争论的话题）
- be beyond doubt（毫无疑问）
- distribute its benefits more fairly（更公平地分配利益）

## 3. Mental Health in Modern Society（现代社会的心理健康）

**英文正文**

For decades, mental health was a topic rarely discussed in public, and those who suffered often did so in silence. Fortunately, attitudes have shifted, and society is beginning to recognize that mental well-being is no less important than physical health. This change could not have come too soon. In an age defined by constant connectivity and relentless competition, rates of anxiety and depression have risen sharply. The causes are complex, ranging from economic pressure to the isolating effects of social media. Addressing them requires not only better access to professional help, but also a cultural change that treats seeking help not as a sign of weakness, but as an act of courage.

**中文译文**

几十年来，心理健康是一个很少被公开讨论的话题，那些受其困扰的人往往默默承受。幸运的是，观念已经转变，社会开始认识到心理健康的重要性不亚于身体健康。这一转变来得再及时不过了。在一个以无休止的连接和激烈竞争为特征的时代，焦虑和抑郁的比例急剧上升。原因是复杂的，从经济压力到社交媒体带来的孤立感不一而足。要解决这些问题，不仅需要更好地获得专业帮助，还需要一种文化转变——把寻求帮助视为勇气的表现，而非软弱的标志。

**重点词汇与短语**

- do so in silence（默默承受）
- no less important than（不亚于……的重要性）
- could not have come too soon（来得再及时不过了）
- an age defined by（以……为特征的时代）
- relentless competition（无休止的竞争）
- a sign of weakness / an act of courage（软弱的标志 / 勇气的表现）

## 4. Balancing Economy and Environment（经济与环境的平衡）

**英文正文**

For much of the twentieth century, economic growth and environmental protection were widely seen as opposing goals. It was assumed that a nation had to choose between prosperity and a healthy planet. This assumption has increasingly been called into question. There is growing evidence that sustainable practices can drive innovation and create new industries rather than destroy them. Indeed, many economists now argue that the real choice facing us is not growth versus the environment, but short-term gain versus long-term survival. Reconciling these two imperatives is difficult, yet it is arguably the defining challenge of our generation, and the manner in which we respond will determine the world we leave behind.

**中文译文**

在二十世纪的大部分时间里，经济增长和环境保护被广泛视为相互对立的目标。人们曾认为，一个国家必须在繁荣和健康的地球之间做出选择。这一假设越来越受到质疑。越来越多的证据表明，可持续的做法能够推动创新、催生新产业，而不是摧毁它们。事实上，许多经济学家现在认为，我们面临的真正选择不是增长与环境之争，而是短期利益与长期生存之争。调和这两个当务之急十分困难，但这可以说是我们这一代人面临的决定性挑战，而我们应对的方式将决定我们留给后代一个怎样的世界。

**重点词汇与短语**

- be seen as opposing goals（被视为对立的目标）
- call into question（质疑）
- drive innovation（推动创新）
- short-term gain versus long-term survival（短期利益与长期生存）
- reconcile two imperatives（调和两个当务之急）
- the defining challenge（决定性的挑战）

## 5. The Power of Critical Thinking（批判性思维的力量）

**英文正文**

In an era of information overload, the ability to think critically has never been more valuable. We are bombarded daily with news, opinions and advertisements, each competing for our attention and our trust. Critical thinking is what allows us to distinguish reliable evidence from mere assertion, to detect bias and to resist manipulation. It is not, as some suppose, a purely negative habit of doubting everything; rather, it is a disciplined method of arriving at well-founded conclusions. What we need, then, is not less information but better judgment. And judgment, unlike information, cannot simply be downloaded; it must be cultivated through practice, curiosity and the courage to question even our own beliefs.

**中文译文**

在信息过载的时代，批判性思维的能力从未如此宝贵。我们每天都被新闻、观点和广告狂轰滥炸，它们都在争夺我们的注意力和信任。批判性思维使我们能够区分可靠的证据与单纯的断言，察觉偏见，抵御操控。它并非如某些人所想的那样，是一种凡事怀疑的纯负面习惯；相反，它是一种得出有充分依据结论的严谨方法。那么，我们需要的不是更少的信息，而是更好的判断力。而判断力与信息不同，无法简单地下载获取，它必须通过实践、好奇心以及勇于质疑自己信念的勇气来培养。

**重点词汇与短语**

- an era of information overload（信息过载的时代）
- be bombarded with（被……狂轰滥炸）
- distinguish...from...（区分……与……）
- detect bias（察觉偏见）
- arrive at well-founded conclusions（得出有充分依据的结论）
- be cultivated through practice（通过实践来培养）
