## 一、科技与伦理

### 1. AI and Ethics（人工智能与伦理）

Not only has artificial intelligence transformed the way we work, but it has also raised profound ethical questions. Machines can now make decisions that affect people's lives, from hiring to medical diagnosis. The difficulty is that AI has no conscience; it simply follows the data it was given. Consequently, the responsibility for its consequences rests with us. It is precisely because the stakes are so high that we must approach AI with both excitement and caution. The future will be shaped less by the technology itself than by the choices we make today.

人工智能不仅改变了我们的工作方式，也引发了深刻的伦理问题。如今机器能做出影响人们生活的决定，从招聘到医疗诊断。困难在于，AI 没有良知，它只是遵循被输入的数据。因此，其后果的责任在我们。正因为风险如此之高，我们对待 AI 必须既兴奋又审慎。塑造未来的，与其说是技术本身，不如说是我们今天的抉择。

**词与短语**：raise ethical questions（引发伦理问题）、rests with us（在于我们）、the stakes are so high（风险如此之高）

**语法点睛**：**否定词开头的倒装句（Not only has...）** —— Not only 置于句首时，助动词要提前构成部分倒装，强调"不仅……而且……"。

**语法示例**：
- Not only did she sing, but she also danced.（她不仅唱歌，还跳舞。）
- Not only is he smart, but he is also kind.（他不仅聪明，而且善良。）

### 2. The Digital Divide（数字鸿沟）

The digital divide refers to the gap between those who have access to technology and those who do not. While some enjoy the benefits of high-speed internet, others remain cut off from the digital world. This divide is not merely about convenience; it is about opportunity. Education, jobs and services increasingly require digital access. Consequently, closing this gap has become a matter of social justice. What was once a luxury is now a necessity, and those left behind will fall further behind.

数字鸿沟指的是能接触技术者与不能者之间的差距。一些人享受高速互联网的便利，另一些人则与数字世界隔绝。这一鸿沟不只是便利问题，而是机会问题。教育、就业和服务越来越依赖数字接入。因此，弥合这一差距已成为社会公正问题。曾经的奢侈品如今成了必需品，被落下的人只会落后得更远。

**词与短语**：refer to（指的是）、be cut off from（与……隔绝）、a matter of social justice（社会公正问题）

**语法点睛**：**what 引导的主语从句（What was once a luxury）** —— What was once a luxury is now a necessity，what 引导主语从句，表示"曾经是……的东西"。

**语法示例**：
- What seemed impossible is now real.（曾经看似不可能的事如今已成现实。）
- What we need most is time.（我们最需要的是时间。）

### 3. Automation and Jobs（自动化与就业）

Few forces have reshaped the economy as profoundly as automation. It has made production faster and cheaper, yet it has also displaced countless workers. The question is no longer whether jobs will change, but how we will adapt. History suggests that new technologies create new jobs even as they destroy old ones. Nevertheless, the transition can be painful for those without the right skills. What is needed, therefore, is not resistance to change but investment in education and retraining.

很少有力量能像自动化那样深刻地重塑经济。它让生产更快更廉价，却也取代了无数工人。问题已不再是工作是否会改变，而是我们将如何适应。历史表明，新技术在摧毁旧工作的同时也会创造新工作。然而，对缺乏相应技能的人来说，转型可能是痛苦的。因此，我们需要的不是抗拒变化，而是投资教育和再培训。

**词与短语**：as profoundly as（像……那样深刻地）、displace workers（取代工人）、investment in education（投资教育）

**语法点睛**：**比较结构 as...as（Few forces...as...as）** —— Few forces have reshaped...as profoundly as automation，as + 副词 + as 表示同级比较。

**语法示例**：
- Few things matter as much as health.（很少有事情像健康一样重要。）
- Nothing motivates as powerfully as passion.（没有什么像热爱一样有力地激励人。）

### 4. Data and Privacy（数据与隐私）

In the digital economy, data has been called the new oil. Companies collect vast amounts of information about us, often without our full awareness. This data powers services we enjoy, but it also creates risks. The line between convenience and surveillance is becoming dangerously blurred. If we are not careful, we may trade away our privacy for small benefits. Protecting personal data is not a technical issue alone; it is a matter of fundamental rights.

在数字经济中，数据被称为"新石油"。公司在往在我们不完全知情的情况下收集海量信息。这些数据支撑着我们享受的服务，却也带来风险。便利与监控之间的界限正变得危险地模糊。如果我们不小心，可能会为蝇头小利出卖隐私。保护个人数据不只是技术问题，更是基本权利问题。

**词与短语**：the new oil（新石油）、blur the line（模糊界限）、trade away（出卖）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时的被动语态（has been called）** —— data has been called the new oil，has been + 过去分词，表示"已被称作"。

**语法示例**：
- This city has been called the garden of the south.（这座城市被称为南方花园。）
- The Internet has been described as a double-edged sword.（互联网被形容为双刃剑。）

### 5. Technology and Human Connection（科技与人的连接）

Technology promises to connect us, yet many feel lonelier than ever. We have hundreds of online friends but few people we can truly confide in. Screens have replaced face-to-face conversation in many homes. This paradox lies at the heart of modern life: the more connected we are, the more isolated we can become. The solution is not to reject technology but to use it deliberately. Real connection requires time, attention and presence, none of which can be replaced by an app.

科技承诺连接我们，然而许多人却比以往更孤独。我们有数百个网友，却鲜有可真正倾诉的人。在许多家庭，屏幕取代了面对面的交谈。这一悖论是现代生活的核心：连接得越多，我们反而可能越孤立。解决办法不是拒绝科技，而是有意识地使用它。真正的连接需要时间、专注和在场，这些都无法被一个应用取代。

**词与短语**：confide in（向……倾诉）、at the heart of（在……的核心）、use it deliberately（有意识地使用）

**语法点睛**：**the more...the more（越……越……）** —— the more connected we are, the more isolated we can become，比较级对偶结构。

**语法示例**：
- The more we share, the richer we become.（分享越多，我们越富有。）
- The more you learn, the humbler you feel.（学得越多，越感到谦卑。）

## 二、社会与政治

### 6. The Role of Government（政府的角色）

The proper role of government has been debated for centuries. Some argue that it should do no more than protect rights and enforce contracts. Others believe it must actively promote welfare and reduce inequality. In practice, most societies fall somewhere in between. The challenge is to strike a balance between freedom and security, between individual initiative and collective responsibility. A government that does too much can stifle innovation, while one that does too little can fail the vulnerable.

政府的恰当角色已被争论了几个世纪。有人认为它只需保护权利、执行契约，有人认为它必须积极促进福利、减少不平等。实际上，大多数社会介于两者之间。挑战在于在自由与安全、个人主动性与集体责任之间取得平衡。政府做得太多会扼杀创新，做得太少则会辜负弱势群体。

**词与短语**：be debated for centuries（被争论几个世纪）、strike a balance（取得平衡）、fail the vulnerable（辜负弱势群体）

**语法点睛**：**while 表对比（复现）** —— one that does too much...while one that does too little...，while 连接两种极端。

**语法示例**：
- A plan that is too rigid fails, while one that is too loose fails too.（过于死板的计划会失败，过于松散的计划也会失败。）
- Some prefer action, while others prefer patience.（有人偏爱行动，有人偏爱耐心。）

### 7. Democracy（民主）

Democracy is more than the act of voting. It is a system that depends on informed citizens, free debate and respect for the rule of law. Elections alone do not guarantee freedom if people cannot think or speak freely. That is why a free press and an independent judiciary are essential. Democracy is fragile; it requires constant care. Those who take it for granted may one day find it gone.

民主不仅仅是投票行为。它是一种依赖知情公民、自由辩论和对法治尊重的制度。如果人们不能自由思考和言论，仅靠选举无法保证自由。这就是自由媒体和独立司法必不可少的原因。民主是脆弱的，需要不断呵护。那些视之为理所当然的人，或许有一天会发现它已不复存在。

**词与短语**：more than the act of voting（不仅仅是投票）、the rule of law（法治）、take it for granted（视之为理所当然）

**语法点睛**：**those who...（那些……的人，复现）** —— Those who take it for granted，who 引导定语从句修饰 those。

**语法示例**：
- Those who value freedom must defend it.（珍视自由的人必须捍卫自由。）
- Those who stay silent let wrongs continue.（沉默的人让错误继续。）

### 8. Social Justice（社会正义）

Social justice is the idea that everyone deserves equal rights and opportunities. Yet achieving it is far from simple. Inequality is often inherited, passed down through families and communities. To create a fairer society, we must address not only outcomes but also the conditions that produce them. This means investing in education, healthcare and housing. Justice is not a single act but an ongoing effort that each generation must renew.

社会正义是人人应享有平等权利和机会的理念。然而实现它远非易事。不平等往往是代际传承的，通过家庭和社区延续。要建设更公平的社会，我们不仅要处理结果，还要处理产生结果的条件。这意味着投资教育、医疗和住房。正义不是一次性行动，而是每一代人都必须延续的持续努力。

**词与短语**：equal rights and opportunities（平等权利和机会）、pass down（代代相传）、an ongoing effort（持续的努力）

**语法点睛**：**not only...but also（复现）** —— not only outcomes but also the conditions，not only...but also 连接并列成分。

**语法示例**：
- We must change not only the result but also the cause.（我们不仅要改变结果，还要改变原因。）
- She cares not only about money but also about meaning.（她不仅在乎钱，也在乎意义。）

### 9. Freedom of Speech（言论自由）

Freedom of speech is a cornerstone of any open society. It allows ideas to be tested and truths to emerge. Yet it is not without limits. Speech that incites violence or spreads deliberate lies can harm real people. The challenge is to protect free expression while preventing its abuse. This is a delicate line, and reasonable people may disagree about where to draw it. But erring too far in either direction carries risks.

言论自由是任何开放社会的基石。它让思想得到检验、让真理浮现。但它并非没有限度。煽动暴力或散布蓄意谎言的言论会伤害真实的人。挑战在于保护自由表达的同时防止其被滥用。这是一条微妙的界限，理性的人可能在画界位置上存在分歧。但偏向任何一边太远都有风险。

**词与短语**：a cornerstone（基石）、incite violence（煽动暴力）、a delicate line（微妙的界限）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 may 表可能（may disagree）** —— reasonable people may disagree，may 表示可能性。

**语法示例**：
- Experts may disagree on the details.（专家可能在细节上有分歧。）
- Opinions may differ from person to person.（观点因人而异。）

### 10. Immigration（移民）

Immigration is among the most divisive issues of our time. Immigrants bring skills, energy and cultural richness, yet they can also strain public services and provoke fears. Much of the debate is driven by emotion rather than evidence. A balanced view recognizes both the contributions and the challenges. The question is not whether to have immigration, but how to manage it fairly and humanely. How a society treats newcomers says much about its character.

移民是我们这个时代最具分歧的问题之一。移民带来技能、活力和文化丰富性，但也可能给公共服务带来压力、引发恐惧。许多争论由情绪而非证据驱动。平衡的观点既承认贡献也承认挑战。问题不是要不要移民，而是如何公平、人道地管理它。一个社会如何对待新来者，很能说明它的品格。

**词与短语**：among the most divisive（最具分歧的之一）、strain public services（给公共服务带来压力）、say much about（很能说明）

**语法点睛**：**rather than（而不是）** —— driven by emotion rather than evidence，rather than 表示"而不是"。

**语法示例**：
- We should act on facts rather than fear.（我们应该依据事实而非恐惧行动。）
- She leads by example rather than by words.（她以身作则，而非空谈。）

## 三、经济与全球化

### 11. Globalization Revisited（重思全球化）

Few forces have shaped the modern world as profoundly as globalization. It has lifted millions out of poverty and spread ideas across borders. Nevertheless, its benefits have been unevenly distributed. Many workers have seen their jobs move overseas, and local cultures have been eroded. To what extent globalization is a net good remains contested. What is beyond doubt is that it cannot simply be reversed. The task is to manage it so that its rewards are shared more widely.

很少有力量能像全球化那样深刻塑造现代世界。它使数百万人摆脱贫困、让思想跨越国界。然而其收益分配并不均衡。许多工人眼睁睁看着工作流向海外，本土文化受到侵蚀。全球化在多大程度上是净收益，仍存争议。但毫无疑问的是，它无法简单地逆转。任务在于管理它，使其回报更广泛地共享。

**词与短语**：lift millions out of poverty（使数百万人摆脱贫困）、be unevenly distributed（分配不均）、be beyond doubt（毫无疑问）

**语法点睛**：**to what extent 引导主语从句** —— To what extent globalization is a net good remains contested，疑问词引导主语从句。

**语法示例**：
- To what extent this is true is unclear.（这在多大程度上属实尚不清楚。）
- Whether the plan will work remains to be seen.（计划是否有效仍有待观察。）

### 12. Inequality（不平等）

Inequality has risen sharply in recent decades. The wealthiest individuals now control a staggering share of resources, while many struggle to make ends meet. This concentration of wealth can undermine social cohesion and even democracy. Yet inequality is not inevitable; it results from policies and choices. Progressive taxation, education and social safety nets can all narrow the gap. A society that tolerates extreme inequality stores up trouble for the future.

近几十年来，不平等急剧加剧。最富有的个体如今掌控着惊人的资源份额，而许多人挣扎度日。这种财富集中会破坏社会凝聚力，甚至危及民主。然而不平等并非不可避免，它是政策和选择的结果。累进税制、教育和社会保障网都能缩小差距。容忍极端不平等的社会，是在为未来埋下隐患。

**词与短语**：a staggering share（惊人的份额）、make ends meet（勉强维持生计）、store up trouble（埋下隐患）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时 + while 对比** —— Inequality has risen...while many struggle...，现在完成时与 while 结合。

**语法示例**：
- Prices have risen, while wages have not.（物价上涨了，而工资没有。）
- Technology has advanced, while some habits have not.（技术进步了，而有些习惯没有。）

### 13. Sustainable Development（可持续发展）

Sustainable development seeks to meet present needs without compromising the future. It recognizes that economic growth, social well-being and environmental health are linked. Growth that destroys the environment is not progress but an illusion of it. The transition to sustainability requires changes in how we produce, consume and live. It is a long and difficult road, but there is no alternative. We borrow the earth from our children, not inherit it from our ancestors.

可持续发展旨在满足当下需求而不损害未来。它认识到经济增长、社会福祉和环境健康相互关联。破坏环境的增长不是进步，而是进步的假象。向可持续转型需要改变我们的生产、消费和生活方式。这是一条漫长艰难的路，但别无选择。我们是从子孙那里借用地球，而非从祖先那里继承。

**词与短语**：meet present needs（满足当下需求）、an illusion of progress（进步的假象）、borrow from our children（从子孙那里借用）

**语法点睛**：**not...but（复现）** —— not progress but an illusion of it，not...but 表示"不是……而是……"。

**语法示例**：
- This is not an end but a beginning.（这不是终点，而是起点。）
- Growth is not the goal but the means.（增长不是目的，而是手段。）

### 14. The Future of Work（工作的未来）

The nature of work is undergoing a profound transformation. Routine tasks are increasingly automated, while demand grows for creativity and interpersonal skills. Remote work has blurred the line between office and home. For workers, this brings both freedom and insecurity. The social contract around work is being rewritten, and not everyone will benefit equally. Preparing for this future requires lifelong learning and adaptable institutions.

工作的性质正经历深刻变革。常规任务日益自动化，而对创造力和人际技能的需求在增长。远程办公模糊了办公室与家的界限。对劳动者而言，这既带来自由，也带来不安。围绕工作的社会契约正在被改写，并非所有人都能平等受益。为未来做准备，需要终身学习和能灵活适应的制度。

**词与短语**：undergo a transformation（经历变革）、blur the line（模糊界限）、the social contract（社会契约）

**语法点睛**：**现在进行时的被动语态（is being rewritten）** —— The social contract is being rewritten，is being + 过去分词，表示"正在被改写"。

**语法示例**：
- The rules are being rewritten right now.（规则此刻正在被改写。）
- Our habits are being reshaped by technology.（我们的习惯正被科技重塑。）

### 15. Consumerism（消费主义）

Consumerism teaches that happiness can be bought, yet the evidence suggests otherwise. Beyond a certain point, more possessions bring little additional joy. In fact, the endless pursuit of things often leaves people feeling emptier. It also places a heavy burden on the planet. A growing number of people are turning to simpler, more meaningful ways of living. Perhaps the deepest freedom is wanting less rather than having more.

消费主义教导我们幸福可以买到，然而证据表明并非如此。超过某一点后，更多的物质带来的快乐微乎其微。事实上，无休止地追逐物质常常让人更加空虚，还给地球带来沉重负担。越来越多的人转向更简单、更有意义的生活方式。也许最深刻的自由，是想要得更少，而非拥有得更多。

**词与短语**：the evidence suggests otherwise（证据表明并非如此）、place a burden on（给……带来负担）、a simpler way of living（更简单的生活方式）

**语法点睛**：**leave + 宾语 + 宾补（leaves people feeling emptier）** —— leave somebody doing，表示"使某人处于某种状态"。

**语法示例**：
- The news left us feeling worried.（这消息让我们感到担忧。）
- Her words left me thinking deeply.（她的话让我深思。）

## 四、环境

### 16. Climate Policy（气候政策）

Addressing climate change demands collective action on an unprecedented scale. Individual choices matter, but they cannot substitute for policy. Governments must set clear targets, price carbon and invest in clean energy. The difficulty is that the costs are felt now, while the benefits are long-term. This makes climate policy politically hard but morally urgent. Delay is not a neutral option; it is a choice with consequences.

应对气候变化需要空前规模的集体行动。个人选择重要，但无法替代政策。政府必须设定明确目标、为碳定价、投资清洁能源。困难在于成本当下可见，收益却在长远。这使得气候政策在政治上艰难，在道德上紧迫。拖延并非中立选项，而是一种有后果的选择。

**词与短语**：on an unprecedented scale（以空前的规模）、substitute for（替代）、morally urgent（道德上紧迫）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作主语（Addressing）** —— Addressing climate change demands...，动名词短语作主语。

**语法示例**：
- Solving this problem needs teamwork.（解决这个问题需要团队合作。）
- Reducing emissions requires global effort.（减少排放需要全球努力。）

### 17. Biodiversity（生物多样性）

Biodiversity is the web of life that sustains our planet. Yet species are disappearing at an alarming rate, driven by habitat loss, pollution and climate change. Each extinction weakens the ecosystems on which we all depend. The loss is not just ecological but economic and cultural. Protecting biodiversity is not a luxury for nature lovers; it is a necessity for survival. We are part of nature, not apart from it.

生物多样性是维系地球的生命之网。然而，由于栖息地丧失、污染和气候变化，物种正以惊人的速度消失。每一次灭绝都削弱了我们赖以生存的生态系统。这种损失不仅是生态的，也是经济和文化的。保护生物多样性不是自然爱好者的奢侈品，而是生存的必需品。我们是自然的一部分，而非置身其外。

**词与短语**：the web of life（生命之网）、at an alarming rate（以惊人的速度）、a necessity for survival（生存的必需品）

**语法点睛**：**过去分词作状语（driven by...）** —— species are disappearing...driven by habitat loss，过去分词短语表原因。

**语法示例**：
- Driven by curiosity, she explored the forest.（出于好奇，她探索了森林。）
- Inspired by the story, he wrote a poem.（受这个故事启发，他写了一首诗。）

### 18. The Circular Economy（循环经济）

The linear economy—take, make, dispose—is reaching its limits. A circular economy, by contrast, designs waste out of the system. Products are made to be repaired, reused and recycled. This approach conserves resources and reduces pollution. Transitioning to it requires innovation in design and changes in consumer behavior. The circular economy is not just an environmental ideal; it is an economic opportunity.

线性经济——取用、制造、丢弃——正逼近极限。相比之下，循环经济从设计上消除浪费。产品被设计为可修复、可重复使用、可回收。这种方式节约资源、减少污染。向它转型需要设计创新和消费者行为的改变。循环经济不只是环境理想，也是经济机遇。

**词与短语**：reach its limits（逼近极限）、design waste out（从设计上消除浪费）、an economic opportunity（经济机遇）

**语法点睛**：**不定式的被动语态（to be repaired）** —— Products are made to be repaired, reused and recycled，to be + 过去分词。

**语法示例**：
- The house is designed to be energy-saving.（这房子被设计成节能的。）
- The device is made to be easily fixed.（这个设备被造得易于维修。）

### 19. The Energy Transition（能源转型）

The shift from fossil fuels to clean energy is the defining project of our age. It is technically feasible but politically and economically challenging. Renewable energy has become dramatically cheaper, yet storage and grids remain obstacles. The transition will create winners and losers, and a just transition must support those displaced. The pace of change is still too slow to meet climate goals. Urgency and fairness must go hand in hand.

从化石燃料向清洁能源的转变，是我们这个时代的决定性工程。它在技术上可行，但在政治和经济上充满挑战。可再生能源已大幅降价，但储能和电网仍是障碍。转型会造就赢家和输家，公正的转型必须支持受冲击者。变化的速度仍太慢，无法实现气候目标。紧迫与公平必须并行。

**词与短语**：the defining project（决定性工程）、feasible（可行的）、go hand in hand（并行）

**语法点睛**：**too...to（太……而不能）** —— too slow to meet climate goals，too + 形容词 + to do，表示"太……以致不能"。

**语法示例**：
- The change is too slow to make a difference.（变化太慢，难以带来不同。）
- The problem is too complex to solve alone.（问题太复杂，无法独自解决。）

### 20. Environmental Ethics（环境伦理）

Environmental ethics asks what we owe to nature and to future generations. For much of history, nature was seen as a resource to be exploited. A growing movement argues that nature has value beyond its use to us. This shift in thinking has profound implications for policy and daily life. We are not owners of the earth but stewards of it. The choices we make today echo through time.

环境伦理追问我们欠自然和后代什么。在历史的大部分时间里，自然被视为可开发的资源。一场日益壮大的运动主张，自然具有超越其使用价值的价值。这一思想转变对政策和日常生活有深远影响。我们不是地球的主人，而是它的守护者。我们今天的选择会在时间长河中回响。

**词与短语**：owe to（亏欠）、be exploited（被开发）、stewards of the earth（地球的守护者）

**语法点睛**：**what 引导的宾语从句（what we owe）** —— Environmental ethics asks what we owe...，what 引导宾语从句。

**语法示例**：
- We must consider what we leave behind.（我们必须考虑留下什么。）
- She asked what we could do to help.（她问我们能做什么来帮忙。）

## 五、教育与知识

### 21. The Value of Knowledge（知识的价值）

Knowledge is often valued for its usefulness, but its worth runs deeper. It frees the mind from ignorance and equips us to question authority. A society that neglects knowledge becomes vulnerable to manipulation and error. Knowledge is also cumulative; each generation builds on the last. To dismiss learning as impractical is to misunderstand what makes civilization possible. We are the inheritors of a long tradition of inquiry.

知识常因其有用而受重视，但它的价值更为深刻。它把心灵从无知中解放，赋予我们质疑权威的能力。忽视知识的社会容易受到操纵和谬误的侵害。知识还是累积的，每一代都建立在前一代之上。把学习斥为不切实际，就是误解了文明得以可能的原因。我们是一长串求索传统的继承者。

**词与短语**：run deeper（更为深刻）、question authority（质疑权威）、dismiss as（斥之为）

**语法点睛**：**不定式作主语（To dismiss...is...）** —— To dismiss learning as impractical is to misunderstand...，不定式作主语和表语。

**语法示例**：
- To give up now is to lose everything.（现在放弃就是失去一切。）
- To understand others is to understand ourselves.（理解他人就是理解自己。）

### 22. Education Reform（教育改革）

Education systems everywhere are under pressure to change. The world students will enter is very different from the one their parents knew. Yet many schools still teach as they did a century ago. Reform should focus less on memorizing facts and more on learning how to learn. Creativity, collaboration and critical thinking matter more than ever. The goal of education is not to fill minds but to kindle them.

各地的教育体系都面临变革压力。学生将进入的世界与父母所知的截然不同。然而许多学校仍像一百年前那样教学。改革应少聚焦于死记硬背，多聚焦于学会学习。创造力、协作和批判性思维比以往更重要。教育的目标不是填满头脑，而是点燃头脑。

**词与短语**：be under pressure（面临压力）、memorize facts（死记硬背）、kindle minds（点燃头脑）

**语法点睛**：**less...more（少……多……）** —— focus less on memorizing and more on learning，less/more 形成对比。

**语法示例**：
- We should talk less and listen more.（我们应该少说多听。）
- Focus less on speed and more on quality.（少关注速度，多关注质量。）

### 23. Lifelong Learning（终身学习）

The idea that education ends with graduation is obsolete. In a world of rapid change, skills can become outdated within years. Lifelong learning is no longer a luxury but a survival skill. It keeps the mind sharp and the career resilient. Fortunately, technology has made learning more accessible than ever. The question is not whether to keep learning, but what to learn next.

"教育随毕业而结束"的观念已经过时。在快速变化的世界里，技能可能在几年内过时。终身学习不再是奢侈品，而是生存技能。它让头脑敏锐、让职业有韧性。幸运的是，科技让学习比以往更易获得。问题不是是否继续学习，而是下一步学什么。

**词与短语**：become obsolete（变得过时）、a survival skill（生存技能）、accessible（易获得的）

**语法点睛**：**more accessible than ever（复现）** —— more accessible than ever，比较级 + than ever 表示"比以往更……"。

**语法示例**：
- Knowledge is more available than ever before.（知识比以往更容易获取。）
- Learning is more flexible than ever.（学习比以往更灵活。）

### 24. Science and Society（科学与社会）

Science has transformed human life more than any other force. Yet its authority is increasingly questioned in some quarters. Part of the problem is that science is often poorly communicated. Scientists and the public can seem to inhabit different worlds. Bridging this gap is essential for addressing challenges like climate change and public health. A society that distrusts science is poorly equipped for the future.

科学对人类生活的改变超过任何其他力量。然而在某些地方，它的权威正日益受到质疑。部分问题在于科学常常被糟糕地传达。科学家与公众似乎生活在不同世界。弥合这一鸿沟对于应对气候变化、公共卫生等挑战至关重要。不信任科学的社会，为未来所做的准备是欠缺的。

**词与短语**：more than any other force（超过任何其他力量）、be poorly communicated（被糟糕地传达）、be poorly equipped（准备欠缺）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more than any other（复现）** —— more than any other force，比较级 + any other 表示"比任何其他都……"。

**语法示例**：
- This invention changed life more than any other.（这项发明比任何其他都更改变生活。）
- She worked harder than any other student.（她比任何其他学生都努力。）

### 25. The Limits of Expertise（专业的局限）

Experts play a vital role in a complex society, yet expertise has its limits. Experts can be wrong, biased or captured by vested interests. The pandemic showed both the value and the limits of expert advice. This does not mean we should dismiss expertise, but that we should engage with it critically. The wisest approach is to respect knowledge while questioning authority. Humility and curiosity serve us better than blind faith.

专家在复杂社会中扮演重要角色，但专业也有局限。专家可能出错、有偏见或被既得利益俘获。疫情既展示了专业建议的价值，也展示了其局限。这并不意味着我们应摒弃专业，而是应批判性地对待它。最明智的做法是尊重知识、同时质疑权威。谦逊和好奇比盲信更能帮助我们。

**词与短语**：vested interests（既得利益）、engage with it critically（批判性地对待）、blind faith（盲信）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can + be 表可能（can be wrong）** —— Experts can be wrong, biased...，can 表示"可能"。

**语法示例**：
- Even experts can be mistaken.（即使是专家也可能出错。）
- Good advice can be hard to find.（好建议可能很难找到。）

## 六、心理与哲学

### 26. The Nature of Happiness（幸福的本质）

Happiness is one of those words everyone understands until asked to define it. Some treat it as a destination to reach; others, as a journey to savor. Philosophers have long argued that happiness is not something we stumble upon but something we practice. It grows not from the abundance of what we possess, but from the depth of what we appreciate. To be happy, then, is less a matter of changing our circumstances than of changing our gaze.

幸福是那种人人都懂、直到被要求下定义时才说不清道不明的词。有人视之为待抵达的终点，有人视之为待品味的旅程。哲学家们早就主张，幸福不是偶然撞见的东西，而是修炼出来的东西。它的生长不源于我们拥有的丰盛，而源于我们感恩的深度。那么，要幸福，与其改变处境，不如改变目光。

**词与短语**：stumble upon（偶然撞见）、the abundance of what we possess（拥有的丰盛）、change our gaze（改变目光）

**语法点睛**：**not...but（复现）** —— not something we stumble upon but something we practice，not...but 表对比。

**语法示例**：
- Success is not what we find but what we build.（成功不是我们找到的，而是我们建立的。）
- Peace is not a place but a state of mind.（平静不是地点，而是一种心境。）

### 27. Free Will（自由意志）

Do we truly choose our actions, or are they determined by causes beyond our control? This ancient question still divides philosophers and scientists. Some argue that our decisions are the product of brain processes we do not control. Others insist that the experience of choosing is too fundamental to dismiss. Whatever the answer, the belief in free will shapes how we hold people responsible. The debate is not merely academic; it touches the foundations of ethics and law.

我们真的在选择自己的行为，还是被我们无法控制的原因所决定？这个古老的问题至今仍让哲学家和科学家分歧。有人认为我们的决定是我们无法控制的大脑过程的产物，有人坚持选择感太根本、无法否定。无论答案如何，对自由意志的信念塑造了我们追究责任的方式。这场辩论并非纯学术，它触及伦理与法律的根基。

**词与短语**：divide philosophers（使哲学家分歧）、too fundamental to dismiss（太根本而无法否定）、hold responsible（追究责任）

**语法点睛**：**whatever 引导让步从句（Whatever the answer）** —— Whatever the answer...，whatever 表示"无论什么"。

**语法示例**：
- Whatever the result, we tried our best.（无论结果如何，我们尽力了。）
- Whatever happens, stay calm.（无论发生什么，保持冷静。）

### 28. Identity（身份认同）

Who are we, really? Identity is partly given—by family, culture and history—and partly chosen. In the modern world, people are freer than ever to shape their own identities. Yet this freedom can also be disorienting. The search for identity is a search for a coherent story about oneself. It is never finished, and that is perhaps the point. We are always becoming who we are.

我们究竟是谁？身份一部分是既定的——由家庭、文化和历史赋予——一部分是选择的。在现代世界，人们比以往更自由地塑造自己的身份。然而这种自由也可能令人迷失。对身份的追寻，是对关于自己的连贯故事的追寻。它永无完结，而这或许正是意义所在。我们始终在成为自己。

**词与短语**：partly given, partly chosen（部分是既定，部分是选择）、disorienting（令人迷失的）、a coherent story（连贯的故事）

**语法点睛**：**破折号插入语（—by family...—）** —— 破折号引出插入语，补充说明"既定"的来源。

**语法示例**：
- The answer—simple but deep—changed my view.（那个答案——简单却深刻——改变了我的看法。）
- Her dream—to become a doctor—drove her forward.（她的梦想——成为一名医生——推动她前进。）

### 29. The Meaning of Life（生命的意义）

The question of life's meaning has occupied thinkers for millennia. Some find meaning in purpose, others in love or service. What matters is not so much the answer as the search itself. A meaningful life is often one lived for something larger than oneself. This can be family, craft, faith or a cause. Meaning is not discovered once and for all; it is created day by day. The unexamined life, as Socrates warned, is not worth living.

生命意义的问题困扰了思想家数千年。有人从目标中寻找意义，有人从爱或奉献中寻找。重要的与其说是答案，不如说是追寻本身。有意义的人生，往往是为比自身更大的事物而活——可以是家庭、事业、信仰或事业。意义不是一劳永逸地发现的，而是日复一日创造的。正如苏格拉底警告的，未经审视的人生不值得过。

**词与短语**：occupy thinkers（困扰思想家）、something larger than oneself（比自身更大的事物）、day by day（日复一日）

**语法点睛**：**not so much...as（与其说……不如说）** —— not so much the answer as the search itself，比较结构。

**语法示例**：
- It is not so much talent as effort that matters.（与其说是天赋，不如说是努力。）
- The issue is not so much money as time.（问题与其说是钱，不如说是时间。）

### 30. Mindfulness（正念）

Mindfulness is the practice of paying attention to the present moment without judgment. In a world of constant distraction, it is a rare and valuable skill. It teaches us to notice our thoughts without being swept away by them. Research suggests it can reduce stress and improve well-being. Yet mindfulness is not a quick fix; it requires patient practice. Its deepest gift is the ability to be fully present to our own lives.

正念是不加评判地关注当下的练习。在充满干扰的世界里，这是一项稀有而宝贵的技能。它教会我们觉察思绪而不被其裹挟。研究表明它能减轻压力、改善幸福感。然而正念不是速效药，它需要耐心的练习。它最深的馈赠，是能全然在场于自己生活的能力。

**词与短语**：the present moment（当下）、be swept away（被裹挟）、a quick fix（速效药）

**语法点睛**：**without + 动名词（without being swept away）** —— without being swept away，without 后接动名词。

**语法示例**：
- We should act without hesitating.（我们应该毫不犹豫地行动。）
- She listens without interrupting.（她倾听而不打断。）

## 七、文化与艺术

### 31. The Purpose of Art（艺术的目的）

What is art for? Some say it exists for beauty, others for self-expression. Art also serves deeper purposes: it can challenge, console and connect. Great art holds a mirror to society and lets us see ourselves more clearly. It gives form to emotions that words cannot capture. In times of crisis, people turn to art for meaning and comfort. Art is not a luxury; it is a fundamental human need.

艺术是为了什么？有人说它为了美，有人说为了自我表达。艺术还有更深的目的：它能挑战、慰藉和连接。伟大的艺术为社会竖起镜子，让我们更清楚地看见自己。它为言语无法捕捉的情感赋予形式。在危机时刻，人们向艺术寻求意义和慰藉。艺术不是奢侈品，而是人类的基本需求。

**词与短语**：hold a mirror to（为……竖起镜子）、give form to（赋予形式）、turn to art（向艺术寻求）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can（复现）** —— it can challenge, console and connect，can 后接三个并列动词原形。

**语法示例**：
- Art can inspire, comfort and unite.（艺术能激励、慰藉和凝聚。）
- A story can teach, move and change us.（一个故事能教导、打动和改变我们。）

### 32. Cultural Identity（文化认同）

Cultural identity is the sense of belonging to a particular group and its traditions. It provides roots in a rapidly changing world. Yet identity is not fixed; it evolves as cultures meet and mix. The challenge is to honor one's heritage while remaining open to others. A confident culture can engage with the world without fear of losing itself. Identity is strengthened, not weakened, by genuine exchange.

文化认同是对特定群体及其传统的归属感。在快速变化的世界里，它提供根基。然而认同并非固定不变，它随着文化的相遇与交融而演变。挑战在于尊重自己的传承，同时对他人保持开放。一个自信的文化能与世界交流而不必担心失去自我。真正的交流只会加强而非削弱认同。

**词与短语**：a sense of belonging（归属感）、provide roots（提供根基）、honor one's heritage（尊重传承）

**语法点睛**：**被动语态（is strengthened, not weakened）** —— Identity is strengthened, not weakened，被动语态 + not 对比。

**语法示例**：
- Trust is built, not bought.（信任是建立的，不是买来的。）
- Character is forged, not given.（品格是锤炼的，不是天生的。）

### 33. Language and Thought（语言与思维）

The relationship between language and thought has long fascinated scholars. Do we think in words, or merely clothe thoughts in words afterward? Some argue that language shapes how we perceive the world; others insist thought is largely universal. Wherever the truth lies, a richer vocabulary does not merely describe a richer inner life—it helps create it. To learn a word is to acquire a tool for noticing what previously escaped us.

语言与思维的关系长期吸引着学者。我们是用词语思考，还是仅在事后给思想披上词语的外衣？有人认为语言塑造我们感知世界的方式，有人坚持思维在很大程度上是普遍的。无论真相何在，更丰富的词汇不只是描述更丰富的内心——它本身就帮助创造这种内心。学会一个词，就是获得一件察觉从前被忽略之物的工具。

**词与短语**：fascinate scholars（吸引学者）、clothe thoughts in words（给思想披上词语外衣）、escape us（被我们忽略）

**语法点睛**：**wherever 引导让步从句（Wherever the truth lies）** —— Wherever the truth lies...，wherever 表示"无论在哪里"。

**语法示例**：
- Wherever you go, keep learning.（无论你去哪，都要持续学习。）
- Wherever the truth lies, we must seek it.（无论真相何在，我们都必须追寻它。）

### 34. The Media（媒体）

The media is often called the fourth estate, reflecting its power to hold authority to account. Yet this power carries responsibility. A media driven by profit and attention may sensationalize rather than inform. The rise of social platforms has fragmented the public conversation. In this environment, the ability to evaluate sources has become essential. A free but irresponsible press can be as harmful as a censored one.

媒体常被称为"第四权力"，反映其监督权力的力量。然而这一力量伴随着责任。被利润和流量驱动的媒体可能耸人听闻而非如实报道。社交平台的兴起割裂了公共对话。在这种环境下，评估信息来源的能力变得至关重要。自由但不负责任的媒体，其危害不亚于被审查的媒体。

**词与短语**：the fourth estate（第四权力）、hold to account（监督/问责）、sensationalize（耸人听闻）

**语法点睛**：**as...as（复现）** —— as harmful as a censored one，as + 形容词 + as 表同级比较。

**语法示例**：
- Silence can be as harmful as lies.（沉默可能和谎言一样有害。）
- A careless word can hurt as much as a blow.（一句无心的话和一拳一样伤人。）

### 35. Literature and Empathy（文学与同理心）

Reading literature is often said to build empathy. By entering the minds of characters unlike ourselves, we learn to see the world through other eyes. A novel can make us feel the weight of choices we will never face. In this way, literature is a training ground for compassion. It reminds us that other people have inner lives as rich as our own. Few activities expand the moral imagination more powerfully.

人们常说阅读文学能培养同理心。进入与自己不同的人物的内心，我们学会透过他人的眼睛看世界。一部小说能让我们感受到我们永远不会面对的选择的分量。就这样，文学成了慈悲的训练场。它提醒我们，他人有着和我们一样丰富的内心。很少有活动能比文学更有力地拓展道德想象力。

**词与短语**：see through other eyes（透过他人的眼睛看）、a training ground for compassion（慈悲的训练场）、the moral imagination（道德想象力）

**语法点睛**：**被动语态（is often said）** —— Reading literature is often said to build empathy，is said to do 表示"据说"。

**语法示例**：
- This book is said to change lives.（据说这本书能改变人生。）
- He is said to be very wise.（据说他非常睿智。）

## 八、健康与医学

### 36. Public Health（公共卫生）

Public health is often invisible when it works. Clean water, vaccines and sanitation have saved more lives than any medicine. Yet because their success is quiet, they are easily taken for granted. The pandemic was a stark reminder of how much we depend on public health. Investment in prevention is far cheaper than treating disease. A society that neglects public health pays a heavy price in the end.

公共卫生在起作用时常常是隐形的。清洁的水、疫苗和卫生设施拯救的生命比任何药物都多。然而正因其成功悄无声息，它们容易被当作理所当然。疫情是对我们多么依赖公共卫生的严峻提醒。投资预防远比治病便宜。忽视公共卫生的社会最终要付出沉重代价。

**词与短语**：save more lives than（比……拯救更多生命）、be taken for granted（被当作理所当然）、a stark reminder（严峻的提醒）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时 + 比较级（have saved more lives than）** —— have saved more lives than any medicine，现在完成时 + 比较级。

**语法示例**：
- Prevention has saved more lives than cure.（预防比治疗拯救了更多生命。）
- Vaccines have prevented more deaths than drugs.（疫苗比药物预防了更多死亡。）

### 37. Mental Health（心理健康）

For decades, mental health was a topic rarely discussed in public. Those who suffered often did so in silence. Fortunately, attitudes have shifted, and mental well-being is now recognized as no less important than physical health. Yet stigma persists, and treatment remains hard to reach for many. Addressing this requires not only better services but a cultural change. Seeking help is not a sign of weakness but an act of courage.

几十年来，心理健康是一个很少被公开讨论的话题。受困扰者往往默默承受。幸运的是，观念已经转变，心理健康如今被认识到与身体健康同等重要。然而污名依然存在，对许多人来说治疗仍难获得。解决这一问题不仅需要更好的服务，还需要文化转变。求助不是软弱的标志，而是勇气的表现。

**词与短语**：do so in silence（默默承受）、no less important than（不亚于）、an act of courage（勇气的表现）

**语法点睛**：**no less...than（不亚于）** —— no less important than physical health，no less + 形容词 + than 表示"不亚于"。

**语法示例**：
- Rest is no less vital than work.（休息和工作的必要性不亚于彼此。）
- Kindness is no less powerful than strength.（善良的力量不亚于强壮。）

### 38. Medical Ethics（医学伦理）

Medical ethics grapples with some of the hardest questions we face. How should scarce organs be allocated? How much risk is acceptable in treatment? Who decides when life support should end? These questions cannot be answered by science alone; they require values. Principles like autonomy, beneficence and justice guide the profession. As medicine advances, the ethical dilemmas multiply. Wisdom must keep pace with technology.

医学伦理面对的是我们所面临的一些最艰难的问题。稀缺的器官该如何分配？治疗中多大的风险可接受？谁来决定何时终止生命支持？这些问题不能仅靠科学回答，还需要价值观。自主、行善、公正等原则指导着这个行业。随着医学进步，伦理困境成倍增加。智慧必须与技术同步。

**词与短语**：grapple with（努力应对）、allocate organs（分配器官）、keep pace with（与……同步）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词被动（cannot be answered）** —— cannot be answered by science alone，情态动词 + 被动语态。

**语法示例**：
- These questions cannot be answered easily.（这些问题无法轻易回答。）
- The risk must be weighed carefully.（风险必须仔细权衡。）

### 39. Aging（衰老）

Aging is not merely a biological process; it is a social and philosophical one. Modern medicine has extended life, but has it extended the quality of life? Many societies are unprepared for an aging population. Older people are often marginalized despite their experience and wisdom. A humane society values its elders and supports their dignity. How we treat the old reveals our deepest values. We will all, if fortunate, grow old.

衰老不仅是生物学过程，也是社会与哲学过程。现代医学延长了寿命，但它延长了生活质量吗？许多社会对老龄化人口准备不足。老年人尽管拥有经验和智慧，却常被边缘化。人道的社会尊重长者、维护他们的尊严。我们如何对待老人，揭示了最深的价值观。幸运的话，我们都会变老。

**词与短语**：the quality of life（生活质量）、be marginalized（被边缘化）、reveal our values（揭示我们的价值观）

**语法点睛**：**插入语（if fortunate）** —— We will all, if fortunate, grow old，if fortunate 是省略的插入条件。

**语法示例**：
- We will all, if we live long enough, face aging.（活得够久，我们都会面对衰老。）
- The plan, if successful, will help many.（这个计划若成功，将帮助很多人。）

### 40. Health Inequality（健康不平等）

Health is profoundly unequal. Where people are born, how much they earn and what they eat all shape how long they live. The wealthy enjoy longer, healthier lives than the poor in the same city. This gap is not primarily about medical care but about social conditions. Addressing health inequality means tackling poverty, housing and education. Health is a mirror that reflects a society's fairness.

健康是极不平等的。人们出生在哪里、收入多少、吃什么，都影响着他们的寿命。在同一座城市里，富人比穷人享有更长、更健康的生命。这一差距主要不在于医疗，而在于社会条件。解决健康不平等，意味着解决贫困、住房和教育。健康是一面镜子，映照出一个社会的公平程度。

**词与短语**：profoundly unequal（极不平等）、tackle poverty（解决贫困）、a mirror（一面镜子）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 longer, healthier than（复现）** —— longer, healthier lives than the poor，比较级并列 + than。

**语法示例**：
- The rich live longer, healthier lives than the poor.（富人比穷人活得更长、更健康。）
- Better education leads to better, fairer chances.（更好的教育带来更好、更公平的机会。）

## 九、批判与思辨

### 41. Critical Thinking（批判性思维）

In an age of information overload, critical thinking has never been more valuable. We are bombarded with claims competing for our trust. Critical thinking lets us distinguish evidence from assertion and detect bias. It is not a habit of doubting everything, but a method of arriving at well-founded conclusions. What we need is not less information but better judgment. And judgment must be cultivated through practice and the courage to question even our own beliefs.

在信息过载的时代，批判性思维从未如此宝贵。我们被争夺信任的各种论断狂轰滥炸。批判性思维让我们区分证据与断言、察觉偏见。它不是凡事怀疑的习惯，而是得出有充分依据结论的方法。我们需要的不是更少的信息，而是更好的判断。而判断必须通过实践和勇于质疑自己信念的勇气来培养。

**词与短语**：be bombarded with（被……狂轰滥炸）、distinguish from（区分）、well-founded conclusions（有充分依据的结论）

**语法点睛**：**强调结构 What we need is...（我们需要的是……）** —— What we need is not less information but better judgment，what 引导主语从句 + 表语。

**语法示例**：
- What we need is not more speed but more care.（我们需要的不是更快，而是更用心。）
- What matters is not the answer but the question.（重要的不是答案，而是问题。）

### 42. Bias（偏见）

Bias is a subtle and pervasive force. We like to think we are objective, yet our judgments are shaped by hidden assumptions. Confirmation bias leads us to favor evidence that supports what we already believe. Awareness of bias is the first step toward overcoming it. This requires intellectual humility and a willingness to be wrong. The goal is not perfect objectivity but honest inquiry. We should hold our opinions lightly.

偏见是一股微妙而普遍的力量。我们喜欢自认为客观，然而我们的判断受隐藏假设的塑造。确认偏误让我们偏爱支持既有观点的证据。意识到偏见是克服它的第一步。这需要智识上的谦逊和愿意认错的胸怀。目标不是完美的客观，而是诚实的探究。我们应轻握自己的观点。

**词与短语**：a pervasive force（普遍的力量）、confirmation bias（确认偏误）、hold our opinions lightly（轻握观点）

**语法点睛**：**定语从句 that（复现）** —— evidence that supports what we already believe，that 引导定语从句。

**语法示例**：
- We favor ideas that agree with us.（我们偏爱与我们一致的观点。）
- Seek evidence that challenges your view.（寻找挑战你观点的证据。）

### 43. The Post-truth Era（后真相时代）

We live, some say, in a post-truth era, where feelings trump facts and narratives trump evidence. Social media rewards outrage over accuracy. False stories spread faster than true ones. This poses a grave threat to democratic debate. Restoring a shared reality is among the great challenges of our time. It requires better media literacy and institutions worthy of trust. Truth is fragile, and once lost, it is hard to recover.

有人说，我们生活在一个后真相时代——情绪压倒事实，叙事压倒证据。社交媒体奖励愤怒而非准确。虚假故事比真实故事传播得更快。这对民主辩论构成严重威胁。重建共同的现实感是我们这个时代的重大挑战之一。这需要更好的媒体素养和值得信赖的制度。真相是脆弱的，一旦失去便难以恢复。

**词与短语**：trump facts（压倒事实）、reward outrage（奖励愤怒）、a shared reality（共同的现实）

**语法点睛**：**where 引导非限制性定语从句** —— a post-truth era, where feelings trump facts，where 补充说明时代。

**语法示例**：
- We live in an age where information flows freely.（我们生活在一个信息自由流动的时代。）
- This is a world where attention is scarce.（这是一个注意力稀缺的世界。）

### 44. Argumentation（论证）

A good argument is not a fight but a search for truth. It presents reasons, considers counterarguments and aims at understanding. Many disagreements fail because people argue to win rather than to learn. The strongest positions are those tested against the best objections. Listening well is as important as speaking well. The measure of an argument is not how loudly it is made but how well it holds up. We reason best when we reason together.

好的论证不是争斗，而是对真理的探寻。它提出理由、考虑反方、旨在理解。许多分歧失败，是因为人们为赢而辩，而非为学而辩。最强的立场，是经得起最有力反驳检验的立场。善于倾听与善于表达同样重要。衡量论证的标准，不在于它多响亮，而在于它多站得住脚。我们共同思考时，思考得最好。

**词与短语**：a search for truth（对真理的探寻）、argue to win（为赢而辩）、hold up（站得住脚）

**语法点睛**：**as...as（复现）** —— Listening well is as important as speaking well，同级比较。

**语法示例**：
- Asking well is as important as answering well.（善于提问和善于回答同样重要。）
- Patience is as valuable as talent.（耐心和天赋一样宝贵。）

### 45. Skepticism（怀疑精神）

Skepticism is often misunderstood as cynicism, but they are opposites. The skeptic asks for evidence, while the cynic assumes the worst. Healthy skepticism is the engine of science and good journalism. It protects us from fraud and folly. Yet skepticism must be balanced with openness, for the closed mind learns nothing. The goal is to doubt wisely, not to doubt everything. An examined belief is stronger than an inherited one.

怀疑精神常被误解为犬儒主义，但二者相反。怀疑者要求证据，犬儒者假定最坏。健康的怀疑是科学和好新闻的动力，它保护我们免受欺诈和愚蠢。然而怀疑必须与开放相平衡，因为封闭的心灵什么都学不到。目标在于明智地怀疑，而非怀疑一切。经过审视的信念，比承袭而来的信念更牢固。

**词与短语**：be misunderstood as（被误解为）、the engine of science（科学的动力）、doubt wisely（明智地怀疑）

**语法点睛**：**while 表对比（复现）** —— The skeptic asks for evidence, while the cynic assumes the worst，while 连接对比。

**语法示例**：
- The skeptic questions, while the cynic dismisses.（怀疑者质疑，犬儒者否定。）
- One builds, while the other tears down.（一个建设，另一个拆毁。）

## 十、个人与社会责任

### 46. Civic Duty（公民责任）

Democracy rests on the active participation of ordinary citizens. Voting is the most basic duty, but it is only the beginning. Civic life also means staying informed, serving on juries and speaking up for what is right. When citizens disengage, power flows to the few. Apathy is not neutrality; it is a choice that favors the status quo. A healthy democracy depends on people who care enough to act.

民主建立在普通公民积极参与之上。投票是最基本的责任，但这只是开始。公民生活还意味着保持知情、担任陪审员、为正义发声。当公民脱离参与时，权力便流向少数人。冷漠并非中立，而是一种偏袒现状的选择。健康的民主，依赖于足够在意、愿意行动的人。

**词与短语**：rest on（建立在……之上）、stay informed（保持知情）、favor the status quo（偏袒现状）

**语法点睛**：**动名词并列（staying, serving, speaking）** —— means staying informed, serving on juries and speaking up，动名词并列作宾语。

**语法示例**：
- Being a citizen means voting, paying taxes and staying informed.（做公民意味着投票、纳税、保持知情。）
- Helping means giving time, money or attention.（帮助意味着付出时间、金钱或关注。）

### 47. Leadership（领导力）

Leadership is the art of turning shared purpose into action. It is not the same as authority or rank; titles do not make leaders. True leaders inspire trust and empower those around them. They take responsibility when things go wrong and share credit when things go right. Leadership is exercised as much in quiet decisions as in public moments. In every field, the quality of leadership shapes the quality of results.

领导力是把共同目标转化为行动的艺术。它不同于权威或职位，头衔造就不了领导者。真正的领导者激发信任、赋能周围的人。出错时他们承担责任，顺利时分享功劳。领导力既体现在公开时刻，也体现在安静的决断中。在每个领域，领导力的质量塑造着成果的质量。

**词与短语**：turn purpose into action（把目标转化为行动）、empower those around（赋能周围的人）、share credit（分享功劳）

**语法点睛**：**as much...as（复现）** —— as much in quiet decisions as in public moments，as much...as 表同等程度。

**语法示例**：
- Leadership shows as much in listening as in speaking.（领导力在倾听与表达中同等体现。）
- Character shows as much in small acts as in big ones.（品格在小事与大事中同样体现。）

### 48. Ethics in Business（商业伦理）

Business and ethics are too often seen as opposites. Yet a business that cheats its customers or exploits its workers cannot thrive for long. Trust is the currency of commerce; once lost, it is hard to regain. Ethical companies attract loyal customers and committed employees. Doing the right thing is not a cost but an investment. In the long run, integrity and profit are not enemies but allies.

商业与伦理常被视为对立。然而欺骗顾客、剥削员工的生意难以长久兴旺。信任是商业的货币，一旦失去便难以挽回。有道德的公司吸引忠诚的顾客和尽职的员工。做正确的事不是成本，而是投资。从长远看，诚信与利润不是敌人，而是盟友。

**词与短语**：be seen as opposites（被视为对立）、the currency of commerce（商业的货币）、in the long run（从长远看）

**语法点睛**：**not...but（复现）** —— not a cost but an investment；not enemies but allies，两处 not...but。

**语法示例**：
- Honesty is not a weakness but a strength.（诚实不是弱点，而是力量。）
- Reputation is not a luxury but an asset.（声誉不是奢侈品，而是资产。）

### 49. Social Responsibility（社会责任）

We are not isolated individuals; our choices ripple through society. What we buy, how we treat others and whether we vote all shape the world around us. Social responsibility means recognizing this power and using it well. It is easy to blame institutions while ignoring our own role. Change begins when ordinary people take responsibility for the common good. The world becomes better through countless small acts of conscience.

我们不是孤立的个体，我们的选择会在社会中泛起涟漪。我们买什么、如何待人、是否投票，都塑造着周围的世界。社会责任意味着认识到这种力量并善用它。责备制度而忽视自身角色是容易的。当普通人开始为公共利益承担责任时，改变就开始了。世界因无数小小的良知之举而变得更美好。

**词与短语**：ripple through（泛起涟漪）、blame institutions（责备制度）、the common good（公共利益）

**语法点睛**：**三个并列的主语从句（What..., how..., whether...）** —— What we buy, how we treat others and whether we vote all shape...，并列名词性从句作主语。

**语法示例**：
- What we say, how we act and who we help all define us.（我们说什么、如何做、帮助谁，都定义着我们。）
- Where we live, what we eat and how we rest all affect health.（住在哪、吃什么、如何休息都影响健康。）

### 50. The Individual and Society（个人与社会）

The relationship between the individual and society is a perennial theme. We need society for security, education and meaning; society needs us for vitality and renewal. The tension lies in balancing personal freedom with social obligation. Too much conformity crushes the spirit; too little leaves chaos. A good society makes room for both the individual and the collective. The whole is built, after all, from the contributions of each.

个人与社会的关系是一个永恒的主题。我们需要社会提供安全、教育和意义；社会需要我们提供活力与更新。张力在于平衡个人自由与社会义务。过度的顺从压垮精神，过度的放任导致混乱。好的社会为个人与集体都留有空间。毕竟，整体是由每个人的贡献构建而成的。

**词与短语**：a perennial theme（永恒的主题）、balance freedom with obligation（平衡自由与义务）、make room for（为……留空间）

**语法点睛**：**too much...too little 对偶** —— Too much conformity crushes the spirit; too little leaves chaos，用分号连接对偶结构。

**语法示例**：
- Too much work exhausts us; too little bores us.（工作太多让我们疲惫，太少让我们无聊。）
- Too much caution stalls us; too little endangers us.（过于谨慎让我们停滞，过于冒进让我们危险。）

## 十一、人工智能

### 51. The Rise of AI（AI 的崛起）

Artificial intelligence has advanced faster than almost anyone predicted. Tasks that once required human judgment are now handled by machines. From medicine to finance, AI is reshaping entire fields. This progress brings enormous promise, yet it also brings uncertainty. We have never faced a technology that learns and improves on its own. The rise of AI forces us to ask what it means to be human.

人工智能的进步比几乎任何人预测的都快。曾经需要人类判断的任务如今由机器处理。从医疗到金融，AI 正在重塑整个领域。这一进步带来巨大希望，也带来不确定性。我们从未面对过一种能自主学习、自我改进的技术。AI 的崛起迫使我们追问：做人意味着什么。

**词与短语**：faster than predicted（比预测更快）、reshape fields（重塑领域）、enormous promise（巨大希望）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时 + 比较级（has advanced faster than）** —— has advanced faster than almost anyone predicted，现在完成时 + 比较级。

**语法示例**：
- Technology has changed faster than we expected.（科技变化比我们预期的更快。）
- The world has changed more than we imagined.（世界变化超乎我们的想象。）

### 52. AI and Creativity（AI 与创造力）

Can machines be truly creative? AI can now write poems, compose music and paint pictures. Yet creativity involves more than producing new things. It includes emotion, intention and personal experience. A machine can imitate style, but can it feel the joy of creation? I believe AI can assist human creativity, not replace it. The spark of true art remains human.

机器能真正具有创造力吗？如今 AI 能写诗、作曲、画画。然而创造力不只是产出新事物，还包括情感、意图和个人经历。机器能模仿风格，但它能感受创作的喜悦吗？我认为 AI 能辅助人类的创造力，而非取代它。真正艺术的火花依然属于人类。

**词与短语**：compose music（作曲）、imitate style（模仿风格）、the spark of true art（真正艺术的火花）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can + 疑问句** —— Can machines be truly creative? can 表能力，疑问句引出思辨。

**语法示例**：
- Can a machine truly understand beauty?（机器能真正理解美吗？）
- Can technology ever replace human warmth?（技术能取代人类的温暖吗？）

### 53. Machine Morality（机器道德）

As machines make more decisions, the question of machine morality grows urgent. Should a self-driving car protect its passenger or a pedestrian? Who is responsible when an AI makes a harmful choice? These questions have no easy answers. We must build ethics into technology from the start. Machines cannot be moral, but they can be designed to reflect our values. The responsibility for their choices ultimately rests with us.

随着机器做出更多决定，机器道德的问题日益紧迫。自动驾驶汽车该保护乘客还是行人？AI 做出有害选择时谁来负责？这些问题没有简单的答案。我们必须从一开始就把伦理融入技术。机器无法有道德，但可以被设计为反映我们的价值观。它们选择的责任最终在我们。

**词与短语**：machine morality（机器道德）、a self-driving car（自动驾驶汽车）、build ethics into（把伦理融入）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 should 疑问 + 被动（should...protect）** —— Should a self-driving car protect...，情态动词用于提问和表达义务。

**语法示例**：
- Should machines make life-or-death choices?（机器该做生死抉择吗？）
- Who should be responsible for the damage?（谁该为损害负责？）

### 54. AI and Employment（AI 与就业）

AI will not simply destroy jobs; it will transform them. Some tasks will disappear, while new roles will emerge. The workers who thrive will be those who can learn and adapt. Governments and companies must invest in retraining. If we manage this transition well, AI can raise living standards. The danger lies not in the technology but in our failure to prepare. Adaptation is the key to survival.

AI 不会简单地摧毁工作，而是会改造工作。有些任务会消失，同时新岗位会出现。能够学习和适应的劳动者才会兴旺。政府和公司必须投资再培训。如果我们妥善管理这一转型，AI 能提高生活水平。危险不在技术，而在我们未能做好准备。适应是生存的关键。

**词与短语**：transform jobs（改造工作）、new roles（新岗位）、invest in retraining（投资再培训）

**语法点睛**：**第一条件句（If...can）** —— If we manage this transition well, AI can raise living standards，if 条件句 + can。

**语法示例**：
- If we prepare well, change can benefit us.（准备充分，变化就能造福我们。）
- If we ignore the shift, we may fall behind.（忽视转变，我们可能落后。）

### 55. The Singularity（奇点）

Some futurists predict a technological singularity—a point where AI surpasses human intelligence. If such a moment arrives, its consequences would be impossible to predict. It could bring untold benefits or unprecedented risks. Whether it will happen remains deeply uncertain. We should prepare for the possibility without being paralyzed by fear. The future is not written; it is shaped by our choices. Wisdom, not speed, should guide our steps.

一些未来学家预言技术奇点——AI 超越人类智能的时刻。如果这一时刻到来，其后果将无法预测。它可能带来难以言说的好处，或前所未有的风险。它是否会发生仍极不确定。我们应为这种可能做准备，而不被恐惧所麻痹。未来尚未写就，它由我们的选择塑造。指引我们脚步的应是智慧，而非速度。

**词与短语**：the singularity（奇点）、surpass human intelligence（超越人类智能）、be paralyzed by fear（被恐惧麻痹）

**语法点睛**：**第二条件句（If...would）** —— If such a moment arrived, its consequences would be impossible，虚拟假设。

**语法示例**：
- If this happened, the results would be hard to predict.（如果这发生，结果将难以预测。）
- If we acted wisely, we would avoid the risks.（如果我们明智行动，就能避免风险。）

## 十二、生物伦理

### 56. Gene Editing（基因编辑）

Gene editing holds the power to cure inherited diseases. By changing DNA, scientists may eliminate conditions that have plagued families for generations. Yet the same technology raises profound ethical questions. Should we edit genes to enhance, not just to heal? Where do we draw the line between treatment and enhancement? The power to rewrite life must be used with great care. We are, in a sense, becoming editors of our own species.

基因编辑拥有治愈遗传病的力量。通过改变 DNA，科学家或许能消除困扰家族数代的疾病。然而同样的技术引发了深刻的伦理问题。我们是否应该编辑基因来增强，而不只是治疗？治疗与增强的界限在哪里？改写生命的力量必须慎之又慎地使用。从某种意义上说，我们正在成为自己物种的编辑者。

**词与短语**：gene editing（基因编辑）、cure inherited diseases（治愈遗传病）、draw the line（划界）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 may/must（may eliminate / must be used）** —— may 表可能，must be used 表义务 + 被动。

**语法示例**：
- This power must be handled with care.（这种力量必须谨慎对待。）
- The line between healing and enhancing may blur.（治疗与增强的界限可能模糊。）

### 57. Cloning（克隆）

Cloning raises questions that science alone cannot answer. If we could clone a human being, should we? The technology challenges our ideas of identity and individuality. A clone would share DNA but not memories or experience. Each person, clone or not, would still be a unique individual. The debate is less about science than about values. What we can do is not the same as what we should do.

克隆提出的问题是科学单独无法回答的。如果我们能克隆一个人，我们该这样做吗？这项技术挑战了我们对身份和个体性的观念。克隆人共享 DNA，但不共享记忆和经历。每个人，无论是否克隆，仍将是独特的个体。这场辩论与其说是关于科学，不如说是关于价值观。我们能做什么，不等于我们该做什么。

**词与短语**：clone a human being（克隆一个人）、individuality（个体性）、a unique individual（独特的个体）

**语法点睛**：**第二条件句（If we could...should we?）** —— If we could clone a human being, should we?，虚拟假设 + 疑问。

**语法示例**：
- If we could live forever, should we?（如果我们能永生，我们该吗？）
- If it were possible, would it be right?（如果可能，那对吗？）

### 58. Designer Babies（设计婴儿）

The idea of "designer babies" provokes both hope and horror. Some dream of eliminating disease before birth. Others fear a future of genetic inequality. If only the wealthy can afford enhancement, the gap will widen. This raises questions about fairness and human dignity. We must decide what kind of future we want. Technology gives us options; values tell us which to choose.

"设计婴儿"的构想既引发希望，也引发恐惧。有人梦想在出生前消除疾病。有人担心基因不平等的未来。如果只有富人能负担增强，差距就会扩大。这引发了关于公平和人类尊严的问题。我们必须决定想要怎样的未来。技术给我们选项，价值观告诉我们要选哪个。

**词与短语**：designer babies（设计婴儿）、provoke hope and horror（引发希望与恐惧）、genetic inequality（基因不平等）

**语法点睛**：**第一条件句（If...will）** —— If only the wealthy can afford enhancement, the gap will widen，真实条件句。

**语法示例**：
- If access is unequal, the gap will grow.（如果获取机会不平等，差距就会扩大。）
- If we are careless, the risks will rise.（如果我们粗心，风险就会上升。）

### 59. End-of-Life Decisions（临终决定）

As medicine advances, decisions about the end of life grow more complex. When should treatment stop, and who should decide? These are among the hardest questions a family can face. Respect for life must be balanced with mercy for the suffering. Clear wishes, expressed in advance, can ease the burden on loved ones. Dignity at the end of life is a profound human concern. We should talk about death before it is too late.

随着医学进步，关于临终的决定日益复杂。治疗该何时停止，谁该决定？这些是一个家庭可能面对的最艰难的问题之一。对生命的尊重必须与对痛苦的怜悯相平衡。提前表达的明确意愿能减轻亲人的负担。生命尽头的尊严是深刻的人类关切。我们应该趁早谈论死亡。

**词与短语**：end-of-life decisions（临终决定）、respect for life（对生命的尊重）、ease the burden（减轻负担）

**语法点睛**：**被动语态 + 情态动词（must be balanced）** —— Respect for life must be balanced with mercy，must be + 过去分词。

**语法示例**：
- Life and mercy must be weighed together.（生命与怜悯必须一起权衡。）
- Wishes expressed early can be honored later.（提前表达的意愿日后能得到尊重。）

### 60. The Ethics of Enhancement（增强的伦理）

Should we enhance healthy humans, not just heal the sick? Enhancement might make us smarter, stronger or longer-lived. Yet it could also deepen inequality and change human nature. If enhancement is available only to the rich, society will divide further. The question is not merely technical but deeply moral. We are deciding what humanity should become. Some doors, once opened, cannot be closed again.

我们应该增强健康的人，而不只是治疗病人吗？增强或许让我们更聪明、更强壮、更长寿。但它也可能加深不平等、改变人性。如果增强只有富人能获得，社会将进一步分裂。这个问题不仅是技术的，更是深刻道德的。我们正在决定人类应该成为什么。有些门一旦打开，就无法再关上。

**词与短语**：enhance humans（增强人类）、deepen inequality（加深不平等）、change human nature（改变人性）

**语法点睛**：**比较级（smarter, stronger, longer-lived）** —— make us smarter, stronger or longer-lived，比较级并列。

**语法示例**：
- Enhancement might make us faster and stronger.（增强或许让我们更快、更强。）
- The rich may grow richer and the gap wider.（富人可能更富，差距更大。）

## 十三、气候变化

### 61. The Carbon Budget（碳预算）

The carbon budget is the amount of emissions we can still release. Beyond it, warming becomes dangerously severe. We have already used much of this budget. The remaining amount demands urgent and deep cuts. Every year of delay shrinks our room for action. The science is clear; the politics is the hard part. We are running out of time to spend wisely.

碳预算是我们还能排放的量。超出它，变暖就会危险地加剧。我们已经用掉了预算的大部分。剩余的量要求紧迫而深刻的减排。每拖延一年，我们的行动空间就缩小一分。科学是清晰的，难在政治。我们正在耗尽明智使用的时间。

**词与短语**：the carbon budget（碳预算）、deep cuts（深刻减排）、run out of time（耗尽时间）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时（have used）** —— We have already used much of this budget，现在完成时强调已用掉。

**语法示例**：
- We have already spent most of our time.（我们已经花掉了大部分时间。）
- They have used up the available funds.（他们已经用完了可用的资金。）

### 62. Climate Justice（气候正义）

Climate change is not experienced equally. The poorest countries, which contributed least to emissions, suffer the most. This is the heart of climate justice. Those who caused the problem should help those who bear its costs. Yet reaching agreement on this has proven difficult. Wealthy nations have both the means and the duty to lead. Justice demands that the burden be shared fairly. The climate crisis is, at bottom, a moral crisis.

气候变化并非被平等地承受。排放贡献最少的贫困国家受害最深。这正是气候正义的核心。造成问题者应帮助承担代价者。然而就此达成一致已被证明很难。富裕国家既有能力也有责任带头。正义要求负担被公平分担。气候危机归根结底是道德危机。

**词与短语**：climate justice（气候正义）、contribute to emissions（造成排放）、bear the costs（承担代价）

**语法点睛**：**which 非限制性定语从句（复现）** —— The poorest countries, which contributed least...，which 补充说明。

**语法示例**：
- The small island, which lies near the sea, is at risk.（靠近海的小岛正面临风险。）
- The plan, which everyone supported, finally failed.（那个人人支持的计划最终失败了。）

### 63. Adaptation and Mitigation（适应与减缓）

Facing climate change requires both mitigation and adaptation. Mitigation means cutting emissions to slow warming. Adaptation means preparing for changes already underway. Neither alone is enough; we need both. Some damage is now unavoidable, so we must adapt. At the same time, we must cut emissions to limit future harm. The two strategies are partners, not rivals. We need to act on both fronts at once.

应对气候变化需要减缓和适应并行。减缓意味着减排以放缓变暖。适应意味着为已在发生的变化做准备。单靠任何一方都不够，我们需要两者。有些损害如今已不可避免，所以我们必须适应。同时，我们必须减排以限制未来的伤害。这两种策略是伙伴，而非对手。我们需要同时在这两条战线上行动。

**词与短语**：mitigation and adaptation（减缓与适应）、slow warming（放缓变暖）、on both fronts（在两条战线）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作主语（Mitigation means...）** —— Mitigation means cutting emissions，动名词作主语和宾语。

**语法示例**：
- Adapting means changing how we live.（适应意味着改变我们的生活方式。）
- Mitigating means reducing the harm we cause.（减缓意味着减少我们造成的伤害。）

### 64. The Role of Individuals（个人的角色）

Can individual actions matter against a global crisis? It is easy to feel powerless, but our choices add up. What we eat, how we travel and what we buy all have climate impact. Millions of small changes create enormous collective effect. Yet individual action cannot replace policy. We must both live responsibly and demand better laws. Personal change and political change go hand in hand.

面对全球危机，个人行动能起作用吗？感到无力很容易，但我们的选择会累积。我们吃什么、如何出行、买什么，都有气候影响。数百万个小小改变会产生巨大的集体效应。然而个人行动无法替代政策。我们既要负责任地生活，也要要求更好的法律。个人改变与政治改变并行不悖。

**词与短语**：feel powerless（感到无力）、add up（累积）、collective effect（集体效应）

**语法点睛**：**疑问句 + 三个并列主语从句（What we eat, how..., what we buy）** —— 并列的名词性从句作主语。

**语法示例**：
- What we choose and how we live both matter.（我们的选择和生活方式都重要。）
- Where we shop and how we travel both count.（我们在哪购物、如何出行都算数。）

### 65. Hope for the Planet（地球的希望）

It is easy to despair about the climate, but despair is not an option. Around the world, people are taking action and winning victories. Renewable energy is growing faster than expected. Public awareness has never been higher. These trends give real grounds for hope. Hope is not passive; it is a call to act. We can still shape a livable future if we act now.

对气候感到绝望很容易，但绝望不是选项。在世界各地，人们正在采取行动、赢得胜利。可再生能源的增长快于预期，公众意识空前高涨。这些趋势给了我们真实的希望理由。希望不是被动的，而是行动的召唤。如果现在行动，我们仍能塑造一个宜居的未来。

**词与短语**：despair about（对……绝望）、win victories（赢得胜利）、grounds for hope（希望的理由）

**语法点睛**：**第一条件句（if we act）** —— We can still shape a livable future if we act now，if 条件句。

**语法示例**：
- If we unite, we can still make a difference.（团结起来，我们仍能带来改变。）
- If we act today, tomorrow can be better.（今天行动，明天会更好。）

## 十四、收入不平等

### 66. The Wealth Gap（财富差距）

The wealth gap has widened to levels not seen in a century. A tiny fraction of people now owns a huge share of global wealth. Meanwhile, billions struggle to meet basic needs. This concentration of wealth is both an economic and a moral issue. It can undermine trust, opportunity and social stability. A society that allows such gaps stores up future trouble. Shared prosperity is the only durable foundation.

财富差距已扩大到百年未见的水平。极少数人如今拥有全球财富的巨大份额。与此同时，数十亿人难以满足基本需求。这种财富集中既是经济问题，也是道德问题。它会削弱信任、机会和社会稳定。允许如此差距的社会是在为未来埋下隐患。共同繁荣才是唯一持久的基础。

**词与短语**：the wealth gap（财富差距）、a tiny fraction（极少数）、meet basic needs（满足基本需求）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时 + 比较级（has widened to levels）** —— has widened to levels not seen in a century，现在完成时。

**语法示例**：
- The gap has grown faster than anyone expected.（差距扩大得比任何人预期的都快。）
- Wealth has concentrated in fewer hands.（财富集中在更少的人手中。）

### 67. The Middle Class（中产阶级）

The middle class has long been the backbone of stable societies. It drives consumption, innovation and social cohesion. Yet in many countries, it has been shrinking. Jobs that once supported middle-class life have vanished. Rising costs squeeze families from both sides. A shrinking middle class is a warning sign for democracy. Healthy societies need a broad and secure middle.

中产阶级长期以来是稳定社会的支柱。它推动消费、创新和社会凝聚力。然而在许多国家，它一直在萎缩。曾经支撑中产生活的岗位已经消失。不断上涨的成本从两侧挤压家庭。中产的萎缩是民主的警示信号。健康的社会需要广泛而有保障的中产。

**词与短语**：the backbone of society（社会的支柱）、social cohesion（社会凝聚力）、a warning sign（警示信号）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时（has been shrinking）** —— it has been shrinking，现在完成进行时强调持续萎缩。

**语法示例**：
- The middle class has been getting smaller.（中产阶级一直在变小。）
- Prices have been rising for years.（物价多年来一直在上涨。）

### 68. Inequality and Democracy（不平等与民主）

Extreme inequality can quietly corrode democracy. When wealth is concentrated, so is power. The rich can influence policy in ways ordinary citizens cannot. This undermines the principle of equal voice. Democracy assumes that every person counts equally. When money speaks louder than votes, that promise is broken. A democracy that tolerates extreme inequality is a democracy at risk.

极端不平等会悄然侵蚀民主。财富集中之处，权力也随之集中。富人能以普通公民无法企及的方式影响政策。这破坏了平等发声的原则。民主的前提是人人平等地重要。当金钱比选票更响亮时，这一承诺就被打破了。容忍极端不平等的民主是岌岌可危的民主。

**词与短语**：corrode democracy（侵蚀民主）、equal voice（平等发声）、speak louder than votes（比选票更响亮）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 louder than（复现）** —— money speaks louder than votes，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- Wealth can speak louder than justice.（财富可能比正义更响亮。）
- Influence can outweigh fairness.（影响力可能压过公平。）

### 69. Universal Basic Income（全民基本收入）

Universal basic income (UBI) is a bold proposal: give everyone a regular payment. Supporters say it would reduce poverty and provide security. Critics warn it would be costly and might discourage work. The evidence so far is mixed and incomplete. Whether UBI works depends on how it is designed. If funded fairly, it could be a powerful tool against insecurity. The debate is about values as much as economics.

全民基本收入（UBI）是一项大胆的提议：给每个人定期发放一笔钱。支持者说它能减少贫困、提供保障。批评者警告它成本高昂，可能抑制工作意愿。迄今为止的证据混杂且不完整。UBI 是否有效取决于如何设计。如果资金筹集公平，它可能成为对抗不安全感的有力工具。这场辩论关乎价值观，也关乎经济。

**词与短语**：universal basic income（全民基本收入）、reduce poverty（减少贫困）、discourage work（抑制工作意愿）

**语法点睛**：**第二条件句（If funded...could）** —— If funded fairly, it could be a powerful tool，虚拟条件（省略 it were）。

**语法示例**：
- If done right, it could help many.（做得好，它能帮助很多人。）
- If funded well, the plan could work.（资金充足，计划就能奏效。）

### 70. Fair Taxation（公平税制）

Taxation is how a society pools resources for the common good. A fair tax system asks more of those who can afford more. Yet the wealthy often pay lower effective rates than workers. Loopholes and avoidance erode the tax base. This shifts the burden onto those least able to bear it. Fair taxation is not punishment; it is investment in a shared future. A society's tax system reveals its true priorities.

税收是社会为公共利益汇集资源的方式。公平的税制要求能承担更多的人多承担。然而富人支付的实际税率往往低于工薪阶层。漏洞和避税侵蚀了税基。这把负担转嫁给了最无力承担的人。公平税制不是惩罚，而是对共同未来的投资。一个社会的税制揭示了它真正的优先事项。

**词与短语**：pool resources（汇集资源）、effective rates（实际税率）、erode the tax base（侵蚀税基）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more of those（更多）+ 被动** —— asks more of those who can afford more，比较级 + 定语从句。

**语法示例**：
- A fair system asks more of those who have more.（公平的制度要求拥有更多的人多承担。）
- The burden should fall on those who can bear it.（负担应落在能承担的人身上。）

## 十五、教育公平

### 71. Equal Access to Education（教育机会平等）

Education is often called the great equalizer, but only if access is equal. Children from wealthy families enjoy better schools and more support. Those born poor face obstacles from their earliest years. This gap in opportunity can last a lifetime. Narrowing it requires investment in early education and struggling schools. A society that wastes talent through inequality harms itself. Equal education is the engine of equal opportunity.

教育常被称为伟大的均衡器，但前提是机会平等。富裕家庭的孩子享受更好的学校和更多支持。出身贫困的孩子从幼年起就面临障碍。这种机会差距可能持续一生。缩小它需要在早期教育和薄弱学校上投资。因不平等而浪费人才的社会，伤害的是自己。平等教育是平等机会的引擎。

**词与短语**：the great equalizer（伟大的均衡器）、face obstacles（面临障碍）、a lifetime（一生）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 better schools, more support（复现）** —— better schools and more support，比较级并列。

**语法示例**：
- Rich children get better resources and more chances.（富孩子获得更好的资源和更多机会。）
- Equal access means fairer outcomes for all.（平等的机会意味着对所有人更公平的结果。）

### 72. The Cost of Education（教育成本）

The cost of education has risen far faster than incomes. Tuition, books and living costs burden families. Many students graduate with heavy debts. This debt can delay marriage, home-buying and risk-taking. When education becomes unaffordable, talent is wasted. We must find ways to make learning accessible to all. The price of ignorance is far higher than the cost of education.

教育成本的增长远远快于收入。学费、书本和生活成本让家庭不堪重负。许多学生毕业时背负沉重债务。这些债务会推迟结婚、买房和冒险。当教育变得负担不起时，人才就被浪费了。我们必须找到让学习惠及所有人的办法。无知的代价远高于教育的成本。

**词与短语**：rise faster than（比……增长更快）、graduate with debts（毕业时负债）、unaffordable（负担不起的）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 faster than（复现）** —— has risen far faster than incomes，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- Costs have risen faster than wages.（成本上涨快于工资。）
- Prices grew faster than expected.（价格涨得比预期快。）

### 73. Rural Education（乡村教育）

Rural schools face challenges that urban schools rarely see. They often lack teachers, resources and funding. Talented young teachers prefer to work in cities. This leaves rural children with fewer opportunities. Yet every child deserves a quality education, wherever they live. Technology can help bridge the gap. Investment in rural education is investment in the whole nation.

乡村学校面临城市学校很少遇到的挑战。它们常常缺乏教师、资源和资金。有才华的年轻教师更愿在城市工作。这让乡村孩子的机会更少。然而每个孩子都值得优质教育，无论住在哪里。技术可以帮助弥合差距。投资乡村教育就是投资整个国家。

**词与短语**：rural schools（乡村学校）、lack teachers（缺乏教师）、bridge the gap（弥合差距）

**语法点睛**：**wherever 引导让步从句（复现）** —— wherever they live，wherever 表示"无论在哪里"。

**语法示例**：
- Every child matters, wherever they are born.（每个孩子都重要，无论出生在哪里。）
- Talent exists everywhere, wherever it is found.（人才无处不在，无论在哪里被发现。）

### 74. Education and Social Mobility（教育与社会流动）

Education is the most powerful engine of social mobility. It allows children to rise beyond the circumstances of their birth. A poor but talented student can, through learning, change her destiny. Yet this ladder is broken in many places. When opportunity depends on wealth, mobility stalls. Restoring the ladder of education is a moral priority. A just society lets talent, not birth, decide.

教育是社会流动最强大的引擎。它让孩子超越出身的境遇。一个贫穷但有才华的学生，能通过学习改变命运。然而在许多地方，这架阶梯已经断裂。当机会取决于财富时，流动就停滞了。修复教育阶梯是道德上的优先事项。公正的社会让才华而非出身决定命运。

**词与短语**：social mobility（社会流动）、rise beyond circumstances（超越境遇）、a moral priority（道德优先事项）

**语法点睛**：**最高级 the most powerful（复现）** —— the most powerful engine，the most + 形容词。

**语法示例**：
- Education is the most effective way to change lives.（教育是改变人生最有效的方式。）
- Knowledge is the most valuable inheritance.（知识是最宝贵的遗产。）

### 75. The Future of Schools（学校的未来）

What will schools look like in the future? Classrooms may become more flexible and personalized. Technology may allow students to learn at their own pace. Teachers may shift from lecturers to guides. Yet the human element will remain essential. Learning is not just information transfer; it is relationship. The best future schools will combine technology with humanity. The goal stays the same: to kindle young minds.

未来的学校会是什么样？课堂可能变得更灵活、更个性化。技术可能让学生按自己的节奏学习。教师可能从讲授者转变为引导者。然而人的因素仍将不可或缺。学习不只是信息传递，更是关系。最好的未来学校将把技术与人性结合。目标不变：点燃年轻的心灵。

**词与短语**：learn at their own pace（按自己的节奏学习）、shift from...to...（从……转向……）、kindle young minds（点燃年轻心灵）

**语法点睛**：**may + 动词原形（may become）** —— Classrooms may become more flexible，may 表对将来的推测。

**语法示例**：
- Future schools may look very different.（未来的学校可能大不相同。）
- Learning may become more personalized.（学习可能变得更个性化。）

## 十六、文化多样性

### 76. The Value of Diversity（多样性的价值）

Diversity is not a slogan but a source of strength. Different perspectives lead to better ideas and solutions. A team of varied backgrounds sees problems from more angles. Diversity also enriches culture, food and art. Yet diversity requires effort: we must learn to listen across differences. A society that embraces diversity is more creative and resilient. Uniformity, by contrast, breeds stagnation.

多样性不是口号，而是力量的源泉。不同的视角带来更好的想法和解决方案。背景多样的团队能从更多角度看问题。多样性还丰富文化、美食和艺术。然而多样性需要努力：我们必须学会跨越差异倾听。拥抱多样性的社会更有创造力、更具韧性。相比之下，千篇一律滋生停滞。

**词与短语**：a source of strength（力量的源泉）、varied backgrounds（多样的背景）、embrace diversity（拥抱多样性）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more angles / more creative（复现）** —— more angles、more creative and resilient，比较级。

**语法示例**：
- Different voices produce better answers.（不同的声音产生更好的答案。）
- Diverse teams are more innovative.（多元的团队更有创新力。）

### 77. Cultural Appropriation（文化挪用）

Cultural appropriation has become a heated topic. It refers to adopting elements of a culture without understanding or respect. Borrowing can be appreciation, but it can also be exploitation. The line between the two is often blurred and contested. What feels like tribute to some may feel like theft to others. The key is respect, context and consent. We should learn from other cultures without taking what is not ours.

文化挪用已成为一个热门话题。它指的是在不理解、不尊重的情况下采用一种文化的元素。借鉴可以是欣赏，但也可能是剥削。两者之间的界限常常模糊且有争议。对一些人来说像致敬，对另一些人可能像偷窃。关键在于尊重、语境和同意。我们应该向其他文化学习，而不拿走不属于我们的东西。

**词与短语**：cultural appropriation（文化挪用）、blurred and contested（模糊且有争议）、tribute（致敬）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作主语（Borrowing）** —— Borrowing can be appreciation，动名词作主语。

**语法示例**：
- Learning from others is a virtue.（向他人学习是美德。）
- Taking without asking is wrong.（不问自取是错误的。）

### 78. Language Loss（语言流失）

Languages are disappearing at an alarming rate. When a language dies, a unique way of seeing the world dies with it. Knowledge, stories and wisdom vanish forever. Globalization, though it connects us, also erases linguistic diversity. We should document and protect endangered languages. Every language is a library of human experience. Losing one is like burning a book that has no copy.

语言正以惊人的速度消失。一种语言死亡时，一种看待世界的独特方式也随之消亡。知识、故事和智慧永远消失。全球化虽然连接我们，却也抹去了语言多样性。我们应该记录和保护濒危语言。每种语言都是一座人类经验的图书馆。失去一种语言，就像烧掉一本没有副本的书。

**词与短语**：at an alarming rate（以惊人的速度）、die with it（随之消亡）、linguistic diversity（语言多样性）

**语法点睛**：**though 插入让步（though it connects us）** —— Globalization, though it connects us, also erases...，though 引导让步从句。

**语法示例**：
- Change, though it brings progress, also brings loss.（变化虽带来进步，也带来损失。）
- The city, though it is busy, has its charm.（这座城市虽然繁忙，却有其魅力。）

### 79. Multiculturalism（多元文化主义）

Multiculturalism holds that different cultures can coexist and enrich each other. In diverse cities, people of many backgrounds live side by side. They share streets, schools and workplaces. This mixing can spark creativity and understanding. Yet it also requires tolerance and open dialogue. A multicultural society must balance unity with difference. The challenge is to celebrate diversity without losing common ground.

多元文化主义认为不同文化可以共存并相互丰富。在多元的城市里，不同背景的人们比邻而居。他们共享街道、学校和工作场所。这种交融能激发创造力和理解。然而它也需要宽容和开放的对话。多元文化社会必须平衡统一与差异。挑战在于赞颂多样性而不失去共同基础。

**词与短语**：multiculturalism（多元文化主义）、side by side（比邻而居）、common ground（共同基础）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 must（复现）** —— A multicultural society must balance unity with difference，must 表义务。

**语法示例**：
- We must balance freedom with order.（我们必须平衡自由与秩序。）
- A diverse society must find common ground.（多元社会必须找到共同基础。）

### 80. Unity in Diversity（多样中的统一）

The motto "unity in diversity" captures a deep ideal. It means we can be different and yet belong together. Our differences—of language, faith and custom—do not have to divide us. What unites us can be stronger than what separates us. Shared values and common goals can bridge our gaps. This unity is not uniformity; it is harmony. The strongest societies are those that embrace both.

"多样中的统一"这句格言捕捉了一个深刻的理想。它意味着我们可以彼此不同，却仍归属在一起。我们的差异——语言、信仰和习俗——不必使我们分裂。使我们团结的东西可以强于使我们分离的东西。共同的价值观和目标能弥合我们的差距。这种统一不是千篇一律，而是和谐。最强大的社会是那些两者兼纳的社会。

**词与短语**：unity in diversity（多样中的统一）、belong together（归属在一起）、bridge our gaps（弥合差距）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 stronger than（复现）** —— What unites us can be stronger than what separates us，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- Our common ground can be deeper than our differences.（我们的共同点能比差异更深。）
- What joins us matters more than what divides us.（使我们联合的比使我们分裂的更重要。）

## 十七、媒体伦理

### 81. Journalism Ethics（新闻伦理）

Journalism carries a public trust. Journalists should report truthfully, fairly and independently. They should verify facts and correct errors promptly. Sensationalism and partisanship betray that trust. When the press fails, the public is left in the dark. A free society depends on honest reporting. Good journalism is a public service, not just a business.

新闻业承载着公众信任。记者应如实、公正、独立地报道。他们应核实事实、及时更正错误。耸人听闻和党派偏见背叛了这种信任。媒体失职时，公众就被蒙在鼓里。自由的社会依赖诚实的报道。好新闻是公共服务，而不仅仅是一门生意。

**词与短语**：a public trust（公众信任）、verify facts（核实事实）、betray that trust（背叛信任）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 should（复现）** —— Journalists should report truthfully...，should 表义务。

**语法示例**：
- Reporters should always check their sources.（记者应始终核实消息来源。）
- The media should serve the public interest.（媒体应服务公共利益。）

### 82. Privacy vs. Public Interest（隐私 vs 公共利益）

The press often faces a dilemma: privacy versus public interest. How far should journalists go to uncover a story? Public figures give up some privacy, but not all. The right to know must be weighed against the right to privacy. Sensational intrusion can destroy lives for a headline. Responsible journalism respects this delicate balance. What is legal is not always what is ethical.

媒体常常面临两难：隐私与公共利益之争。记者为挖掘新闻该走多远？公众人物放弃了一些隐私，但并非全部。知情权必须与隐私权相权衡。为了一条头条而耸人听闻地侵扰，可能毁掉人的生活。负责任的新闻尊重这种微妙的平衡。合法的并不总是合乎道德的。

**词与短语**：public interest（公共利益）、uncover a story（挖掘新闻）、a delicate balance（微妙的平衡）

**语法点睛**：**被动语态 + 情态动词（must be weighed）** —— The right to know must be weighed against...，must be + 过去分词。

**语法示例**：
- Privacy must be balanced against truth.（隐私必须与真相相平衡。）
- Two rights must be weighed carefully.（两种权利必须仔细权衡。）

### 83. Clickbait（标题党）

Clickbait trades accuracy for attention. Its headlines promise more than the story delivers. We click, and we are often disappointed. This model rewards outrage and curiosity over truth. It degrades public discourse and trust. Readers should be wary of headlines that seem too shocking. Real journalism earns attention through honesty, not tricks. We get the media we reward with our clicks.

标题党用准确性换取注意力。它的标题承诺的比正文更多。我们点击后往往失望。这种模式奖励愤怒和好奇，而非真相。它败坏了公共话语和信任。读者应警惕那些看起来过于耸动的标题。真正的新闻通过诚实而非花招赢得关注。我们用点击喂养出怎样的媒体，就得到怎样的媒体。

**词与短语**：trade accuracy for attention（用准确性换注意力）、degrade public discourse（败坏公共话语）、be wary of（警惕）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more than（复现）** —— promises more than the story delivers，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- The headline promises more than the article gives.（标题承诺的比文章给的更多。）
- Hype delivers less than it promises.（炒作兑现的比承诺的少。）

### 84. Media Ownership（媒体所有权）

Who owns the media matters. When a few companies control most outlets, diverse voices shrink. Concentrated ownership can shape what stories are told and how. It can also serve the interests of owners over the public. A healthy democracy needs many independent sources. Media diversity protects against propaganda and groupthink. Power over information is power over minds. We should ask who is behind the news we read.

谁拥有媒体很重要。当少数公司控制大多数媒体时，多元的声音就萎缩了。集中的所有权能塑造报道什么、如何报道。它还可能为所有者的利益而非公众服务。健康的民主需要众多独立的来源。媒体多样性可以抵御宣传和群体思维。对信息的权力就是对思想的权力。我们应该追问所读新闻背后的操手是谁。

**词与短语**：media ownership（媒体所有权）、concentrated ownership（集中的所有权）、groupthink（群体思维）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can（复现）** —— Concentrated ownership can shape...，can 表可能。

**语法示例**：
- Ownership can influence what we hear.（所有权能影响我们听到什么。）
- A single source can narrow our view.（单一来源会窄化我们的视野。）

### 85. The Public's Trust（公众信任）

Trust in the media has fallen sharply in recent years. People doubt what they read and watch. This erosion of trust threatens democracy itself. Without credible information, citizens cannot make sound choices. Rebuilding trust requires transparency, accuracy and accountability. The media must earn back the public's confidence. Trust, once broken, is painfully slow to restore.

近年来，公众对媒体的信任急剧下降。人们怀疑他们所读所看的一切。这种信任的侵蚀威胁着民主本身。没有可信的信息，公民无法做出明智的选择。重建信任需要透明、准确和问责。媒体必须赢回公众的信心。信任一旦破裂，恢复起来痛苦而缓慢。

**词与短语**：the erosion of trust（信任的侵蚀）、credible information（可信的信息）、accountability（问责）

**语法点睛**：**现在完成时（has fallen）** —— Trust has fallen sharply，现在完成时强调已发生的下降。

**语法示例**：
- Public trust has fallen to record lows.（公众信任已降至历史低点。）
- Confidence has eroded over the years.（信心多年来已被侵蚀。）

## 十八、国际关系

### 86. Global Cooperation（全球合作）

The greatest problems of our time do not respect borders. Climate change, pandemics and poverty are global challenges. No nation can solve them alone. Global cooperation is not idealism; it is necessity. History shows that nations achieve more together than apart. Yet cooperation requires trust, compromise and shared rules. The future of our planet depends on working together. We rise or fall as one world.

我们这个时代最大的问题不受国界限制。气候变化、疫情和贫困都是全球性挑战。没有哪个国家能独自解决。全球合作不是理想主义，而是必需。历史表明，国家团结比分裂能成就更多。然而合作需要信任、妥协和共同规则。地球的未来取决于携手合作。我们作为一个世界，一荣俱荣，一损俱损。

**词与短语**：respect borders（受国界限制）、global challenges（全球性挑战）、work together（携手合作）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more together than apart（复现）** —— nations achieve more together than apart，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- We achieve more together than alone.（我们团结比独自能成就更多。）
- Countries gain more by cooperating than by competing.（国家合作比竞争获益更多。）

### 87. Diplomacy（外交）

Diplomacy is the art of resolving differences without force. It requires patience, listening and quiet skill. Good diplomats build trust across deep divides. They seek common ground where none seems to exist. Diplomacy is slow, and its victories are often invisible. Yet war is far costlier than even imperfect peace. The work of dialogue may seem thankless, but it saves lives. Talking is always better than fighting.

外交是不诉诸武力解决分歧的艺术。它需要耐心、倾听和沉静的技巧。优秀的外交官在深刻分歧之间建立信任。他们在看似没有共同点的地方寻找共同点。外交是缓慢的，其胜利往往不可见。然而战争的代价远高于不完美的和平。对话的工作或许看似不讨喜，但它拯救生命。谈判永远好过打仗。

**词与短语**：resolve differences（解决分歧）、across deep divides（跨越深刻分歧）、seek common ground（寻找共同点）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 far costlier than（复现）** —— war is far costlier than even imperfect peace，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- Peace is cheaper than war.（和平比战争廉价。）
- Dialogue is wiser than conflict.（对话比冲突更明智。）

### 88. Conflict and Peace（冲突与和平）

Conflict is as old as humanity, but so is the longing for peace. Wars are often born of fear, pride and misunderstanding. They leave wounds that last for generations. Peace, by contrast, is built slowly through justice and trust. Lasting peace requires more than the absence of fighting. It demands reconciliation and shared prosperity. The road to peace is hard, but it is the only road worth taking. We must choose peace not once, but every day.

冲突与人类一样古老，但和平的渴望同样古老。战争往往源于恐惧、傲慢和误解。它留下持续数代的创伤。相比之下，和平是通过正义和信任缓慢建立的。持久的和平需要的不仅是停止战斗，它要求和解与共同繁荣。通往和平的道路艰难，但它是唯一值得走的路。我们必须选择和平，不是一次，而是每一天。

**词与短语**：leave wounds（留下创伤）、the absence of fighting（没有战斗）、reconciliation（和解）

**语法点睛**：**as...as（复现）** —— Conflict is as old as humanity，as + 形容词 + as 表同级比较。

**语法示例**：
- War is as old as history itself.（战争与历史本身一样古老。）
- The longing for peace is as deep as the fear of war.（对和平的渴望与对战争的恐惧一样深。）

### 89. International Law（国际法）

International law sets rules that nations agree to follow. It governs trade, human rights and the use of force. Yet it lacks the enforcement power of domestic law. Nations sometimes ignore it when it suits them. This weakness has led some to dismiss international law. But even imperfect law shapes behavior and sets standards. It gives the weak a voice against the strong. The alternative is a world ruled by force alone.

国际法规定了各国同意遵守的规则。它规范贸易、人权和武力使用。然而它缺乏国内法那样的强制力。国家有时在符合自身利益时无视它。这种弱点让一些人轻视国际法。但即使不完美的法律也能塑造行为、确立标准。它让弱者对强者有发声的渠道。否则，世界就只剩强权统治。

**词与短语**：international law（国际法）、the use of force（武力使用）、enforcement power（强制力）

**语法点睛**：**定语从句 that（复现）** —— rules that nations agree to follow，that 引导定语从句。

**语法示例**：
- Rules that nations accept shape behavior.（各国接受的规则塑造行为。）
- Laws that lack enforcement are often ignored.（缺乏执行力的法律常被无视。）

### 90. Global Governance（全球治理）

Global governance is the system of rules that manage shared problems. It includes institutions like the United Nations and trade bodies. These structures are imperfect and often criticized. Yet without them, cooperation would be far harder. Global challenges demand global responses. Reform is needed, but reform is better than collapse. We must strengthen, not abandon, our shared institutions. The world needs more coordination, not less.

全球治理是管理共同问题的规则体系。它包括联合国和贸易机构等组织。这些结构并不完美，且常受批评。然而没有它们，合作将困难得多。全球性挑战需要全球性应对。改革是必要的，但改革好过崩溃。我们必须加强而非抛弃共享的机构。世界需要更多而非更少的协调。

**词与短语**：global governance（全球治理）、shared problems（共同问题）、coordination（协调）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 better than / more...not less（复现）** —— reform is better than collapse；more coordination, not less。

**语法示例**：
- Cooperation is better than isolation.（合作好过孤立。）
- We need more dialogue, not less.（我们需要更多而非更少的对话。）

## 十九、公共政策

### 91. Evidence-based Policy（循证政策）

Good policy should rest on evidence, not ideology. Decisions should be tested against real-world data. Yet politics often favors emotion over facts. Leaders may ignore evidence that contradicts their goals. This leads to policies that sound good but fail in practice. Evidence-based policy means being willing to change course. It requires humility and a commitment to truth. What works in theory must prove itself in reality.

好的政策应建立在证据而非意识形态之上。决策应经受现实数据的检验。然而政治常常偏爱情绪胜过事实。领导者可能无视与自身目标相悖的证据。这导致听起来不错、实践中却失败的政策。循证政策意味着愿意改变路线。它需要谦逊和对真相的承诺。理论上有效的东西必须在现实中证明自己。

**词与短语**：evidence-based policy（循证政策）、rest on evidence（基于证据）、change course（改变路线）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 should / must（复现）** —— Good policy should rest on evidence...must prove itself，should 与 must 表义务。

**语法示例**：
- Decisions should be based on facts.（决策应基于事实。）
- Ideas must survive contact with reality.（想法必须经受现实的检验。）

### 92. Public Goods（公共物品）

Public goods like clean air and national defense benefit everyone. They cannot easily be provided by markets alone. This is why governments step in to supply them. Yet public goods are often undervalued precisely because they are shared. We notice them only when they are gone. Investing in public goods is investing in the common future. A society that neglects them pays the price later. We all breathe the same air; we all share the same fate.

清洁空气、国防等公共物品惠及每个人。它们难以仅靠市场提供。这就是政府介入供给的原因。然而公共物品恰恰因为共享而常被低估。我们只有在失去它们时才注意到。投资公共物品就是投资共同的未来。忽视它们的社会日后要付出代价。我们呼吸同样的空气，共享同样的命运。

**词与短语**：public goods（公共物品）、national defense（国防）、step in（介入）

**语法点睛**：**被动语态（are undervalued）** —— public goods are often undervalued，被动语态。

**语法示例**：
- Shared things are often neglected.（共享的东西常被忽视。）
- Public goods are easily taken for granted.（公共物品容易被当作理所当然。）

### 93. Regulation（监管）

Regulation is often framed as a burden on business. But good regulation protects consumers, workers and the environment. It sets the rules that make markets fair and safe. Without it, the strong would exploit the weak. The question is not whether to regulate, but how well. Over-regulation can stifle, while under-regulation can harm. Smart regulation balances freedom with protection. It is not the enemy of prosperity but its guardian.

监管常被描述为企业的负担。但好的监管保护消费者、工人和环境。它制定让市场公平、安全的规则。没有监管，强者就会剥削弱者。问题不是要不要监管，而是如何监管好。过度监管会扼杀活力，监管不足则会造成伤害。明智的监管平衡自由与保护。它不是繁荣的敌人，而是繁荣的守护者。

**词与短语**：a burden on business（企业的负担）、stifle（扼杀）、the guardian of prosperity（繁荣的守护者）

**语法点睛**：**while 表对比（复现）** —— Over-regulation can stifle, while under-regulation can harm，while 连接对比。

**语法示例**：
- Too much control stifles, while too little harms.（控制过多扼杀，过少则伤害。）
- Over-protection weakens, while under-protection risks.（过度保护削弱，保护不足则有风险。）

### 94. Policy and Behavior（政策与行为）

Policy shapes behavior in subtle and powerful ways. Tax incentives, rules and nudges steer our choices. A small change in policy can shift the actions of millions. This is why good policy design matters so much. It can encourage saving, health and public spirit. Bad policy, by contrast, can reward harmful behavior. We should design rules that bring out the best in people. The right incentives make good behavior easy and bad behavior costly.

政策以微妙而有力的方式塑造行为。税收激励、规则和助推引导着我们的选择。政策上一个小小的改变，就能改变数百万人的行动。这就是好的政策设计如此重要的原因。它能鼓励储蓄、健康和公益精神。相反，糟糕的政策会奖励有害的行为。我们应该设计出激发人性之善的规则。正确的激励让善行变易、让恶行变难。

**词与短语**：shape behavior（塑造行为）、steer our choices（引导选择）、bring out the best（激发最佳）

**语法点睛**：**比较级（make good easy and bad costly）** —— make good behavior easy and bad behavior costly，形容词作宾补。

**语法示例**：
- Good rules make honesty easy and cheating hard.（好规则让诚实容易、作弊困难。）
- The right incentives make effort rewarding.（正确的激励让努力有回报。）

### 95. Long-term Thinking（长远思考）

Short-term thinking is the enemy of good policy. Politicians chase quick wins while ignoring tomorrow. This bias toward the present costs future generations dearly. Climate, debt and infrastructure all demand long horizons. We must learn to value the future as much as the present. Institutions can help by taking a longer view. A society that plans for decades outlives one that plans for years. The future is built by those who think ahead.

短期思维是良好政策的敌人。政客追逐速胜，却忽视明天。这种偏向当下的思维让后代付出沉重代价。气候、债务和基础设施都需要长远的眼光。我们必须学会像重视当下一样重视未来。机构可以通过放长眼光来帮忙。规划数十年的社会，比规划数年的社会更长久。未来由那些未雨绸缪的人构建。

**词与短语**：short-term thinking（短期思维）、chase quick wins（追逐速胜）、a long horizon（长远眼光）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 outlives（复现）** —— A society that plans for decades outlives one that plans for years，比较级。

**语法示例**：
- Those who plan ahead outlast those who do not.（未雨绸缪者比不规划者更长久。）
- Long-term thinking outlives short-term gain.（长远思考比短期利益更持久。）

## 二十、科技创新

### 96. The Pace of Innovation（创新速度）

Innovation is accelerating at a breathtaking pace. Technologies that took decades to spread now go global in years. Each new tool builds on the last, compounding progress. This speed brings both opportunity and disorientation. We must learn faster just to keep up. The gap between change and our ability to adapt is widening. Yet acceleration also holds the promise of solving old problems. The future arrives faster than we expect.

创新正以惊人的速度加速。曾经需要数十年才普及的技术，如今几年就能遍及全球。每一种新工具都建立在前一种之上，使进步不断累积。这种速度既带来机遇，也带来迷惘。为了跟上，我们必须学得更快。变化与我们适应能力之间的差距正在扩大。然而加速也带来解决老问题的希望。未来比我们预期的来得更快。

**词与短语**：at a breathtaking pace（以惊人的速度）、go global（遍及全球）、compounding progress（累积的进步）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 faster than（复现）** —— The future arrives faster than we expect，比较级 + than。

**语法示例**：
- Change comes faster than we can adapt.（变化来得比我们适应的更快。）
- The world moves quicker than our habits.（世界变化比我们的习惯更快。）

### 97. Innovation and Risk（创新与风险）

Every innovation carries both promise and risk. The same technology that heals can also harm. Progress without caution can lead to disaster. Yet fear of risk must not freeze us into inaction. The wise path is to innovate while managing danger. We should test, learn and correct as we go. A society too afraid to fail will also fail to grow. Courage and caution must walk together.

每一项创新都既带来希望，也带来风险。治愈疾病的技术同样可能造成伤害。没有谨慎的进步可能导致灾难。然而对风险的恐惧不应让我们裹足不前。明智之路是在管理危险的同时创新。我们应该边试、边学、边纠正。一个太害怕失败的社会也无法成长。勇气与谨慎必须并肩同行。

**词与短语**：promise and risk（希望与风险）、freeze into inaction（裹足不前）、manage danger（管理危险）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 must（复现）** —— Courage and caution must walk together，must 表义务。

**语法示例**：
- Boldness must be balanced with care.（大胆必须与谨慎平衡。）
- Progress must not outrun wisdom.（进步不可跑在智慧前面。）

### 98. Open Science（开放科学）

Open science makes research freely available to all. Knowledge, once shared, multiplies. Closed science, by contrast, slows discovery and widens gaps. The pandemic showed how vital open data can be. When researchers share, progress accelerates for everyone. Openness also invites scrutiny, which strengthens findings. The more we share knowledge, the faster we solve problems. Science thrives on openness, not secrecy.

开放科学让研究对所有人免费开放。知识一旦共享，就会倍增。相比之下，封闭的科学延缓发现、扩大差距。疫情展示了开放数据何其重要。当研究者共享时，进步对所有人加速。开放还引来审视，从而强化了成果。我们分享的知识越多，解决问题就越快。科学因开放而繁荣，而非因保密。

**词与短语**：open science（开放科学）、accelerate progress（加速进步）、invite scrutiny（引来审视）

**语法点睛**：**the more...the faster（越……越……，复现）** —— The more we share knowledge, the faster we solve problems。

**语法示例**：
- The more we share, the faster we grow.（分享越多，成长越快。）
- The more open we are, the stronger we become.（越开放，越强大。）

### 99. Innovation for Good（为善创新）

Innovation is not an end in itself; it should serve human needs. The best inventions solve real problems and improve lives. We should direct creativity toward health, education and sustainability. Technology can widen or narrow inequality, depending on our choices. Innovation for good means asking who benefits. A breakthrough that helps only the few is a missed chance. The measure of progress is not novelty but human flourishing. Let our tools serve people, not the reverse.

创新不是目的本身，它应服务于人的需要。最好的发明解决真实问题、改善生活。我们应把创造力引向健康、教育和可持续。技术能扩大或缩小不平等，取决于我们的选择。为善创新意味着追问谁受益。只惠及少数人的突破是错失的机会。衡量进步的不是新奇，而是人的福祉。让工具服务人，而非相反。

**词与短语**：an end in itself（目的本身）、direct creativity toward（把创造力引向）、human flourishing（人的福祉）

**语法点睛**：**祈使句（Let our tools serve）** —— Let our tools serve people，let 引导的祈使句。

**语法示例**：
- Let technology serve humanity, not the reverse.（让技术服务人类，而非相反。）
- Let progress lift everyone, not just the few.（让进步惠及所有人，而非少数。）

### 100. The Innovation Mindset（创新思维）

The innovation mindset begins with curiosity and courage. It asks "what if" and dares to try. Failure is not a dead end but a step toward success. Innovators see problems as opportunities in disguise. This mindset can be learned and practiced. It requires openness, persistence and a willingness to question. The future belongs to those who keep asking better questions. Curiosity is the spark; action is the flame. Together, they light the way forward.

创新思维始于好奇和勇气。它追问"如果……会怎样"，并敢于尝试。失败不是死胡同，而是通往成功的一步。创新者把问题看作伪装的机会。这种思维可以学习和练习。它需要开放、坚持和敢于质疑。未来属于那些不断提出更好问题的人。好奇是火花，行动是火焰。二者结合，照亮前路。

**词与短语**：the innovation mindset（创新思维）、opportunities in disguise（伪装的机会）、light the way（照亮前路）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作主语（Failure...）** —— Failure is not a dead end，名词 + 系表；Innovators see problems as...。

**语法示例**：
- Curiosity opens doors that fear keeps closed.（好奇打开恐惧紧闭的门。）
- Asking questions is the first step to answers.（提问是得到答案的第一步。）
