# B1 分级阅读 · 中级

> 对应 CEFR B1（约 2,000–3,000 词）。50 篇短文使用现在完成时、条件句、关系从句等中级语法，适合作为独立成段表达的背诵范本。

## 一、经历与回忆

### 1. An Unforgettable Day（难忘的一天）

Two years ago, I took part in a school trip to the mountains. It was the first time I had ever been so far from home. We climbed for five hours before we reached the top. I was very tired, but the view was amazing. Since that day, I have loved hiking.

两年前，我参加了一次学校登山旅行。那是我第一次离家那么远。我们爬了五个小时才到山顶。我非常累，但景色美极了。从那以后，我就爱上了徒步。

**词与短语**：take part in（参加）、reach the top（到山顶）、since that day（从那天起）

### 2. My Childhood（我的童年）

I have many happy memories of my childhood. I grew up in a small town where everyone knew each other. My friends and I played in the streets until it got dark. I did not have many toys, but I was never bored. Those were simple but happy days.

我有很多快乐的童年回忆。我在一个大家都彼此认识的小镇长大。我和朋友们在街上玩到天黑。我没有很多玩具，但从不觉得无聊。那是简单而快乐的日子。

**词与短语**：grow up（长大）、know each other（彼此认识）、simple but happy（简单而快乐）

### 3. A Life Lesson（一个人生教训）

When I was young, I often told small lies. One day, my mother found out and was very sad. She did not shout, but she told me that trust is like paper: once it is broken, it is hard to fix. I have remembered her words ever since.

小时候我常撒小谎。有一天妈妈发现了，非常难过。她没有吼我，而是告诉我：信任就像纸，一旦破了就很难修复。从那以后我一直记得她的话。

**词与短语**：tell lies（撒谎）、find out（发现）、ever since（从那以后）

### 4. My Best Memory（我最美好的回忆）

My best memory is from a family dinner. It was a cold winter night. We sat around the table, ate hot food and talked for hours. There was no phone, no TV, just us. I realized that happiness is not about things, but about the people around you.

我最美好的回忆来自一次家庭晚餐。那是一个寒冷的冬夜。我们围坐在桌旁，吃着热饭菜，聊了好几个小时。没有手机、没有电视，只有我们。我意识到幸福不在于物质，而在于身边的人。

**词与短语**：sit around the table（围坐在桌旁）、for hours（好几个小时）、not about things（不在于物质）

### 5. A Time I Helped Someone（我帮助别人的一次经历）

Last year, I helped an old woman carry her bags. She was very thankful and smiled at me warmly. It was a small thing, but it made my whole day. Since then, I have tried to help others whenever I can. Kindness costs nothing.

去年我帮一位老奶奶提袋子。她非常感激，对我温暖地微笑。这是件小事，却让我一整天都很开心。从那以后，我尽量随时帮助别人。善良不需要任何代价。

**词与短语**：be thankful（感激）、make my day（让我开心）、whenever I can（只要我能）

## 二、梦想与目标

### 6. My Dream（我的梦想）

Everyone has a dream, and so do I. I have wanted to be a teacher since I was a child. In my opinion, teaching is a meaningful job because teachers help students become better people. I am studying hard to make my dream come true.

每个人都有一个梦想，我也是。我从小就梦想当老师。在我看来，教书是有意义的工作，因为老师帮助学生成为更好的人。我正在努力学习以实现梦想。

**词与短语**：so do I（我也是）、in my opinion（在我看来）、come true（实现）

### 7. Setting Goals（设定目标）

Setting goals is important in life. A goal gives you direction and keeps you focused. I always write down my goals at the beginning of each month. Then I break them into small steps. This way, big goals do not seem so scary.

设定目标在生活中很重要。目标给你方向，让你保持专注。我总是在每月初写下目标，然后把它们拆成小步骤。这样，大目标就不那么可怕了。

**词与短语**：set goals（设定目标）、give you direction（给你方向）、break into steps（拆成步骤）

### 8. Never Give Up（永不放弃）

There is a saying: "Never give up." I learned its meaning when I was learning to swim. At first, I was afraid of the water and wanted to stop. But my coach kept encouraging me. After weeks of practice, I finally learned. Persistence always pays off.

有句谚语叫"永不放弃"。我学游泳时明白了它的含义。起初我怕水，想放弃。但教练一直鼓励我。经过几周练习，我终于学会了。坚持总会有回报。

**词与短语**：never give up（永不放弃）、be afraid of（害怕）、pay off（有回报）

### 9. My Future Self（未来的自己）

I often think about my future self. In ten years, I hope to have a job I love and a peaceful life. I want to be a person who is kind, brave and wise. To become that person, I must start working on myself today.

我常常想象未来的自己。十年后，我希望有一份热爱的工作和宁静的生活。我想成为一个善良、勇敢、睿智的人。要成为那样的人，我必须从今天开始努力。

**词与短语**：my future self（未来的自己）、a peaceful life（宁静的生活）、work on myself（提升自己）

### 10. Chasing Success（追逐成功）

What is success? Some people think it means having a lot of money. Others believe it means being happy. In my view, success is doing something you love and doing it well. Money is important, but it is not everything. True success comes from the heart.

什么是成功？有人认为成功意味着有很多钱，有人认为意味着快乐。在我看来，成功是做自己喜欢的事并把它做好。钱很重要，但不是一切。真正的成功发自内心。

**词与短语**：in my view（在我看来）、not everything（不是一切）、from the heart（发自内心）

## 三、意见与观点

### 11. Is Social Media Good?（社交媒体好吗）

Social media has changed how we communicate. On one hand, it helps us stay in touch with friends far away. On the other hand, it can waste a lot of our time. I think we should use it wisely and not let it control our lives.

社交媒体改变了我们的沟通方式。一方面，它帮我们和远方的朋友保持联系。另一方面，它会浪费我们很多时间。我认为我们应该明智地使用它，不让它控制我们的生活。

**词与短语**：stay in touch（保持联系）、on the other hand（另一方面）、use it wisely（明智地使用）

### 12. The Best Way to Learn（最好的学习方式）

People learn in different ways. Some learn best by reading, others by listening or doing. In my opinion, the best way to learn is to practice. When you use what you learn, it stays in your memory longer. That is why practice is so important.

人们学习的方式各不相同。有人擅长阅读学习，有人擅长听或做。在我看来，最好的学习方式是练习。当你运用所学，它会在记忆中停留更久。这就是练习如此重要的原因。

**词与短语**：in different ways（以不同方式）、stay in memory（留在记忆中）、that is why（这就是为什么）

### 13. City or Countryside（城市还是乡村）

Some people prefer city life, while others love the countryside. Cities offer more jobs and entertainment, but they are noisy and crowded. The countryside is peaceful, but it has fewer opportunities. Each place has its own good and bad points.

有人喜欢城市生活，有人热爱乡村。城市提供更多工作和娱乐，但嘈杂拥挤。乡村宁静，但机会较少。每个地方都有它的优缺点。

**词与短语**：prefer（更喜欢）、offer more（提供更多）、good and bad points（优缺点）

### 14. Money and Happiness（金钱与幸福）

Can money buy happiness? Many people believe it can, but I am not so sure. Money can buy comfort, but not love or health. The happiest people are often those who are grateful for what they have. Happiness is a choice, not a price.

钱能买到幸福吗？很多人相信能，但我不太确定。钱能买到舒适，但买不到爱和健康。最幸福的人往往是那些对拥有的一切心存感激的人。幸福是一种选择，而非价格。

**词与短语**：buy happiness（买到幸福）、be grateful for（感激）、a choice（一种选择）

### 15. The Value of Time（时间的价值）

Time is the most valuable thing we have. We can earn more money, but we cannot get back lost time. That is why I try to use my time well. I make a plan for each day and focus on what really matters. Time waits for no one.

时间是我们拥有的最宝贵的东西。我们能赚更多钱，却无法找回失去的时间。所以我尽量善用时间：每天做计划，专注于真正重要的事。时间不等人。

**词与短语**：the most valuable（最宝贵的）、get back（找回）、matter（重要）

## 四、建议与决定

### 16. My Advice to a Friend（给朋友的建议）

My friend was stressed about her exams. I told her not to worry too much. I advised her to make a study plan and take breaks. I also told her that exams are not everything. A few weeks later, she thanked me for my advice.

我的朋友为考试焦虑。我告诉她别太担心，建议她制定学习计划并适当休息。我还告诉她考试不是一切。几周后，她感谢了我的建议。

**词与短语**：be stressed about（为……焦虑）、make a plan（制定计划）、thanks me for（感谢我）

### 17. A Difficult Choice（艰难的选择）

Last year I faced a difficult choice. I could study in my hometown or in a faraway city. Staying home was comfortable, but leaving offered more chances. After thinking for a long time, I chose to leave. It was hard, but I do not regret it.

去年我面临一个艰难的选择：在家乡读书，还是去远方城市。留在家很舒适，但离开有更多机会。考虑了很长时间后，我选择离开。这很难，但我不后悔。

**词与短语**：face a choice（面临选择）、a faraway city（远方城市）、regret（后悔）

### 18. Making a Decision（做一个决定）

Making decisions is part of life. Some decisions are easy, like what to eat. Others are hard, like what to study. When I have to decide something important, I write down the good and bad points. This helps me think clearly and choose better.

做决定是生活的一部分。有些决定容易，比如吃什么。有些很难，比如学什么。当我要做重要决定时，我会写下优缺点。这帮助我清晰思考，做出更好的选择。

**词与短语**：part of life（生活的一部分）、write down（写下）、think clearly（清晰思考）

### 19. Good Advice I Got（我得到的好建议）

The best advice I ever got came from my grandfather. He said: "Work hard, but also rest well. Success is a long journey, not a short race." I think about his words whenever I feel tired or want to give up.

我得到过最好的建议来自爷爷。他说："努力工作，但也要好好休息。成功是长跑，不是短跑。"每当我感到疲惫或想放弃时，我都会想起他的话。

**词与短语**：the best advice（最好的建议）、a long journey（漫长的旅程）、whenever（每当）

### 20. Learning from Mistakes（从错误中学习）

Everyone makes mistakes. The important thing is to learn from them. Last year, I failed a test because I did not prepare well. I was sad, but I did not give up. I changed my study habits and passed the next time. Mistakes can be our best teachers.

每个人都会犯错，重要的是从中学习。去年我因为准备不足而考砸了。我很难过，但没有放弃。我改变了学习习惯，下次通过了。错误可以是我们最好的老师。

**词与短语**：make mistakes（犯错）、learn from（从……学习）、our best teachers（最好的老师）

## 五、人际关系

### 21. True Friendship（真正的友谊）

A true friend is hard to find. A true friend listens to you, supports you and tells you the truth, even when it is hard. I am lucky to have a few true friends. Friendship, like a plant, needs care to grow. I try to be a good friend too.

真正的朋友很难得。真正的朋友倾听你、支持你，即使真相难以开口也如实相告。我很幸运有几个真朋友。友谊像植物，需要用心浇灌才能成长。我也努力做一个好朋友。

**词与短语**：a true friend（真正的朋友）、tell the truth（说实话）、need care（需要呵护）

### 22. My Role Model（我的榜样）

My role model is my mother. She works hard and never complains. She is kind to everyone, even strangers. She has taught me to be honest and brave. I hope to be a person like her one day.

我的榜样是我妈妈。她努力工作、从不抱怨，对每个人都友善，哪怕是陌生人。她教会我诚实和勇敢。我希望有一天能成为像她那样的人。

**词与短语**：a role model（榜样）、never complain（从不抱怨）、a person like her（像她那样的人）

### 23. Family Time（家庭时光）

In our busy lives, family time is precious. My family tries to have dinner together every day. On weekends, we go for walks or watch movies. These small moments make our family closer. I treasure the time with my family.

在忙碌的生活中，家庭时光很珍贵。我们家尽量每天一起吃晚饭。周末我们一起散步或看电影。这些小时刻让家人更亲近。我珍惜与家人相处的时光。

**词与短语**：family time（家庭时光）、have dinner together（一起吃晚饭）、treasure（珍惜）

### 24. Getting Along with Others（与他人相处）

Getting along with others is a useful skill. I try to listen more and judge less. When I disagree with someone, I speak kindly. Respect is the key to every good relationship. Treat others as you want to be treated.

与人相处是一项有用的技能。我尽量多倾听、少评判。与人意见不合时，我语气友善。尊重是每段良好关系的关键。你希望别人怎样待你，就怎样待别人。

**词与短语**：get along with（与……相处）、listen more（多倾听）、treat others（对待他人）

### 25. A Person I Admire（我敬佩的人）

The person I admire most is my English teacher. She is patient and knowledgeable. She makes every lesson interesting. More importantly, she believes in her students. Her encouragement has changed many students, including me.

我最敬佩的人是我的英语老师。她耐心又有学识，把每节课都讲得有趣。更重要的是，她相信她的学生。她的鼓励改变了很多学生，包括我。

**词与短语**：admire（敬佩）、patient and knowledgeable（耐心又有学识）、believe in（信任）

## 六、健康与生活方式

### 26. A Healthy Life（健康的生活）

A healthy life is about more than just food. It includes good sleep, regular exercise and a happy mind. I try to keep a balance in my life. I eat well, sleep enough and do sports. I also spend time with people I love. Health is wealth.

健康的生活不只是关于饮食，还包括良好的睡眠、规律的运动和愉快的心情。我努力保持生活的平衡：吃得好、睡得够、多运动，也和爱的人在一起。健康就是财富。

**词与短语**：more than（不只是）、keep a balance（保持平衡）、health is wealth（健康是财富）

### 27. Stress and How to Deal（压力与应对）

Everyone feels stress sometimes. Too much stress is bad for our health. When I feel stressed, I take a deep breath and go for a walk. I also talk to my friends or write down my feelings. These things help me feel calm again.

每个人有时都会感到压力。压力过大会损害健康。当我感到压力时，我会深呼吸、去散步，也会和朋友聊聊或写下感受。这些能帮我重新平静下来。

**词与短语**：feel stress（感到压力）、take a deep breath（深呼吸）、feel calm（感到平静）

### 28. The Importance of Sleep（睡眠的重要性）

Sleep is very important for our body and mind. When we sleep, our body repairs itself and our brain stores memories. I try to sleep eight hours every night. Since I started sleeping well, I have felt more energetic and focused.

睡眠对我们的身心非常重要。睡觉时，身体自我修复，大脑储存记忆。我尽量每晚睡八小时。自从睡得好，我感觉更有精力、更专注了。

**词与短语**：repair itself（自我修复）、store memories（储存记忆）、energetic and focused（有精力又专注）

### 29. My Eating Habits（我的饮食习惯）

My eating habits have changed a lot. I used to eat a lot of snacks and fast food. Now I eat more vegetables and whole grains. I also drink less sugary drinks. Good eating habits make me feel lighter and healthier. Small changes bring big results.

我的饮食习惯改变了很多。以前我吃很多零食和快餐，现在吃更多蔬菜和全谷物，也少喝含糖饮料。好的饮食习惯让我感觉更轻松、更健康。小改变带来大结果。

**词与短语**：eating habits（饮食习惯）、whole grains（全谷物）、sugary drinks（含糖饮料）

### 30. Mental Health Matters（心理健康很重要）

For a long time, people talked little about mental health. But our mind needs care just like our body. When I feel down, I do not hide it. I talk to someone I trust. Asking for help is not weak; it is brave.

很长一段时间，人们很少谈论心理健康。但我们的心灵和身体一样需要呵护。当我情绪低落时，我不隐藏，而是和信任的人谈谈。求助不是软弱，而是勇敢。

**词与短语**：mental health（心理健康）、feel down（情绪低落）、asking for help（求助）

## 七、旅行与文化

### 31. A Memorable Trip（一次难忘的旅行）

Last year, I went to a famous city for the first time. I was amazed by the old buildings and busy streets. I tried local food and met friendly people. The trip opened my eyes to a new world. I cannot wait to travel again.

去年我第一次去了一座名城。古老的建筑和繁忙的街道让我惊叹。我品尝了当地美食，遇到了友好的人。这次旅行开阔了我的眼界。我迫不及待想再次旅行。

**词与短语**：be amazed by（被……惊叹）、local food（当地美食）、open my eyes（开阔眼界）

### 32. Different Cultures（不同的文化）

The world is full of different cultures. Each culture has its own food, clothes and customs. Learning about other cultures helps us understand people better. It also teaches us to respect differences. I find cultural differences very interesting.

世界充满了不同的文化。每种文化都有自己的饮食、服饰和习俗。了解其他文化帮助我们更好地理解他人，也教会我们尊重差异。我觉得文化差异非常有趣。

**词与短语**：be full of（充满）、its own（它自己的）、respect differences（尊重差异）

### 33. My Favorite City（我最喜欢的城市）

My favorite city is where I was born. It is not the biggest city, but it has a special place in my heart. The food there is delicious and the people are warm. Every time I go back, I feel at home. A city is more than its buildings.

我最喜欢的城市是我出生的地方。它不是最大的城市，但在我心中有特殊的位置。那里的食物美味，人们热情。每次回去，我都感到自在。一座城市不只是它的建筑。

**词与短语**：was born（出生）、a special place（特殊的位置）、feel at home（感到自在）

### 34. Travel Alone or with Friends（独自旅行还是结伴）

Some people love traveling alone, while others prefer traveling with friends. Traveling alone gives you freedom. Traveling with friends gives you company and shared memories. Both are good. It depends on what you want from the trip.

有人喜欢独自旅行，有人喜欢和朋友结伴。独自旅行给你自由，结伴旅行给你陪伴和共同的回忆。两者都好，取决于你想从旅行中得到什么。

**词与短语**：travel alone（独自旅行）、give you freedom（给你自由）、shared memories（共同的回忆）

### 35. Food Around the World（世界各地的美食）

Every country has its own special food. In China, we have noodles and dumplings. In Italy, there is pizza and pasta. In Japan, people love sushi. Trying different foods is like tasting different cultures. Food brings people together.

每个国家都有自己的特色美食。中国有面条和饺子，意大利有披萨和意面，日本人喜爱寿司。品尝不同的食物就像体验不同的文化。美食把人们聚在一起。

**词与短语**：special food（特色美食）、taste different cultures（体验不同文化）、bring together（聚在一起）

## 八、学习与成长

### 36. How I Learn English（我如何学英语）

English is my favorite subject, and I have my own ways to learn it. I watch English videos every day and repeat the sentences. I keep a notebook for new words. I am not afraid to make mistakes when I speak. Practice has helped me a lot.

英语是我最喜欢的科目，我有自己的学习方法。我每天看英语视频并跟读句子，用笔记本记新词，说英语时不怕犯错。练习帮了我很多。

**词与短语**：my own ways（我自己的方法）、keep a notebook（记笔记）、be afraid to（害怕做）

### 37. The Value of Reading（阅读的价值）

Reading is one of the best habits. Through books, I can travel to new worlds and learn from great minds. Reading also improves my writing and thinking. I try to read a little every day. A book is a friend that never leaves you.

阅读是最好的习惯之一。通过书，我可以去往新世界，向伟大的思想学习。阅读还提升我的写作和思考。我尽量每天读一点。书是永不离开你的朋友。

**词与短语**：one of the best habits（最好的习惯之一）、great minds（伟大的思想）、improve（提升）

### 38. Learning a New Skill（学习新技能）

Last year, I decided to learn cooking. At first, I burned the food many times. But I kept practicing and watched many videos. Now I can cook several dishes for my family. Learning a new skill takes time, but it is worth it.

去年我决定学做饭。起初我多次把食物烧糊，但我坚持练习、看了很多视频。现在我能给家人做几道菜了。学习新技能需要时间，但值得。

**词与短语**：decide to（决定做）、keep practicing（坚持练习）、be worth it（值得）

### 39. My Study Habits（我的学习习惯）

Good study habits make learning easier. I study at the same time every day. I turn off my phone so I can focus. I review my notes every week. These habits help me remember more and worry less before exams.

好的学习习惯让学习更轻松。我每天在同一时间学习，关掉手机以便专注，每周复习笔记。这些习惯帮我记得更多，考试前也不再那么焦虑。

**词与短语**：study habits（学习习惯）、turn off（关掉）、review notes（复习笔记）

### 40. Learning from Failure（从失败中学习）

Failure is not the end; it is a lesson. When I failed my first English speech, I felt terrible. But then I watched my video and found my problems. I practiced harder and won a prize the next year. Failure taught me more than success.

失败不是终点，而是一课。第一次英语演讲失败时，我感觉糟透了。但后来我看录像找到了问题，更加努力练习，第二年得了奖。失败教我的比成功还多。

**词与短语**：feel terrible（感觉很糟）、find problems（找到问题）、win a prize（得奖）

## 九、社会与生活

### 41. Life in My City（我所在城市的生活）

Life in my city is busy but colorful. In the morning, people rush to work and school. In the evening, families go out for walks. There are parks, shops and good restaurants. My city has its problems, but I love living here.

我所在城市的生活忙碌却多彩。早上人们赶着上班上学，晚上家人外出散步。这里有公园、商店和好餐厅。我的城市有它的问题，但我喜欢住在这里。

**词与短语**：busy but colorful（忙碌却多彩）、rush to（赶着去）、go out for walks（外出散步）

### 42. Technology in Our Lives（我们生活中的科技）

Technology is everywhere in our lives. We use phones to talk, learn and shop. It has made life easier, but it also has a downside. Some people spend too much time on screens. We should use technology, not be used by it.

科技在我们生活中无处不在。我们用手机交流、学习和购物。它让生活更便利，但也有弊端。有些人花太多时间在屏幕上。我们应该使用科技，而不是被科技使用。

**词与短语**：everywhere（无处不在）、make life easier（让生活更便利）、a downside（弊端）

### 43. The Environment（环境）

The environment is facing serious problems. Air pollution, plastic waste and climate change affect us all. But we can help in small ways. I use fewer plastic bags and recycle as much as I can. Protecting the earth is everyone's job.

环境正面临严重问题。空气污染、塑料垃圾和气候变化影响着我们所有人。但我们可以从小事做起。我少用塑料袋、尽量回收。保护地球是每个人的责任。

**词与短语**：face problems（面临问题）、climate change（气候变化）、recycle（回收）

### 44. Helping Others（帮助他人）

Helping others makes both sides happy. I join a volunteer group at school. We visit old people's homes and clean up parks. When I help someone, I feel useful and warm inside. Small acts of kindness can change the world.

帮助他人让双方都快乐。我参加了学校的志愿者小组，我们看望敬老院、打扫公园。帮助别人时，我感到自己有用、内心温暖。小小的善举可以改变世界。

**词与短语**：a volunteer group（志愿者小组）、feel useful（感到有用）、acts of kindness（善举）

### 45. The Importance of Hobbies（爱好的重要性）

Having a hobby is important for everyone. A hobby gives us a chance to relax and forget our worries. My hobby is photography. When I take photos, I notice the beauty around me. A good hobby makes life richer.

拥有爱好对每个人都很重要。爱好让我们有机会放松、忘记烦恼。我的爱好是摄影。拍照时，我注意到身边的美。好的爱好让生活更丰富。

**词与短语**：forget worries（忘记烦恼）、notice beauty（注意到美）、make life richer（让生活更丰富）

## 十、情感与反思

### 46. A Day I Will Never Forget（我永远不会忘记的一天）

The day I got my first prize was unforgettable. My hands were shaking when I heard my name. As I walked to the stage, I saw my parents smiling. I had worked so hard for that moment. I will treasure it forever.

我第一次得奖的那天难以忘怀。听到自己的名字时，我的手在发抖。走向舞台时，我看到父母在微笑。我为那一刻付出了很多努力。我会永远珍藏它。

**词与短语**：unforgettable（难忘的）、walk to the stage（走向舞台）、treasure it forever（永远珍藏）

### 47. Things I Regret（我后悔的事）

I have a few regrets in life. One is that I did not study hard enough in my first year of school. I wasted a lot of time. Now I know that time is precious. The good news is that it is never too late to change.

人生中我有一些后悔的事。其中一件是上学第一年没有足够努力，浪费了很多时间。现在我知道时间宝贵。好消息是，改变永远不晚。

**词与短语**：a few regrets（一些遗憾）、waste time（浪费时间）、never too late（永远不晚）

### 48. Being Grateful（心怀感恩）

I try to be grateful every day. I am grateful for my health, my family and my friends. Even on bad days, there is always something to thank for. Gratitude turns what we have into enough. A thankful heart is a happy heart.

我努力每天心怀感恩。我感恩健康、家人和朋友。即使在糟糕的日子里，也总有值得感谢的事。感恩让我们拥有的变得足够。感恩的心就是快乐的心。

**词与短语**：be grateful（感恩）、something to thank for（值得感谢的事）、a happy heart（快乐的心）

### 49. My Fears and Hopes（我的恐惧与希望）

Everyone has fears and hopes. I am afraid of failing and letting people down. But I also have hopes. I hope to grow, to learn and to make a difference. Hopes give me courage to face my fears. They keep me going.

每个人都有恐惧和希望。我害怕失败、害怕让人失望。但我也有希望——希望成长、学习和有所作为。希望给我面对恐惧的勇气，让我继续前行。

**词与短语**：let people down（让人失望）、make a difference（有所作为）、keep me going（让我继续前行）

### 50. A Letter to Myself（写给自己的一封信）

Dear future me, I hope you are happy. Remember how hard you worked and how far you have come. Do not forget to rest, to laugh and to love. Keep being kind and brave. No matter what happens, I am proud of you. Yours, me.

亲爱的未来的我：希望你幸福。记住你曾经多么努力、走了多远。别忘了休息、欢笑和去爱。继续保持善良和勇敢。无论发生什么，我都为你骄傲。此致，现在的我。

**词与短语**：how far you have come（你走了多远）、be proud of（为……骄傲）、no matter what（无论发生什么）
