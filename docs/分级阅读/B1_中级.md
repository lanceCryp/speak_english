# B1 分级阅读 · 中级

> 对应 CEFR B1（约 2,000–3,000 词）。短文开始使用现在完成时、条件句、关系从句等中级语法，适合作为独立成段表达的背诵范本。

## 1. An Unforgettable Experience（一次难忘的经历）

**英文正文**

Two years ago, I took part in a school trip to the mountains. It was the first time I had ever been so far from home. We climbed for five hours before we finally reached the top. I was very tired, but the view was amazing. Since that day, I have loved hiking. It taught me that hard work always pays off in the end.

**中文译文**

两年前，我参加了一次学校组织的登山旅行。那是我第一次离家那么远。我们爬了五个小时才终于到达山顶。我非常累，但景色美极了。从那以后，我就爱上了徒步。它教会我：努力最终总会有回报。

**重点词汇与短语**

- take part in（参加）
- the first time I had ever...（我第一次……）
- reach the top（到达山顶）
- since that day（从那天起）
- pay off（得到回报）

## 2. My Dream（我的梦想）

**英文正文**

Everyone has a dream, and so do I. I have wanted to be a teacher since I was a child. In my opinion, teaching is a meaningful job because teachers help students become better people. To achieve my dream, I am studying hard and reading many books. I believe that if I keep trying, my dream will come true one day.

**中文译文**

每个人都有一个梦想，我也是。我从小就梦想成为一名老师。在我看来，教书是一份有意义的工作，因为老师帮助学生成为更好的人。为了实现梦想，我正在努力学习、大量阅读。我相信只要坚持，我的梦想总有一天会实现。

**重点词汇与短语**

- so do I（我也是）
- since I was a child（从我小时候起）
- in my opinion（在我看来）
- a meaningful job（一份有意义的工作）
- come true（实现）

## 3. The Importance of Hobbies（爱好的重要性）

**英文正文**

Having a hobby is important for everyone, especially for people who are busy with work or study. A hobby gives us a chance to relax and forget our worries for a while. For example, my father, who is a very busy man, enjoys fishing on weekends. He says it helps him feel calm. I think everyone should find something they truly enjoy doing.

**中文译文**

拥有一个爱好对每个人都很重要，尤其是对那些忙于工作或学习的人。爱好让我们有机会放松，暂时忘记烦恼。例如，我爸爸是个大忙人，但他周末喜欢钓鱼。他说这能让他感到平静。我认为每个人都应该找到自己真正喜欢做的事。

**重点词汇与短语**

- be busy with（忙于……）
- forget our worries（忘记烦恼）
- for a while（暂时；一会儿）
- enjoy doing（喜欢做……）
- feel calm（感到平静）

## 4. A Difficult Decision（一个艰难的决定）

**英文正文**

Last year, I had to make a difficult decision. I received two offers: one from a school in my hometown, and one from a school in another city. If I stayed home, I would be close to my family. However, going away would give me more chances to grow. After thinking for a long time, I chose to leave. It was hard, but I have never regretted my choice.

**中文译文**

去年，我不得不做一个艰难的决定。我收到了两个录取通知：一个来自我家乡的学校，一个来自另一个城市的学校。如果我留在家里，就能离家人很近。然而，离开会给我更多成长的机会。考虑了很长时间后，我选择了离开。这很难，但我从未后悔自己的选择。

**重点词汇与短语**

- make a decision（做决定）
- receive an offer（收到录取/邀请）
- in my hometown（在我的家乡）
- give me more chances（给我更多机会）
- regret my choice（后悔我的选择）

## 5. Travel Changes You（旅行改变你）

**英文正文**

People often say that travel is the best way to learn. I used to doubt this, but now I agree completely. When you travel, you meet people from different cultures and try new foods. You also learn to solve problems by yourself. Travel has changed me a lot. It has made me more open-minded and more independent than before.

**中文译文**

人们常说，旅行是最好的学习方式。我以前对此表示怀疑，但现在完全同意。旅行时，你会遇到来自不同文化的人，尝试新的食物，也学会自己解决问题。旅行极大地改变了我，它让我比以前更开明、更独立。

**重点词汇与短语**

- the best way to learn（最好的学习方式）
- used to doubt（过去怀疑）
- different cultures（不同的文化）
- solve problems（解决问题）
- open-minded（思想开明的）
