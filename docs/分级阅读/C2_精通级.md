> 对应 CEFR C2（约 16,000 词以上）。50 篇文章接近母语者的思辨与修辞水准，适合高阶学习者精读背诵，体会语言的细微差别与文采。

## 一、哲学沉思

### 1. The Nature of Time（时间的本质）

Time is the most familiar thing in our lives, yet the least understood. We speak of saving it, spending it, wasting it, as though it were a currency. But time is not something we possess; it is the medium in which we exist. The past is memory, the future is expectation, and the present—so elusive—is all we ever truly have. Perhaps that is why the wise have always urged us to attend to the present moment. We cannot add a single hour to our lives, but we can deepen the hours we are given.

时间是我们生命中最熟悉、却又最不被理解的事物。我们说节省时间、花费时间、浪费时间，仿佛它是某种货币。但时间不是我们拥有的东西，而是我们存在的介质。过去是记忆，未来是期待，而当下——如此难以捉摸——才是我们真正拥有的一切。或许正因如此，智者总是敦促我们专注当下。我们无法为生命增加哪怕一个小时，却能加深我们所拥有的每一刻。

**词与短语**：a currency（一种货币）、the medium in which we exist（我们存在的介质）、deepen the hours（加深每一刻）

### 2. On Certainty（论确定性）

We crave certainty as a thirsty man craves water. Uncertainty is uncomfortable, even frightening, and so we cling to beliefs that promise solid ground. Yet the more we learn, the more we see how much remains unknown. The greatest minds have often been the most willing to say "I do not know." There is a deep difference between the certainty of conviction and the certainty of evidence. Wisdom lies not in escaping doubt but in learning to live with it. To be certain is comfortable; to be open is to grow.

我们渴求确定性，如口渴之人渴求清水。不确定令人不适，甚至恐惧，于是我们紧抓那些承诺坚实根基的信念。然而我们学得越多，就越看清未知之广。最伟大的头脑往往最愿意说"我不知道"。信念的确定与证据的确定之间有着深刻的区别。智慧不在于逃避怀疑，而在于学会与之共存。确定令人安逸，开放则带来成长。

**词与短语**：cling to beliefs（紧抓信念）、solid ground（坚实的根基）、live with doubt（与怀疑共存）

### 3. The Self（自我）

What is this "self" we speak of so confidently? Is it a fixed essence, or a story we continually revise? We are not the same person at fifty that we were at fifteen, yet we feel a thread of continuity running through our lives. Memory knits our scattered moments into a single narrative. In a sense, we are the authors of ourselves, writing and rewriting the story of who we are. The self is less a thing we have than a work we make.

我们如此自信地谈论的"自我"究竟是什么？它是一个固定的本质，还是一个我们不断修订的故事？五十岁的我们与十五岁时已非同一人，却能感到一条连续的线贯穿生命。记忆把我们散落的时刻编织成单一的叙事。某种意义上，我们是自己的作者，书写并重写着"我是谁"的故事。自我与其说是一个我们拥有的东西，不如说是一件我们创作的作品。

**词与短语**：a fixed essence（固定的本质）、a thread of continuity（连续的线）、the authors of ourselves（自己的作者）

### 4. Fate and Free Will（命运与自由意志）

Few questions have troubled humanity as deeply as the relationship between fate and free will. Are our lives a script written in advance, or a blank page on which we write? Perhaps the truth lies between: we are dealt a hand, but we choose how to play it. Circumstances limit us, yet within those limits we exercise real choice. To deny our freedom is to deny our responsibility; to ignore our limits is to court frustration. The wise accept what they cannot change and change what they can.

很少有哪个问题能像命运与自由意志的关系那样深深困扰人类。我们的人生是预先写好的剧本，还是一张任由我们书写的白纸？也许真相介于两者之间：我们被分到一手牌，却可以选择如何打。环境限制着我们，但在那些限制之内，我们行使着真实的选择。否认自由，就是否认责任；忽视局限，则是自寻烦恼。智者接受无法改变的，改变能够改变的。

**词与短语**：be dealt a hand（被分到一手牌）、within those limits（在那些限制之内）、court frustration（自寻烦恼）

### 5. On Wisdom（论智慧）

Wisdom is not the same as knowledge. Knowledge is the accumulation of facts; wisdom is the judgment to use them well. A person may know a great deal and yet act foolishly. Wisdom is slow to speak and quick to listen. It knows that certainty is rare and that humility is strength. It is often found not in the young and clever but in those who have lived, loved and lost. Knowledge asks "What?" Wisdom asks "What matters?" The two together make a complete mind.

智慧不同于知识。知识是事实的积累，智慧则是善用它们的判断力。一个人可能博学却行事愚蠢。智慧迟于言、敏于听。它知道确定难得，谦逊是力量。智慧往往不在年轻聪明者身上，而在那些经历过、爱过、失去过的人身上。知识问"是什么"，智慧问"什么重要"。二者合一，方为完整的心灵。

**词与短语**：the accumulation of facts（事实的积累）、slow to speak（迟于言）、a complete mind（完整的心灵）

## 二、文学与美

### 6. The Power of Story（故事的力量）

Human beings are storytelling creatures. Before we wrote, we told tales around fires; today we tell them through books and screens. Stories do more than entertain—they teach us how to live. They let us rehearse courage, grief and love in safety. A single story can shape a culture or change a single heart. We understand ourselves through the stories we tell about our own lives. To be human is, in part, to be a teller of tales.

人类是天生的讲故事者。在有文字之前，我们围火讲述传说；今天，我们通过书籍和屏幕讲述。故事不只是娱乐，它们教会我们如何生活，让我们在安全中预演勇气、悲伤与爱。一个故事可以塑造一种文化，也可以改变一颗心。我们通过讲述自己人生的故事来理解自己。作为人，在某种程度上就是成为讲故事的人。

**词与短语**：storytelling creatures（讲故事的生灵）、rehearse courage（预演勇气）、a teller of tales（讲故事的人）

### 7. On Beauty（论美）

Beauty has puzzled philosophers for centuries. Is it in the object, or in the eye of the beholder? Perhaps it lives in the meeting of the two. Beauty stops us, silences us, and reminds us that life holds more than utility. It cannot be owned or hoarded, yet it enriches everyone who pauses to see it. In a world obsessed with efficiency, beauty is a quiet rebellion. To notice beauty is to resist the flattening of life into mere function.

美困惑了哲学家几个世纪。它是在客体之中，还是在观者眼中？也许它存在于二者的相遇之中。美让我们驻足、噤声，提醒我们生命不止于功用。它无法被占有或囤积，却让每一个停下欣赏的人变得富足。在一个痴迷于效率的世界里，美是一种安静的反叛。留意美，就是抵抗把生命扁平化为纯粹功能。

**词与短语**：the eye of the beholder（观者眼中）、a quiet rebellion（安静的反叛）、the flattening of life（生命的扁平化）

### 8. The Tragic Sense（悲剧感）

The tragic sense is the awareness that loss and sorrow are woven into life. It is not pessimism but honesty. The Greeks knew that tragedy enlarges the soul—that in witnessing great suffering nobly borne, we learn something essential about being human. Tragedy reminds us that love and loss are inseparable, that joy is sharpened by the knowledge of its fragility. To feel the tragic is not to be defeated by it, but to be deepened by it.

悲剧感是对"失去与悲伤交织于生命"的觉知。它不是悲观，而是诚实。希腊人懂得悲剧能拓展灵魂——目睹伟大的苦难被高贵地承受，我们学到了关于为人的本质一课。悲剧提醒我们，爱与失去不可分割，喜悦因其脆弱可知而更加鲜明。感受悲剧，不是被它击败，而是被它加深。

**词与短语**：be woven into（交织于）、enlarge the soul（拓展灵魂）、be deepened by it（被它加深）

### 9. Memory and Identity（记忆与身份）

We are, in a profound sense, what we remember. Strip away memory and identity unravels. Yet memory is not a faithful recording; it is a creative act. We select, shape and even invent the past as we recall it. This is not a flaw but a feature—memory serves meaning as much as accuracy. We remember what matters, and in remembering, we decide what matters. The stories we tell ourselves about the past become the ground on which we stand.

从深刻的意义上说，我们就是我们所记住的一切。剥离记忆，身份便瓦解。然而记忆并非忠实的记录，而是一种创造性的行为。回忆时，我们选择、塑造甚至创造过去。这不是缺陷而是特性——记忆服务于意义，也服务于准确。我们记住重要的东西，而在记住的过程中，我们决定了什么重要。我们对自己讲述的关于过去的故事，成了我们立足之地。

**词与短语**：unravel（瓦解）、a faithful recording（忠实的记录）、serve meaning（服务于意义）

### 10. The Poetic Eye（诗意的眼光）

The poet sees what others overlook. Where the hurried eye sees only a crowded street, the poetic eye finds a thousand small dramas. Poetry is not a special subject but a special way of seeing. It trains us to notice the particular, the fleeting, the quietly beautiful. In an age of distraction, the poetic eye is a form of resistance and a form of gratitude. It reminds us that wonder is not a rare gift but a daily possibility. The world is full of poetry for those with eyes to see it.

诗人看见别人忽略的东西。匆忙的眼睛只看到拥挤的街道，诗意的眼睛却发现一千个小戏剧。诗歌不是特殊的题材，而是特殊的观看方式。它训练我们留意那些独特、短暂、静默之美。在注意力涣散的时代，诗意的眼光是一种抵抗，也是一种感恩。它提醒我们，惊奇不是稀有的天赋，而是日常的可能。对有眼去看的人而言，世界充满诗意。

**词与短语**：a special way of seeing（特殊的观看方式）、the quietly beautiful（静默之美）、with eyes to see（有眼去看）

## 三、人性

### 11. On Courage（论勇气）

Courage is not the absence of fear but the mastery of it. The brave are not those who feel no fear, but those who act in spite of it. Courage takes many quiet forms: speaking the truth when it costs, admitting a mistake, facing another day when life is hard. It is rarely dramatic; more often it is daily and unglamorous. We are all called to courage at some point, usually when we least expect it. And in meeting that call, we discover a strength we did not know we had.

勇气不是没有恐惧，而是驾驭恐惧。勇敢者不是感受不到恐惧的人，而是不顾恐惧仍去行动的人。勇气有许多安静的形式：在代价高昂时说真话，承认错误，在生活艰难时面对新的一天。它很少轰轰烈烈，更多是日常的、不起眼的。我们都在某个时刻被召唤去勇敢，通常是在最意想不到的时候。而在回应那召唤时，我们发现了一种自己都不知道拥有的力量。

**词与短语**：the mastery of it（对它的驾驭）、in spite of it（不顾它）、meet that call（回应那召唤）

### 12. On Kindness（论善良）

Kindness is often mistaken for weakness, yet it is one of the strongest forces we possess. It costs little but can change everything for the one who receives it. The kind person is not naive; they have simply chosen generosity over cynicism. Kindness is contagious, spreading from person to person in ways we rarely see. It is the quiet glue that holds communities together. In a harsh world, kindness is not softness but strength, deliberately chosen.

善良常被误认为软弱，然而它是我们所拥有的最强大的力量之一。它代价微小，却能改变接受者的一切。善良的人并非天真，他们只是选择了慷慨而非犬儒。善良具有传染性，以我们很少看见的方式在人与人之间传播。它是把社区凝聚在一起的静默胶水。在严酷的世界里，善良不是软弱，而是刻意选择的力量。

**词与短语**：be mistaken for（被误认为）、generosity over cynicism（慷慨而非犬儒）、the quiet glue（静默的胶水）

### 13. On Envy（论嫉妒）

Envy is among the most corrosive of emotions. It poisons the one who feels it far more than the one it targets. Envy whispers that another's gain is our loss, that their success diminishes us. But this is a lie. Another's light does not dim our own. The antidote to envy is not achievement but gratitude—learning to want what we have rather than what others have. The person free of envy is rich indeed, whatever they own.

嫉妒是最具腐蚀性的情绪之一。它毒害感受它的人，远甚于它所针对的人。嫉妒低语：别人的所得是我们的损失，他们的成功贬损了我们。但这是谎言。他人的光芒不会使我们的暗淡。嫉妒的解药不是成就，而是感恩——学会想要我们拥有的，而非他人拥有的。摆脱嫉妒的人，无论拥有什么，都是真正富足的。

**词与短语**：the most corrosive（最具腐蚀性的）、diminish us（贬损我们）、the antidote（解药）

### 14. On Forgiveness（论宽恕）

Forgiveness is often misunderstood as excusing wrong. In truth, it is the opposite: it names the wrong clearly and then releases its hold on us. To forgive is not to forget but to refuse to let the past rule the present. It is among the hardest of human acts, and among the most liberating. The one who forgives is freed as much as the one forgiven. Resentment is a poison we drink hoping another will die. Forgiveness is the antidote.

宽恕常被误解为原谅过错。事实上恰恰相反：它清楚地指出过错，然后松开过错对我们的束缚。宽恕不是忘记，而是拒绝让过去统治现在。它是最艰难的人类行为之一，也是最解放的行为之一。宽恕者与被宽恕者一样获得解脱。怨恨是我们喝下的毒药，却盼着别人死去。宽恕才是解药。

**词与短语**：release its hold（松开束缚）、rule the present（统治现在）、drink poison hoping another will die（喝毒药盼别人死）

### 15. On Gratitude（论感恩）

Gratitude is more than a pleasant feeling; it is a discipline. It is the practice of turning attention to what is good, rather than what is lacking. This is not to deny hardship but to refuse to let it be the whole story. The grateful person notices gifts that the complaining person overlooks. Gratitude transforms what we have into enough. It is, perhaps, the surest path to contentment—a quiet revolution of the heart.

感恩不止是愉悦的感觉，它是一门修炼。它练习把注意力转向好的方面，而非缺失的方面。这不是否认困苦，而是拒绝让它成为全部。感恩的人留意到抱怨者忽略的馈赠。感恩把我们拥有的转化为足够。它或许是最可靠的通往满足之路——一场心灵的静默革命。

**词与短语**：a discipline（一门修炼）、the whole story（全部）、the surest path（最可靠的途径）

## 四、社会与文明

### 16. The Price of Progress（进步的代价）

Progress is rarely an unmixed good. Every advance brings losses as well as gains. The machine that eases our labor also disrupts the craft it replaced. The city that offers opportunity also breeds loneliness. This is not an argument against progress, but a call to see it whole. We should embrace change with open eyes, weighing its costs and honoring what is left behind. The best progress is that which carries the best of the past forward with it. Blind optimism is as foolish as blind nostalgia.

进步很少是纯粹的好事。每一次进步都既带来收获也带来损失。减轻我们劳作的机器，也扰乱了它所取代的手艺。提供机会的城市，也滋生了孤独。这不是反对进步的论调，而是呼吁全面地看待它。我们应该睁大眼睛拥抱变化，权衡其代价，珍视被留下的东西。最好的进步，是把过去最美好的部分一并带向未来。盲目的乐观与盲目的怀旧同样愚蠢。

**词与短语**：an unmixed good（纯粹的好事）、breed loneliness（滋生孤独）、see it whole（全面地看待）

### 17. On Community（论共同体）

We are social beings, and yet modern life often leaves us feeling more connected and more alone at once. A true community is more than a collection of individuals; it is a web of mutual care. In community, we are known, needed and held accountable. Such belonging is a deep human need, older than any app. Building community requires time, presence and the willingness to be inconvenienced by others. What we gain in return is worth far more than what we give.

我们是社会性的存在，然而现代生活常让我们同时感到更连接、也更孤独。真正的共同体不只是个体的集合，而是一张相互关怀的网。在共同体中，我们被认识、被需要、被要求负责。这种归属感是一种深刻的人类需求，比任何应用都古老。建设共同体需要时间、在场，以及愿意被他人打扰的胸怀。我们所得的回报，远胜于我们付出的。

**词与短语**：a web of mutual care（相互关怀的网）、be held accountable（被要求负责）、be inconvenienced（被打扰）

### 18. On Work（论工作）

Work occupies the greater part of our waking lives, yet we seldom reflect on what it means. Is work a curse to be endured or a calling to be fulfilled? The answer shapes the texture of our days. Good work engages our abilities and connects us to something larger than ourselves. But even necessary work, done with care, has its dignity. The goal is not to escape work but to find work that enlarges rather than diminishes us. In the end, how we work reveals who we are.

工作占据了我们清醒时光的大部分，我们却很少反思它的意义。工作是必须忍受的诅咒，还是值得实现的召唤？答案塑造了我们日子的质地。好的工作调动我们的能力，把我们与比自身更大的事物相连。但即便是必要的工作，用心去做也有其尊严。目标不是逃避工作，而是找到那种使我们丰盈而非萎缩的工作。归根结底，我们如何工作，揭示了我们是谁。

**词与短语**：the texture of our days（日子的质地）、a calling to be fulfilled（值得实现的召唤）、has its dignity（有其尊严）

### 19. On Leisure（论闲暇）

Leisure is not the same as idleness. True leisure is not the absence of activity but the freedom to do what restores us. It is the space in which we read, think, create and simply be. A culture that prizes only productivity forgets that great ideas often arrive in moments of rest. We fill our spare time with noise because silence frightens us. Yet it is in stillness that the mind renews itself. The quality of our leisure shapes the quality of our lives.

闲暇不同于懒散。真正的闲暇不是没有活动，而是有自由去做那些让我们恢复的事。它是我们阅读、思考、创造、单纯存在的空间。一个只崇尚生产力的文化忘记了：伟大的想法往往在休息的时刻降临。我们用噪音填满空闲，因为沉默让我们害怕。然而正是在静默中，心灵得以更新。我们闲暇的质量，塑造着我们生活的质量。

**词与短语**：not the absence of activity（不是没有活动）、prize productivity（崇尚生产力）、renew itself（自我更新）

### 20. On Tradition（论传统）

Tradition is often dismissed as the enemy of progress, but this is a shallow reading. Traditions are the accumulated wisdom of generations, tested by time. They give us roots and a sense of continuity. Yet tradition can also harden into blind conformity. The task of each generation is to sift tradition—to keep what nourishes and release what binds. A living tradition is not a cage but a foundation. We stand on the shoulders of the past to see the future more clearly.

传统常被斥为进步的敌人，但这是浅薄的解读。传统是代代积累、经时间检验的智慧。它给我们根基和连续感。然而传统也可能僵化为盲目的因循。每一代人的任务是筛选传统——保留滋养我们的，放下束缚我们的。鲜活的传统不是牢笼，而是地基。我们站在过去的肩上，才能更清楚地看见未来。

**词与短语**：be dismissed as（被斥为）、the accumulated wisdom（积累的智慧）、a foundation, not a cage（地基而非牢笼）

## 五、语言与表达

### 21. The Limits of Language（语言的局限）

Language is our most powerful tool, yet it has edges. There are experiences—grief, awe, love—that words can approach but never fully capture. The deepest things are often said in silence or in gesture. Poets spend their lives pushing against the limits of language, trying to say the unsayable. This is not a defect but a mystery: that we can gesture toward more than we can say. Perhaps the truest things are those we can only point at, never quite hold.

语言是我们最有力的工具，但它有边界。有些体验——悲伤、敬畏、爱——词语可以接近却永远无法完全捕捉。最深的感受常常以沉默或姿态表达。诗人穷尽一生冲击语言的边界，试图言说不可言说之物。这不是缺陷而是奥秘：我们能够指向超出言说的东西。也许最真实的事物，是我们只能指认、却永远无法真正握住的东西。

**词与短语**：approach but never capture（接近却无法捕捉）、push against the limits（冲击边界）、the unsayable（不可言说之物）

### 22. On Silence（论沉默）

We live in a world that fears silence, filling every pause with sound. Yet silence is not emptiness; it is a presence of its own. In silence, we hear what noise drowns out—our own thoughts, the needs of others, the still small voice within. Silence is the soil in which reflection grows. It can comfort the grieving and humble the proud. To be comfortable with silence is a rare and valuable skill. Sometimes the most eloquent response is no response at all.

我们生活在一个害怕沉默的世界，用声音填满每一个停顿。然而沉默不是空洞，它自有其在场。在沉默中，我们听见噪音淹没的东西——我们自己的思绪、他人的需要、内心那微小的声音。沉默是反思生长的土壤。它能安慰哀伤者，使骄傲者谦卑。安于沉默是一项稀有而宝贵的技能。有时，最雄辩的回应，是不作回应。

**词与短语**：a presence of its own（自有其在场）、drown out（淹没）、the most eloquent response（最雄辩的回应）

### 23. On Rhetoric（论修辞）

Rhetoric has a bad name, associated with manipulation and empty words. But at its best, rhetoric is the art of persuasion in service of truth. It is the craft of making important things heard. The ability to speak well is a form of power, and like all power it can be used or abused. That is why we must learn not only to persuade but to be persuaded, and to tell the difference. Rhetoric, rightly understood, is not the enemy of truth but its instrument. Words can build or destroy; the choice is ours.

修辞名声不佳，常与操纵和空话联系在一起。但在其最佳状态，修辞是为真理服务的说服艺术，是让重要事物被听见的技艺。善于言说是一种力量，像所有力量一样，它可以被善用，也可以被滥用。正因如此，我们不仅要学会说服，也要学会被说服，并学会分辨二者。正确理解的修辞不是真理的敌人，而是它的工具。言语可以建设，也可以摧毁，选择在我们。

**词与短语**：have a bad name（名声不佳）、in service of truth（为真理服务）、an instrument of truth（真理的工具）

### 24. On Translation（论翻译）

Translation is often described as a loss, and there is truth in that—something always slips through. But it is also a gain: a bridge between worlds that would otherwise never meet. The translator must be both faithful and creative, rendering not just words but meaning and music. Every translation is an interpretation, a conversation across cultures. In a divided world, translation is an act of peace. To translate is to say, in effect, "Your world matters enough for me to enter it."

翻译常被描述为一种损失，这有一定道理——总有东西会流失。但它也是一种收获：一座连接彼此原本永不相遇之世界的桥梁。译者必须既忠实又富创造力，传达的不只是词句，更是意义与韵律。每一次翻译都是一种诠释，一场跨越文化的对话。在分裂的世界里，翻译是一种和平的行为。翻译，实际上是在说："你的世界值得我走进。"

**词与短语**：slip through（流失）、a bridge between worlds（连接世界的桥梁）、an act of peace（和平的行为）

### 25. The Written Word（书面文字）

The written word is among humanity's greatest inventions. It lets the thoughts of the dead speak to the living, and the living speak to those not yet born. Writing fixes fleeting ideas so they can be examined, shared and improved. It is a technology of memory and a medium of civilization. In reading, we hold a conversation with minds we will never meet. To write clearly is to think clearly. The pen, in skilled hands, outlasts the sword.

书面文字是人类最伟大的发明之一。它让逝者的思想与生者对话，让生者与尚未出生的人对话。写作把稍纵即逝的想法固定下来，使其得以被审视、分享和改进。它是记忆的技术、文明的媒介。阅读时，我们与从未谋面的心灵对话。写得清晰，就是思考得清晰。在熟练的手中，笔比剑更持久。

**词与短语**：among humanity's greatest inventions（人类最伟大的发明之一）、a technology of memory（记忆的技术）、outlast the sword（比剑更持久）

## 六、时间与记忆

### 26. On Nostalgia（论怀旧）

Nostalgia was once considered a sickness, but we now know it can be a comfort. It is the bittersweet ache for times we cannot return to. Nostalgia is not about the past as it was, but as we wish it had been—a golden haze over ordinary days. In this, it can deceive us. Yet it also reminds us of what we value, and points toward what we miss. The danger is not in remembering fondly, but in believing the past was better than the present. Gratitude for the past should not become escape from the now.

怀旧曾被视作一种病，但我们现在知道它可以是一种慰藉。它是对我们无法返回的时光的苦涩而甜蜜的眷恋。怀旧不是关于过去本来的样子，而是关于我们希望它曾是的样子——一层笼罩在平凡日子上的金色薄雾。就此而言，它可能欺骗我们。但它也提醒我们所珍视之物，指向我们所怀念之事。危险不在于深情回忆，而在于相信过去比现在更好。对过去的感恩，不应变成对当下的逃避。

**词与短语**：a bittersweet ache（苦涩而甜蜜的眷恋）、a golden haze（金色薄雾）、escape from the now（逃避当下）

### 27. The Present Moment（当下时刻）

We spend so much of our lives everywhere but here. The mind wanders to the past with regret and to the future with worry, while the present slips by unnoticed. Yet the present is the only place where life actually happens. To be present is to give the current moment our full attention, as if it mattered—because it does. This is not easy; it is a practice, like any art. But those who master it discover that the present is not empty but full. Here, now, is where we live.

我们生命中的太多时间都不在此处。思绪带着遗憾飘向过去，带着忧虑飘向未来，而当下悄然溜走。然而当下是生命真正发生的唯一地方。在场，就是给予此刻以全部注意，仿佛它很重要——因为它确实重要。这并不容易，它像任何艺术一样需要练习。但掌握它的人会发现，当下不是空虚，而是充盈。此处，此刻，才是我们生活的地方。

**词与短语**：slip by unnoticed（悄然溜走）、give full attention（给予全部注意）、not empty but full（不是空虚而是充盈）

### 28. On Regret（论遗憾）

Regret is the shadow of roads not taken. It is painful, but it is also instructive—a sign that we have learned to imagine better choices. The point of regret is not to torment us but to teach us. Dwelling endlessly on the past changes nothing; learning from it changes everything. The mature response to regret is not to erase it but to let it guide us. We cannot rewrite the past, but we can write a wiser future. Regret, well used, is a teacher in disguise.

遗憾是未走之路投下的阴影。它痛苦，却也有教益——它表明我们已学会想象更好的选择。遗憾的意义不是折磨我们，而是教导我们。无休止地沉湎于过去改变不了什么，从中学习却可以改变一切。对遗憾的成熟回应，不是抹去它，而是让它指引我们。我们无法重写过去，却可以书写一个更明智的未来。善加利用的遗憾，是乔装打扮的老师。

**词与短语**：roads not taken（未走之路）、dwell on（沉湎于）、a teacher in disguise（乔装的老师）

### 29. On Hope（论希望）

Hope is not naive optimism; it is the conviction that the future can be better and that our actions matter. Without hope, effort seems pointless, and despair sets in. Yet hope must be earned, not assumed. It grows when we act, even in small ways, toward the good we wish to see. Hope is not the belief that things will turn out well, but the belief that they are worth working for. It is the engine of every human advance. Even in the darkest hour, hope keeps a small light burning.

希望不是天真的乐观，而是"未来可以更好、我们的行动有意义"的信念。没有希望，努力似乎毫无意义，绝望便随之而来。然而希望必须靠争取，而非想当然。当我们哪怕以微小的方式朝着所愿的美好行动时，希望便生长。希望不是相信事情会有好结果，而是相信它们值得为之努力。它是人类每一次进步的引擎。即使在最黑暗的时刻，希望也让一盏小灯继续燃烧。

**词与短语**：naive optimism（天真的乐观）、be earned, not assumed（靠争取而非想当然）、keep a light burning（让灯继续燃烧）

### 30. The Seasons of Life（人生的季节）

Life has its seasons, as surely as the year. There is the spring of youth, the summer of strength, the autumn of harvest, the winter of reflection. Each season has its own beauty and its own tasks. We err when we try to live all seasons at once, or when we mourn a season that has passed. The art of living is to embrace the season we are in, fully and without regret. Winter, too, has its quiet gifts for those who receive them.

人生自有其季节，正如一年四季那样确定。有青春的春天、力量的夏天、收获的秋天、反思的冬天。每个季节都有自己的美和自己的功课。当我们试图同时经历所有季节，或哀悼一个已逝的季节时，我们便犯了错。生活的艺术在于全然地拥抱我们所处的季节，毫无遗憾。冬天，对那些愿意领受的人，也有它安静的馈赠。

**词与短语**：as surely as the year（正如一年四季那样确定）、the season we are in（我们所处的季节）、quiet gifts（安静的馈赠）

## 七、伦理与美德

### 31. On Integrity（论正直）

Integrity is the quality of being whole—of having our actions match our words and our private life match our public face. It is easier to perform virtue than to possess it. The person of integrity does the right thing even when no one is watching, because they answer to a standard beyond applause. Integrity is not built in grand moments but in a thousand small, unobserved choices. It is the quiet consistency of a life lived in truth. Trust, once broken, is hard to rebuild; integrity is what makes trust possible.

正直是"完整"的品质——让行动与言语一致，让私生活与公众形象一致。表演美德比拥有美德容易。正直的人即使在无人观看时也做正确的事，因为他们服从于超越掌声的标准。正直不是在大场面中建立的，而是在一千个微小、无人察觉的选择中建立的。它是真诚生活带来的安静的始终如一。信任一旦破裂就难以重建，而正直正是让信任成为可能的东西。

**词与短语**：perform virtue（表演美德）、a standard beyond applause（超越掌声的标准）、quiet consistency（安静的始终如一）

### 32. On Justice（论正义）

Justice is the foundation on which any decent society stands. It means that the rules apply equally, that the powerful cannot bully the weak, that every person's dignity is recognized. Yet justice is not only a matter of law; it is a matter of the heart. We feel its absence keenly—the sting of unfairness is one of the first moral emotions children know. To build a just society is the work of a lifetime and of many lifetimes. Justice delayed is justice denied, and justice for some is not justice at all.

正义是任何体面社会赖以立足的根基。它意味着规则平等适用，强者不能欺凌弱者，每个人的尊严都得到承认。然而正义不仅是法律之事，也是心灵之事。我们对它的缺失感受敏锐——不公平带来的刺痛，是儿童最早认识的道德情感之一。建设公正的社会，是一生乃至许多代人的事业。迟到的正义不是正义，只属于部分人的正义根本不是正义。

**词与短语**：the foundation of society（社会的根基）、the sting of unfairness（不公平的刺痛）、justice delayed（迟到的正义）

### 33. On Humility（论谦逊）

Humility is not thinking less of yourself; it is thinking of yourself less. It is the recognition that we are all fallible, that our knowledge is partial and our time is short. The humble person is teachable, able to admit error and learn from anyone. This is not weakness but a profound strength, for it keeps the mind open. Arrogance closes doors; humility opens them. The greatest minds are often the most humble, for they have seen how much they do not know. Humility is the soil in which wisdom grows.

谦逊不是看轻自己，而是少想自己。它是认识到我们都会犯错、我们的知识是片面的、我们的时间短暂。谦逊的人可教，能够承认错误、向任何人学习。这不是软弱，而是一种深沉的力量，因为它让心灵保持开放。傲慢关闭大门，谦逊打开大门。最伟大的头脑往往最谦逊，因为他们已看清自己有多少未知。谦逊是智慧生长的土壤。

**词与短语**：think of yourself less（少想自己）、be teachable（可教）、the soil of wisdom（智慧的土壤）

### 34. On Compassion（论慈悲）

Compassion is the ability to feel with another, to enter into their suffering as if it were our own. It is more than pity, which looks down; compassion stands beside. It moves us to act, not merely to feel. Compassion begins with attention—truly seeing the person in front of us. In a hurried world, attention is a gift we can give freely. The compassionate life is not an easy one, for it opens us to the pain of others. But it is the life most worth living. In caring for others, we become fully human.

慈悲是与他者同感的能力，走进他们的苦难，如同那是我们自己的一般。它不止于俯视的怜悯，慈悲是并肩而立。它推动我们行动，而不只是感受。慈悲始于专注——真正看见面前的人。在匆忙的世界里，专注是我们能自由给予的礼物。慈悲的人生并不轻松，因为它让我们对他人的痛苦敞开。但这是最值得过的人生。在关怀他人之中，我们成为完整的人。

**词与短语**：pity looks down（怜悯俯视）、stand beside（并肩而立）、open us to the pain（让我们对痛苦敞开）

### 35. The Examined Life（审视的人生）

Socrates famously declared that the unexamined life is not worth living. What did he mean? He meant that a life lived on autopilot—by habit, convention and the expectations of others—is only half-lived. To examine life is to ask why we do what we do, what we value and whether we are living by our own lights. This questioning is uncomfortable, for it may unsettle our comfortable assumptions. Yet it is the only path to authenticity. The examined life is not the easy life, but it is a life that is truly our own.

苏格拉底曾著名地宣称，未经审视的人生不值得过。他是什么意思？他的意思是，靠自动驾驶度过的人生——被习惯、习俗和他人的期望所驱动——只活了一半。审视人生，就是追问我们为何做所做的事、我们珍视什么、我们是否按自己的标准生活。这种追问令人不适，因为它可能动摇我们舒适的假设。然而它是通往真实的唯一道路。被审视的人生不是轻松的人生，但它是真正属于我们自己的人生。

**词与短语**：on autopilot（靠自动驾驶）、by our own lights（按自己的标准）、the path to authenticity（通往真实之路）

## 八、自然与宇宙

### 36. On Nature（论自然）

We speak of "nature" as though it were something outside us, a place we visit on weekends. But we are nature—we are made of the same elements, subject to the same rhythms. The breath we draw and the water we drink are nature flowing through us. To damage the natural world is to damage ourselves, slowly but surely. Those who spend time in wild places often report a sense of homecoming, a recognition deeper than words. We protect nature not out of charity but out of self-knowledge. We are its children, not its masters.

我们谈论"自然"，仿佛它是我们之外的东西，一个周末去游玩的地方。但我们就是自然——由相同的元素构成，遵循相同的节律。我们吸入的空气、饮用的水，都是流经我们的自然。伤害自然世界，就是伤害我们自己，缓慢却确定。在荒野中度过时光的人常说有一种回家的感觉，一种深于言语的辨认。我们保护自然，不是出于施舍，而是出于自知。我们是它的孩子，不是它的主人。

**词与短语**：the same rhythms（相同的节律）、a sense of homecoming（回家的感觉）、its children, not its masters（它的孩子而非主人）

### 37. The Human Scale（人类的尺度）

There is a strange disproportion between the vastness of the universe and the smallness of human life. We build towers and write histories, yet we are brief sparks against the dark. This thought can be crushing—or it can be liberating. If the universe is indifferent to us, then meaning is ours to make. Our smallness does not make our lives insignificant; it makes them precious. We are the universe made conscious, the cosmos reflecting on itself. Even a spark can illuminate the dark, for a time.

宇宙的浩瀚与人类生命的渺小之间，有一种奇异的失衡。我们建造高塔、书写历史，却只是黑暗中的短暂火花。这一念头可以令人崩溃，也可以令人解脱。如果宇宙对我们漠不关心，那么意义便由我们自己创造。我们的渺小并不使我们的生命无足轻重，反而使它珍贵。我们是变得有意识的宇宙，是反思自身的星辰。即便是一粒火花，也能在一段时间里照亮黑暗。

**词与短语**：brief sparks against the dark（黑暗中的短暂火花）、meaning is ours to make（意义由我们创造）、the universe made conscious（变得有意识的宇宙）

### 38. On Wonder（论惊奇）

Children are natural philosophers because they are filled with wonder. They ask why the sky is blue and where the stars go in the morning. Somewhere along the way, many of us lose this capacity, trading wonder for familiarity. But wonder is not childish; it is the root of science, art and religion alike. It is the recognition that the world is deeper and stranger than we know. To recover wonder is to recover the freshness of the world. The truly educated person is one who has never lost the capacity to be astonished.

孩子是天生的哲学家，因为他们充满惊奇。他们问天空为什么是蓝的、早晨星星去了哪里。在成长路上，许多人失去了这种能力，用熟悉取代了惊奇。但惊奇并非幼稚，它是科学、艺术和宗教共同的根源。它是对"世界比我们所知更深邃、更奇异"的认识。重拾惊奇，就是重拾世界的新鲜。真正有教养的人，是那些从未失去惊奇能力的人。

**词与短语**：trade wonder for familiarity（用熟悉取代惊奇）、the root of science（科学的根源）、be astonished（感到惊奇）

### 39. The Fragile Earth（脆弱的地球）

From space, the Earth is a small blue marble, fragile and alone in the vast dark. This image, more than any argument, changed how many people see our home. It revealed that our world is finite—its air, water and soil are not endless. We are the first generation to truly understand this, and the last with the power to act in time. The Earth will survive us; the question is whether we will survive ourselves. Stewardship of this fragile planet is the defining moral task of our age. We hold the future in our hands, whether we choose to or not.

从太空望去，地球是一颗小小的蓝色弹珠，在浩瀚黑暗中脆弱而孤独。这一图景比任何论据都更能改变许多人看待家园的方式。它揭示了我们的世界是有限的——空气、水和土壤并非无穷无尽。我们是第一代真正理解这一点的人，也是最后一代有能力及时行动的人。地球会在我们之后存续；问题在于我们自己能否存续。守护这颗脆弱的星球，是我们这个时代决定性的道德使命。无论我们是否选择，未来都已在我们手中。

**词与短语**：a small blue marble（蓝色弹珠）、finite（有限的）、the defining moral task（决定性的道德使命）

### 40. On Solitude in Nature（自然中的独处）

There is a solitude found in nature that is different from loneliness. To stand alone among mountains or by the sea is to feel, paradoxically, connected—to something larger than oneself. The noise of human life fades, and one hears a deeper rhythm. Many of the greatest works of thought and art were conceived in such moments. Nature does not speak in words, but those who listen carefully hear much. We go into nature not to escape life but to return to it with clearer eyes. Solitude in nature is not emptiness but fullness.

自然中能找到一种不同于孤独的独处。独自立于群山之间或大海之畔，反而会感到一种连接——与比自身更大的事物相连。人世喧嚣消退，人便听见一种更深的节律。许多最伟大的思想与艺术作品，都孕育于这样的时刻。自然不以言语说话，但仔细倾听的人能听见许多。我们走进自然，不是逃避生活，而是为了带着更清晰的眼睛回到生活。自然中的独处不是空虚，而是丰盈。

**词与短语**：paradoxically（反而）、a deeper rhythm（更深的节律）、with clearer eyes（带着更清晰的眼睛）

## 九、艺术与创造

### 41. On Creativity（论创造力）

Creativity is often romanticized as a lightning bolt of inspiration. In reality, it is usually the slow fruit of discipline. The artist works daily, even when inspiration is absent, trusting that the muse visits the prepared mind. Creativity is not a gift granted to a few but a capacity present in all, waiting to be cultivated. It thrives on curiosity, on play, on the willingness to fail. The creative act is a small act of courage—making something where nothing was. To create is to participate in the ongoing work of the world.

创造力常被浪漫化为灵感的闪电。实际上，它通常是自律结出的缓慢果实。艺术家每日工作，即便灵感缺席，也相信缪斯眷顾有准备的头脑。创造力不是只赐予少数人的天赋，而是存在于所有人身上的能力，等待被培养。它靠好奇、玩耍和甘愿失败而茁壮。创造行为是一种小小的勇气——在空无之处造出某物。创造，就是参与到世界正在进行的工程之中。

**词与短语**：a lightning bolt（一道闪电）、the prepared mind（有准备的头脑）、make something where nothing was（在空无之处造出某物）

### 42. The Creative Life（创造的人生）

The creative life is not reserved for artists. Anyone who brings care, imagination and originality to their work lives creatively. It is the difference between doing a job and doing it as only you can. The creative life requires courage, for it means departing from the well-worn path. It also requires patience, for mastery comes slowly. But those who live this way report a deeper satisfaction than any applause can give. To live creatively is to be fully alive. We are all capable of more originality than we believe.

创造的人生不专属于艺术家。任何把用心、想象和独创投入工作的人，都在创造地生活。这是"做一份工作"与"以唯有你能做到的方式做它"之间的区别。创造的人生需要勇气，因为它意味着离开那条走熟的路。它也需要耐心，因为精通来得缓慢。但以这种方式生活的人，会体验到比任何掌声都更深沉的满足。创造地生活，就是全然鲜活地生活。我们都拥有比自己以为的更多的独创性。

**词与短语**：be reserved for（专属于）、the well-worn path（走熟的路）、be fully alive（全然鲜活）

### 43. On Music（论音乐）

Music is the most abstract of the arts, and perhaps for that reason the most universal. It needs no translation; it speaks directly to the heart. A melody can carry grief or joy more purely than words. Music binds people together—in song, in dance, in shared silence at a concert. It is also deeply personal, a soundtrack to the private moments of our lives. Science has shown that music touches the oldest parts of the brain. Perhaps that is why it moves us so deeply: it speaks to something older than language, older even than thought.

音乐是最抽象的艺术，或许正因如此也最普遍。它无需翻译，直接对心灵说话。一段旋律能比言语更纯粹地承载悲伤或喜悦。音乐把人们联结在一起——在歌声中、在舞蹈中、在音乐会上的共同静默中。它也是极为私人的，是我们生活私密时刻的配乐。科学表明，音乐触及大脑最古老的部分。或许正因如此它才如此打动我们：它对某种比语言更古老、甚至比思想更古老的东西说话。

**词与短语**：the most abstract of the arts（最抽象的艺术）、speak directly to the heart（直接对心灵说话）、older than language（比语言更古老）

### 44. On Craft（论技艺）

Craft is the marriage of skill and care. The craftsman does not merely make an object; they pour attention into it, and the object bears the mark. Craft takes time, and that is its point—it is a quiet rebellion against haste. In a world of disposable things, the handmade object stands for permanence. The practice of craft also shapes the maker, teaching patience, precision and pride in good work. We have lost much in the age of mass production, but craft is not dead. Wherever someone does a thing well and slowly, craft lives on.

技艺是技能与用心的结合。手艺人不仅制作物品，他们向其中倾注关注，物品便留下了印记。技艺需要时间，而这正是它的意义所在——它是对匆忙的安静反叛。在一个一次性物品泛滥的世界里，手工制品代表着恒久。技艺的实践也塑造着制作者，教会他们耐心、精准和对好作品的自豪。在大规模生产的时代我们失去了很多，但技艺并未消亡。凡是有人把一件事做得又好又慢的地方，技艺便生生不息。

**词与短语**：the marriage of skill and care（技能与用心的结合）、bear the mark（留下印记）、live on（生生不息）

### 45. On Failure and Art（论失败与艺术）

Every artist knows failure intimately. For every finished work, there are countless discarded attempts. Failure is not the opposite of creativity but an essential part of it. It is through failure that we learn what does not work and inch toward what does. The fear of failure, more than failure itself, is what silences so many would-be creators. To create is to accept that most attempts will fall short, and to continue anyway. Mastery is not the absence of failure but a long friendship with it. The work that matters is often found on the far side of many failures.

每个艺术家都熟悉失败。每一件完成的作品背后，都有无数次被放弃的尝试。失败不是创造力的对立面，而是它的重要部分。正是通过失败，我们才知道什么行不通，并向行得通的方向寸寸推进。比失败本身更能让众多潜在创作者沉默的，是对失败的恐惧。创造，就是接受大多数尝试都会不足，却依然继续。精通不是没有失败，而是与失败的长久友谊。真正重要的作品，往往在无数次失败的那一头。

**词与短语**：know intimately（熟悉）、inch toward（寸寸推进）、a long friendship with it（与它的长久友谊）

## 十、反思与智慧

### 46. On Aging（论变老）

Aging is a subject our culture avoids, as though it were a defeat rather than a privilege. Yet to grow old is to be given more years than many are granted. Age brings losses, to be sure—of strength, of speed, of certainties. But it can also bring gifts: perspective, patience, a deeper appreciation of what matters. The old have seen fashions come and go, and know what endures. A society that honors its elders honors its own future. For we are all, if we are fortunate, growing old.

变老是我们文化回避的话题，仿佛它是一种失败而非特权。然而能够变老，是被赐予了比许多人更多的岁月。诚然，年龄带来失去——力量、速度、确定性。但它也能带来馈赠：洞察、耐心、对重要之物的更深领悟。老人见过潮流来去，知道什么能够长存。尊敬长者的社会，是在尊敬自己的未来。因为幸运的话，我们都正在变老。

**词与短语**：a privilege（一种特权）、what endures（能够长存的东西）、honor its elders（尊敬长者）

### 47. On Mortality（论死亡）

We live, all of us, in the shadow of mortality, though we rarely admit it. The knowledge that our time is finite is both a burden and a gift. It is a burden because it casts uncertainty over all our plans. It is a gift because it makes every moment precious. Those who forget death waste their days; those who remember it live them fully. Contemplating mortality is not morbid but clarifying—it asks us what we truly want to do with the time we have. Death is not the opposite of life but its frame. It is what gives our days their urgency and their worth.

我们所有人都活在死亡的阴影下，尽管很少承认。知道自己时间有限，既是负担，也是礼物。它是负担，因为它给所有计划投下不确定性；它是礼物，因为它让每一刻都珍贵。忘记死亡的人虚度光阴，记得死亡的人充分生活。沉思死亡不是病态，而是澄清——它问我们，我们真正想用所拥有的时间做什么。死亡不是生命的对立面，而是它的画框。正是它赋予我们的日子以紧迫和价值。

**词与短语**：a burden and a gift（负担与礼物）、cast uncertainty（投下不确定性）、its frame（它的画框）

### 48. On Success Redefined（重新定义成功）

We are taught to measure success in wealth, status and applause. But these measures, taken alone, are poor guides to a good life. There are many wealthy and admired people who are deeply unhappy. Perhaps success should be measured differently—by the quality of our relationships, the integrity of our work, the depth of our contentment. True success is living in a way that is true to one's own values. It is not a destination to reach but a manner of traveling. The most successful life is one you would choose again, knowing all it contained.

我们被教导用财富、地位和掌声来衡量成功。但这些标准，单独来看，是通往美好人生的糟糕向导。有许多富有且受人仰慕的人却深深不快乐。也许成功应该以不同的方式来衡量——以我们关系的质量、工作的正直、满足的深度。真正的成功，是以忠于自己价值观的方式生活。它不是要抵达的终点，而是一种行走的方式。最成功的人生，是你在了解其全部内容后仍愿意再选一次的人生。

**词与短语**：poor guides（糟糕的向导）、the depth of contentment（满足的深度）、a manner of traveling（一种行走的方式）

### 49. On Contentment（论知足）

Contentment is often confused with complacency, but they are not the same. The complacent person stops striving; the content person strives without being ruled by dissatisfaction. Contentment is the ability to rest in the present while still moving forward. It is gratitude for what is, combined with hope for what may be. Our culture pushes us to want ever more, as if happiness were always one purchase away. Contentment is the quiet wisdom that enough is enough. The person who has learned this secret is rich beyond measure.

知足常与自满混淆，但二者不同。自满的人停止进取；知足的人进取却不被不满足所支配。知足是安于当下、同时仍向前的能力。它是对已有之物的感恩，与对未来可能的希望相结合。我们的文化驱使我们想要越来越多，仿佛幸福总在又一次购买之后。知足是一种安静的智慧：足够即是足够。学会这一秘密的人，富足无可估量。

**词与短语**：be confused with（与……混淆）、be ruled by dissatisfaction（被不满足支配）、rich beyond measure（富足无可估量）

### 50. The Art of Living（生活的艺术）

To live well is the highest art, and the least taught. We learn mathematics and medicine, but rarely how to live. The art of living has no single method, for each life is unique. Yet certain principles recur across the ages: attend to the present, cherish those you love, do work that matters, and keep wonder alive. The unexamined life may be comfortable, but the examined life is worth living. In the end, we are not judged by what we accumulated but by how we lived—whether we loved well, worked truly, and left the world a little better than we found it.

好好生活是最高的艺术，却最少被教授。我们学习数学和医学，却很少学习如何生活。生活的艺术没有单一方法，因为每个生命都是独特的。然而某些原则穿越时代反复出现：专注当下、珍惜所爱、做有意义的工作、保持惊奇。未经审视的人生或许安逸，但被审视的人生才值得过。归根结底，评判我们的不是我们积累了什么，而是我们如何生活——是否爱得真挚、工作得诚实，是否让世界比我们到来时好了那么一点点。

**词与短语**：the highest art（最高的艺术）、recur across the ages（穿越时代反复出现）、leave the world a little better（让世界好一点点）
