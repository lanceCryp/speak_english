## 一、科技与未来

### 1. Artificial Intelligence（人工智能）

Artificial intelligence is changing the world faster than ever. It can write, translate, drive and even create art. Some people welcome this progress, while others worry about losing their jobs. In my view, AI is a tool. Whether it helps or harms us depends on how we use it. We should guide its development carefully.

人工智能正以前所未有的速度改变世界。它能写作、翻译、驾驶甚至创作艺术。有人欢迎这种进步，有人担心失去工作。在我看来，AI 是一种工具，利或弊取决于我们如何使用它。我们应该谨慎引导它的发展。

**词与短语**：faster than ever（比以往更快）、welcome progress（欢迎进步）、depend on（取决于）

### 2. The Internet Age（互联网时代）

We live in the Internet age. Information that once took days to find is now available in seconds. The Internet has connected the world and created new opportunities. However, it has also brought problems like false information and privacy risks. The key is to use the Internet wisely and think critically.

我们生活在互联网时代。曾经要花几天才能找到的信息，现在几秒就能获得。互联网连接了世界、创造了新机会。但它也带来虚假信息和隐私风险等问题。关键是要明智地使用互联网、批判性地思考。

**词与短语**：available in seconds（几秒即可获得）、false information（虚假信息）、think critically（批判性思考）

### 3. Smartphones（智能手机）

Smartphones have become part of our daily lives. We use them to communicate, navigate and pay. They are powerful tools, but they can also distract us. Many people check their phones every few minutes. I believe we should control our phones, not the other way around.

智能手机已成为我们日常生活的一部分。我们用它们交流、导航、支付。它们是强大的工具，但也会分散注意力。很多人每隔几分钟就看手机。我认为我们应该控制手机，而不是反过来被手机控制。

**词与短语**：part of daily lives（日常生活的一部分）、distract us（分散注意力）、the other way around（反过来）

### 4. Online Learning（在线学习）

Online learning has become very popular. It allows people to study anytime and anywhere. Students can learn at their own pace and choose from countless courses. However, it requires strong self-discipline. Without it, many learners give up halfway. Online learning is convenient, but it is not for everyone.

在线学习变得非常流行。它让人们随时随地学习，可以按自己的节奏学，选择无数课程。但它需要很强的自律。没有自律，很多人半途而废。在线学习很方便，但并不适合所有人。

**词与短语**：at their own pace（按自己的节奏）、countless courses（无数课程）、give up halfway（半途而废）

### 5. The Future of Work（工作的未来）

The way we work is changing rapidly. Automation may replace many routine jobs, while new jobs will appear. Skills like creativity, communication and adaptability will become more important. To succeed in the future, we must keep learning and stay flexible. The only constant is change.

我们的工作方式正在迅速变化。自动化可能取代许多常规工作，同时新工作也会出现。创造力、沟通力和适应力等技能将变得更加重要。要在未来成功，我们必须不断学习、保持灵活。唯一不变的是变化。

**词与短语**：replace jobs（取代工作）、adaptability（适应力）、keep learning（不断学习）

## 二、社会议题

### 6. Education（教育）

Education is often called the key to a better future. It teaches us how to think, question and understand the world. A well-educated person is more likely to find a good job and make wise decisions. More importantly, education helps break the cycle of poverty. Every society should invest in it.

教育常被称为通往更美好未来的钥匙。它教会我们思考、质疑和理解世界。受过良好教育的人更可能找到好工作、做出明智决定。更重要的是，教育有助于打破贫困循环。每个社会都应投资教育。

**词与短语**：the key to（……的钥匙）、make wise decisions（做出明智决定）、break the cycle（打破循环）

### 7. Healthcare（医疗保健）

Access to healthcare is a basic need. In many places, however, medical care is expensive and hard to reach. People delay treatment because they cannot afford it, which makes their condition worse. A society is judged by how it treats its weakest members. Healthcare should be available to all.

获得医疗保健是一项基本需求。然而在许多地方，医疗服务昂贵且难以获得。人们因负担不起而拖延治疗，导致病情恶化。评判一个社会，要看它如何对待最弱势的成员。医疗保健应惠及所有人。

**词与短语**：access to（获得……的途径）、afford（负担得起）、judge a society（评判社会）

### 8. Inequality（不平等）

Inequality is a growing problem in many countries. The gap between the rich and the poor is widening. This can lead to social problems and a sense of unfairness. While complete equality is impossible, we should work to give everyone a fair chance. Opportunity should not depend on birth.

不平等在许多国家日益严重，贫富差距不断扩大。这会导致社会问题和不公平感。虽然完全平等不可能，但我们应该努力给每个人公平的机会。机会不应取决于出身。

**词与短语**：the gap between（……之间的差距）、a sense of unfairness（不公平感）、a fair chance（公平的机会）

### 9. Urbanization（城市化）

More and more people are moving to cities. Cities offer better jobs, education and entertainment. But rapid urbanization also brings problems, such as overcrowding, high housing prices and traffic. Cities must plan carefully to stay livable. A good city serves both its people and its environment.

越来越多的人迁往城市。城市提供更好的工作、教育和娱乐。但快速城市化也带来问题，如过度拥挤、高房价和交通拥堵。城市必须精心规划才能宜居。好城市既服务人民，也善待环境。

**词与短语**：more and more（越来越多）、rapid urbanization（快速城市化）、stay livable（保持宜居）

### 10. An Aging Society（老龄化社会）

Many societies are getting older. People live longer, but birth rates are falling. An aging society needs more healthcare and pensions, which puts pressure on younger generations. We must find ways to care for the elderly while keeping society strong. Aging is not a problem; lack of preparation is.

许多社会正在老龄化。人们寿命更长，但出生率在下降。老龄化社会需要更多医疗和养老金，给年轻一代带来压力。我们必须找到照顾老人、同时保持社会活力的办法。老龄化不是问题，缺乏准备才是。

**词与短语**：birth rates（出生率）、put pressure on（给……压力）、care for the elderly（照顾老人）

## 三、环境与可持续

### 11. Climate Change（气候变化）

Climate change is one of the greatest challenges of our time. Temperatures are rising, ice is melting and weather is becoming more extreme. Scientists agree that human activity is the main cause. If we do not act now, the consequences will be serious. Everyone has a part to play.

气候变化是我们这个时代最大的挑战之一。气温上升、冰川融化、天气愈发极端。科学家一致认为人类活动是主因。如果我们现在不行动，后果将很严重。每个人都有责任。

**词与短语**：one of the greatest challenges（最大的挑战之一）、the main cause（主因）、act now（立即行动）

### 12. Renewable Energy（可再生能源）

Renewable energy comes from sources that never run out, like the sun, wind and water. Unlike fossil fuels, it produces little pollution. Many countries are building more solar and wind power. Renewable energy is the key to a cleaner future, though it still has challenges such as storage.

可再生能源来自永不枯竭的资源，如太阳、风和水。与化石燃料不同，它几乎不产生污染。许多国家正在建设更多太阳能和风能。可再生能源是更清洁未来的关键，尽管它仍面临储能等挑战。

**词与短语**：run out（耗尽）、fossil fuels（化石燃料）、the key to（……的关键）

### 13. Plastic Pollution（塑料污染）

Plastic pollution is harming our planet. Millions of tons of plastic end up in the ocean every year, killing sea animals. Plastic takes hundreds of years to break down. We can help by using fewer plastic bags, recycling and choosing reusable products. Small actions add up.

塑料污染正在伤害我们的地球。每年数百万吨塑料流入海洋，杀害海洋动物。塑料需要数百年才能分解。我们可以通过少用塑料袋、回收利用和选择可重复使用的产品来帮忙。小行动汇聚成大改变。

**词与短语**：end up in（最终流入）、break down（分解）、add up（累积）

### 14. Protecting Wildlife（保护野生动物）

Many wild animals are in danger of disappearing. They lose their homes because of deforestation and hunting. Once a species disappears, it is gone forever. We should protect natural habitats and support conservation efforts. Every species plays a role in nature's balance.

许多野生动物濒临灭绝。由于森林砍伐和捕猎，它们失去了家园。一个物种一旦消失，就永远没有了。我们应该保护自然栖息地、支持保护工作。每个物种在自然平衡中都扮演着角色。

**词与短语**：in danger of（濒临）、deforestation（森林砍伐）、play a role（扮演角色）

### 15. Sustainable Living（可持续生活）

Sustainable living means meeting our needs without harming future generations. It involves saving energy, reducing waste and buying wisely. I have started taking small steps, like carrying my own water bottle and shopping locally. A sustainable life is not about sacrifice; it is about balance.

可持续生活意味着满足我们需求的同时不损害后代。它包括节约能源、减少浪费和理性消费。我已开始从小事做起，比如自带水杯、支持本地购物。可持续生活不是牺牲，而是平衡。

**词与短语**：meet our needs（满足需求）、future generations（后代）、take small steps（从小事做起）

## 四、职场与事业

### 16. Choosing a Career（选择职业）

Choosing a career is one of the biggest decisions in life. Some people choose a job for money, others for passion. Ideally, a career should offer both. Before deciding, we should understand our strengths and interests. A career is a long journey, so choose one you can enjoy for years.

选择职业是人生最大的决定之一。有人为钱选工作，有人为热爱。理想情况下，职业应两者兼具。做决定前，我们应该了解自己的优势和兴趣。职业是漫长的旅程，所以要选一个能享受多年的。

**词与短语**：one of the biggest decisions（最大的决定之一）、for money（为了钱）、a long journey（漫长的旅程）

### 17. Job Interviews（求职面试）

A job interview is your chance to show who you are. Preparation is the key to success. I always research the company and practice common questions. During the interview, I stay calm and speak clearly. Confidence and honesty leave a good impression. Practice makes a big difference.

求职面试是展示自己的机会。准备是成功的关键。我总是研究公司、练习常见问题。面试时保持冷静、表达清晰。自信和诚实会留下好印象。练习带来很大不同。

**词与短语**：the key to success（成功的关键）、stay calm（保持冷静）、leave a good impression（留下好印象）

### 18. Teamwork（团队合作）

Teamwork is essential in almost every job. A good team achieves more than any individual. In a team, we share ideas, support each other and solve problems together. However, teamwork also requires communication and compromise. Learning to work with others is a valuable skill for life.

团队合作几乎在每个工作中都至关重要。好的团队比任何个人成就更多。在团队中，我们分享想法、互相支持、共同解决问题。但团队合作也需要沟通和妥协。学会与人合作是一项宝贵的人生技能。

**词与短语**：be essential（至关重要）、achieve more（成就更多）、compromise（妥协）

### 19. Work-life Balance（工作与生活的平衡）

Many people struggle to balance work and life. They work long hours and have little time for family or hobbies. Over time, this harms both health and relationships. I believe we should set clear boundaries and make time to rest. A balanced life is a happier and more productive life.

许多人难以平衡工作与生活。他们长时间工作，几乎没有时间陪伴家人或发展爱好。久而久之，这会损害健康和人际关系。我认为我们应该设定明确的界限、留出休息时间。平衡的生活更快乐、更高效。

**词与短语**：struggle to（努力做……）、set boundaries（设定界限）、a balanced life（平衡的生活）

### 20. Leadership（领导力）

Leadership is not just about giving orders. A good leader listens, inspires and takes responsibility. Great leaders bring out the best in their team. They lead by example and stay calm in difficult times. Anyone can become a leader with practice, empathy and courage.

领导力不只是发号施令。好的领导者倾听、激励并承担责任。伟大的领导者激发团队的最佳表现。他们以身作则，在困难时刻保持冷静。只要有实践、同理心和勇气，任何人都能成为领导者。

**词与短语**：give orders（发号施令）、bring out the best（激发最佳表现）、lead by example（以身作则）

## 五、文化与交流

### 21. Cultural Differences（文化差异）

Every culture has its own way of doing things. Greetings, food and even silence can mean different things in different places. Understanding these differences helps us avoid misunderstandings. When we meet people from other cultures, we should stay open-minded and curious. Diversity makes the world richer.

每种文化都有自己的行事方式。问候、食物甚至沉默在不同地方含义不同。理解这些差异有助于避免误解。遇到来自其他文化的人时，我们应该保持开放和好奇。多样性让世界更丰富。

**词与短语**：its own way（它自己的方式）、avoid misunderstandings（避免误解）、stay open-minded（保持开放）

### 22. Language Learning（语言学习）

Learning a language opens doors to new worlds. It lets us connect with people and understand their culture. The best way to learn is to use the language every day, not just study rules. I practice by watching films, listening to music and talking to native speakers. Mistakes are part of learning.

学习一门语言打开了通往新世界的大门。它让我们与人连接、理解他们的文化。最好的方法是每天使用这门语言，而不只是学规则。我通过看电影、听音乐和与母语者交流来练习。犯错是学习的一部分。

**词与短语**：open doors（打开大门）、native speakers（母语者）、part of learning（学习的一部分）

### 23. Globalization（全球化）

Globalization has brought the world closer together. Goods, ideas and people move across borders more freely than ever. It has created wealth and spread knowledge. Yet it also has a downside, such as cultural loss and inequality. Globalization is a powerful force that must be managed wisely.

全球化让世界更紧密。商品、思想和人员比以往更自由地跨越国界。它创造了财富、传播了知识。但它也有弊端，如文化流失和不平等。全球化是一股强大的力量，必须明智地加以管理。

**词与短语**：bring closer together（拉近距离）、spread knowledge（传播知识）、be managed wisely（明智地管理）

### 24. Preserving Traditions（保护传统）

Traditions connect us to our past and give us a sense of identity. In a fast-changing world, many traditions are disappearing. Some people think this is natural, while others want to protect them. I believe we should preserve valuable traditions while staying open to change. Both old and new have a place.

传统把我们与过去相连，给我们认同感。在快速变化的世界里，许多传统正在消失。有人认为这是自然的，有人则想保护它们。我认为我们应该保护有价值的传统，同时对变化保持开放。新旧各有其位置。

**词与短语**：a sense of identity（认同感）、disappearing（正在消失）、preserve traditions（保护传统）

### 25. The Power of Media（媒体的力量）

The media shapes how we see the world. It informs us, but it can also mislead us. News can be biased, and not everything we read is true. That is why media literacy is so important. We should check facts, compare sources and think for ourselves. An informed mind is a free mind.

媒体塑造我们看待世界的方式。它告知我们信息，但也可能误导我们。新闻可能带有偏见，不是我们读到的一切都是真的。这就是媒体素养如此重要的原因。我们应该核实事实、比较来源、独立思考。有见识的头脑才是自由的头脑。

**词与短语**：shape how we see（塑造我们的看法）、be biased（有偏见）、media literacy（媒体素养）

## 六、教育

### 26. The Purpose of Education（教育的目的）

What is education really for? Some say it is to prepare students for jobs. Others believe it is to develop the whole person. I think education should do both. It should give us skills to earn a living and wisdom to live a good life. Education is about more than passing exams.

教育究竟是为了什么？有人说它是为工作做准备，有人认为是培养完整的人。我认为教育应该两者兼有——既给我们谋生的技能，也给我们好好生活的智慧。教育不止于通过考试。

**词与短语**：prepare for（为……准备）、develop the whole person（培养完整的人）、more than（不止于）

### 27. Online vs Traditional（线上与线下学习）

Online learning and traditional classrooms each have strengths. Online learning is flexible and accessible, while classrooms offer direct interaction and structure. For me, a mix of both works best. The best method depends on the subject and the learner. There is no single right answer.

在线学习和传统课堂各有优势。在线学习灵活、易获取，而课堂提供直接互动和结构。对我来说，两者结合最好。最好的方法取决于科目和学习者，没有唯一正确答案。

**词与短语**：each have strengths（各有优势）、direct interaction（直接互动）、no single answer（没有唯一答案）

### 28. Studying Abroad（出国留学）

Studying abroad is a dream for many students. It offers a world-class education and the chance to experience a new culture. However, it can also be expensive and lonely at first. Those who study abroad must be independent and adaptable. For many, the rewards are worth the challenges.

出国留学是许多学生的梦想。它提供世界一流的教育和体验新文化的机会。但它也可能昂贵、起初孤独。留学生必须独立、适应力强。对许多人来说，回报值得付出。

**词与短语**：a world-class education（世界一流的教育）、experience a new culture（体验新文化）、be worth the challenges（值得付出）

### 29. Lifelong Learning（终身学习）

Learning does not stop when we leave school. In a fast-changing world, we must keep learning throughout life. Lifelong learning keeps our minds sharp and opens new doors. I try to learn something new every year, whether a skill or a subject. The day we stop learning is the day we stop growing.

学习不会在离开学校时停止。在快速变化的世界里，我们必须终身学习。终身学习让头脑敏锐、开启新大门。我每年都尝试学点新东西，无论是技能还是学科。停止学习的那天，就是停止成长的那天。

**词与短语**：keep learning（持续学习）、keep minds sharp（让头脑敏锐）、stop growing（停止成长）

### 30. Critical Thinking（批判性思维）

Critical thinking is the ability to think clearly and question what we are told. It helps us tell facts from opinions and make better decisions. In a world full of information, this skill is more valuable than ever. We can develop it by asking questions and considering different viewpoints. Think, don't just believe.

批判性思维是清晰思考、质疑所听所闻的能力。它帮我们区分事实与观点、做出更好的决定。在信息泛滥的世界里，这项技能比以往更宝贵。我们可以通过提问和考虑不同观点来培养它。要思考，不要盲信。

**词与短语**：tell facts from opinions（区分事实与观点）、make better decisions（做更好的决定）、viewpoints（观点）

## 七、健康与心理

### 31. Exercise and Health（运动与健康）

Regular exercise is one of the best things we can do for our health. It strengthens the heart, improves mood and helps us sleep better. You do not need to run a marathon; a daily walk is enough. The key is to be consistent. A healthy body supports a healthy mind.

规律运动是我们能为健康做的最好的事之一。它强健心脏、改善情绪、帮助睡眠。你不需要跑马拉松，每天散步就够了。关键是坚持。健康的身体支撑健康的心灵。

**词与短语**：strengthen the heart（强健心脏）、improve mood（改善情绪）、be consistent（坚持）

### 32. Healthy Eating（健康饮食）

Healthy eating is about balance, not perfection. I try to eat more whole foods and less processed food. I cook at home more often and read food labels. Eating well gives me energy and protects my health in the long run. Small changes in diet bring big benefits.

健康饮食关乎平衡，而非完美。我尽量多吃天然食物、少吃加工食品，更多在家做饭，并阅读食品标签。吃得好给我能量，从长远看保护健康。饮食上的小改变带来大好处。

**词与短语**：whole foods（天然食物）、processed food（加工食品）、in the long run（从长远看）

### 33. Stress Management（压力管理）

Stress is a normal part of life, but too much of it is harmful. Managing stress means finding healthy ways to cope. I exercise, practice deep breathing and talk to people I trust. I also try to keep a positive attitude. How we handle stress shapes our well-being.

压力是生活的正常部分，但过多有害。管理压力意味着找到健康的应对方式。我运动、练习深呼吸、和信任的人交谈，也尽量保持积极心态。我们如何应对压力，决定了我们的身心健康。

**词与短语**：a normal part of life（生活的正常部分）、cope（应对）、shape our well-being（塑造我们的健康）

### 34. The Mind-body Connection（身心联系）

Our mind and body are deeply connected. When we are stressed, our body feels it; when we are sick, our mood drops. Taking care of one helps the other. That is why I value both physical exercise and mental rest. A healthy life treats the whole person, not just one part.

我们的身心紧密相连。压力大时，身体能感受到；生病时，情绪低落。照顾好一方有助于另一方。所以我既重视身体锻炼，也重视精神休息。健康的生活关照完整的人，而非某一部分。

**词与短语**：deeply connected（紧密相连）、take care of（照顾）、the whole person（完整的人）

### 35. Sleep and Productivity（睡眠与效率）

Many people think sleeping less means getting more done. In fact, the opposite is true. Lack of sleep harms focus, memory and mood. Well-rested people work faster and make fewer mistakes. I have learned to treat sleep as a priority, not a luxury. Good sleep is the foundation of productivity.

许多人以为睡得少就能做更多事。事实上恰恰相反。睡眠不足损害专注、记忆和情绪。休息好的人工作更快、犯错更少。我学会把睡眠当作优先事项而非奢侈品。良好睡眠是效率的基础。

**词与短语**：the opposite is true（事实恰恰相反）、lack of sleep（睡眠不足）、a priority（优先事项）

## 八、经济与消费

### 36. Money Management（理财）

Managing money is a life skill everyone needs. I learned it the hard way when I spent my allowance too quickly. Now I make a budget, track my spending and save a little each month. The habit of saving gives me freedom and security. Money works for you when you manage it well.

理财是每个人都需要的生活技能。我是在零花钱花得太快后才吸取教训的。现在我做预算、记录开支、每月存一点钱。储蓄的习惯给我自由和安全感。管理好金钱，它就会为你工作。

**词与短语**：learn the hard way（吃了亏才学会）、make a budget（做预算）、track spending（记录开支）

### 37. Consumer Society（消费社会）

We live in a consumer society that encourages us to buy more. Advertisements tell us that happiness comes from things. But endless buying often leads to clutter and debt, not joy. I try to buy only what I need and value experiences over objects. True wealth is needing less.

我们生活在一个鼓励多买的消费社会。广告告诉我们幸福来自物质。但无休止的购买往往带来杂乱和债务，而非快乐。我尽量只买需要的，重视体验胜过物品。真正的富足是所需更少。

**词与短语**：consumer society（消费社会）、value...over...（重视……胜过……）、true wealth（真正的富足）

### 38. Advertising（广告）

Advertising is everywhere, and it is powerful. It tells us what to buy and even how to feel. Good ads are creative, but some ads are misleading. As consumers, we should think before we buy. Understanding how ads work helps us make smarter choices and avoid impulse buying.

广告无处不在，而且很有力量。它告诉我们买什么，甚至教我们如何感受。好广告有创意，但有些广告有误导性。作为消费者，我们应该先思考再购买。理解广告的运作方式能帮我们做出更明智的选择、避免冲动消费。

**词与短语**：be misleading（有误导性）、make smart choices（做出明智选择）、impulse buying（冲动消费）

### 39. Saving vs Spending（储蓄与消费）

Some people save every penny, while others spend freely. The right balance lies somewhere in between. Saving gives us security for the future, but we should also enjoy the present. I follow a simple rule: save for the future, spend on what truly matters. Balance is the key.

有人拼命储蓄，有人随意消费。正确的平衡在两者之间。储蓄给未来保障，但我们也应享受当下。我遵循一条简单原则：为未来储蓄，把钱花在真正重要的事上。平衡是关键。

**词与短语**：somewhere in between（在两者之间）、enjoy the present（享受当下）、follow a rule（遵循原则）

### 40. The Sharing Economy（共享经济）

The sharing economy lets people share things instead of owning them. Ridesharing and home-sharing are famous examples. It can save money and reduce waste. However, it also raises questions about safety and regulation. Like any new idea, it has both benefits and risks. We should weigh them carefully.

共享经济让人们共享而非拥有。拼车和共享住宿是著名例子。它能省钱、减少浪费，但也引发安全和监管问题。像任何新事物一样，它有利有弊，我们应仔细权衡。

**词与短语**：instead of（而不是）、reduce waste（减少浪费）、weigh them carefully（仔细权衡）

## 九、个人成长

### 41. Overcoming Challenges（克服挑战）

Challenges are a part of life, and how we face them defines us. When I meet a difficulty, I break it into smaller steps and tackle them one by one. I also ask for help when I need it. Every challenge I overcome makes me stronger. Hard times build strong people.

挑战是生活的一部分，我们如何面对它定义了我们。遇到困难时，我把它拆成小步骤，一个个解决。需要时我也寻求帮助。每克服一个挑战，我就变得更强大。艰难岁月造就坚强的人。

**词与短语**：define us（定义我们）、tackle（应对）、build strong people（造就坚强的人）

### 42. Building Confidence（建立自信）

Confidence is not something we are born with; it is something we build. I build confidence by preparing well and celebrating small wins. I also stop comparing myself to others. The more I practice and succeed, the more confident I become. Confidence grows with action.

自信不是天生的，而是培养出来的。我通过充分准备和庆祝小胜利来建立自信，也不再拿自己和别人比较。练习和成功的次数越多，我就越自信。自信随行动而增长。

**词与短语**：be born with（天生就有）、celebrate small wins（庆祝小胜利）、grow with action（随行动增长）

### 43. Time Management（时间管理）

Time management is about using time wisely, not just being busy. I plan my day and set priorities. I do the most important things first and avoid wasting time on distractions. Good time management reduces stress and boosts productivity. Time is the one resource we cannot get back.

时间管理是明智地利用时间，而不只是忙碌。我规划一天、设定优先级，先做最重要的事，避免把时间浪费在分心的事上。好的时间管理减少压力、提高效率。时间是我们无法追回的资源。

**词与短语**：set priorities（设定优先级）、avoid distractions（避免分心）、boost productivity（提高效率）

### 44. Habits and Success（习惯与成功）

Success is built on habits, not occasional effort. Small daily actions add up over time. I have built habits like reading and exercising that support my goals. Good habits make hard things easier. You do not rise to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your habits.

成功建立在习惯之上，而非偶尔的努力。每天的小行动会随时间累积。我养成了阅读、运动等支持目标的习惯。好习惯让难事变易。你达不到目标的高度，只会滑落到习惯的水平。

**词与短语**：add up over time（随时间累积）、support my goals（支持我的目标）、rise to the level（达到……水平）

### 45. Finding Your Passion（找到你的热爱）

Finding your passion is a journey, not a destination. Some people know early what they love; others discover it slowly. The best way is to try new things and notice what excites you. Passion often grows from doing, not from thinking. Follow what makes you lose track of time.

寻找热爱是一段旅程，而非终点。有人很早就知道自己爱什么，有人慢慢发现。最好的方法是尝试新事物、留意什么让你兴奋。热爱往往源于行动，而非空想。追随那些让你忘记时间的事。

**词与短语**：a journey, not a destination（旅程而非终点）、try new things（尝试新事物）、lose track of time（忘记时间）

## 十、观点与辩论

### 46. Is Money Everything?（金钱是万能的吗）

Is money everything? Many people act as if it is, but I disagree. Money can buy comfort, medicine and education, but it cannot buy love, health or time. The richest people are not always the happiest. Money is a tool, not a purpose. Life is about more than wealth.

金钱是万能的吗？很多人表现得好像是，但我不同意。钱能买到舒适、医疗和教育，却买不到爱、健康和时间。最富有的人未必最幸福。金钱是工具，不是目的。生活不止于财富。

**词与短语**：act as if（表现得好像）、a tool, not a purpose（工具而非目的）、more than wealth（不止于财富）

### 47. Freedom and Responsibility（自由与责任）

Freedom and responsibility go hand in hand. True freedom is not doing whatever we want; it is choosing wisely and accepting the results. With every freedom comes a responsibility. A society works only when people use their freedom carefully. Freedom without responsibility is chaos.

自由与责任相伴而行。真正的自由不是为所欲为，而是明智选择并承担结果。每份自由都伴随责任。只有当人们谨慎使用自由时，社会才能运转。没有责任的自由是混乱。

**词与短语**：go hand in hand（相伴而行）、accept the results（承担结果）、chaos（混乱）

### 48. Success and Luck（成功与运气）

How much of success is luck? Hard work matters a lot, but luck also plays a role. People are born into different situations with different opportunities. Recognizing luck makes us humble and grateful. At the same time, we should not use bad luck as an excuse. Work hard and stay open to chances.

成功有多少是运气？努力很重要，但运气也起作用。人们出生在不同环境，机会不同。承认运气让我们谦逊和感恩。同时，我们也不该把坏运气当借口。努力奋斗，并对机遇保持开放。

**词与短语**：play a role（起作用）、be born into（出生于）、stay open to chances（对机遇保持开放）

### 49. The Role of Competition（竞争的作用）

Competition can push us to improve, but too much of it can be harmful. Healthy competition motivates us to do our best. Unhealthy competition creates stress and envy. I believe we should compete with ourselves more than with others. The goal is growth, not just winning.

竞争能推动我们进步，但过度竞争有害。健康的竞争激励我们全力以赴，不健康的竞争制造压力和嫉妒。我认为我们应更多与自己竞争，而非他人。目标是成长，而不只是获胜。

**词与短语**：push us to improve（推动我们进步）、motivate us（激励我们）、the goal is growth（目标是成长）

### 50. Privacy in the Digital Age（数字时代的隐私）

Privacy is becoming a luxury in the digital age. Companies collect our data, and our online actions leave traces. Many people accept this for convenience, but the cost may be high. We should understand how our data is used and protect what matters. Privacy is a right worth defending.

在数字时代，隐私正成为一种奢侈品。公司收集我们的数据，我们的网络行为留下痕迹。许多人为了便利而接受这一点，但代价可能很高。我们应该了解数据如何被使用，并保护重要的东西。隐私是值得捍卫的权利。

**词与短语**：a luxury（一种奢侈品）、leave traces（留下痕迹）、worth defending（值得捍卫）
