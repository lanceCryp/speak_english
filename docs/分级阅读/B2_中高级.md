# B2 分级阅读 · 中高级

> 对应 CEFR B2（约 3,000–4,000 词）。短文涉及抽象与社会话题，使用条件句、被动语态和复杂从句，适合培养论证与成篇表达。

## 1. Technology and Our Lives（科技与我们的生活）

**英文正文**

Technology has changed our lives in ways that were unimaginable a century ago. Thanks to the Internet, we can now communicate with people all over the world in seconds. However, like a double-edged sword, technology also brings problems. Many people spend too much time on their phones and feel lonely in real life. In my view, technology itself is neutral; it depends on how we use it. If we use it wisely, it can make our lives much better.

**中文译文**

科技以一百年前难以想象的方式改变了我们的生活。多亏了互联网，我们现在能在几秒钟内与世界各地的人交流。然而，像一把双刃剑一样，科技也带来了问题。许多人花太多时间在手机上，在现实生活中感到孤独。在我看来，科技本身是中性的，关键在于我们如何使用它。如果我们明智地使用它，它能让我们的生活变得更好。

**重点词汇与短语**

- in ways that were unimaginable（以难以想象的方式）
- all over the world（世界各地）
- a double-edged sword（双刃剑）
- spend time on（花时间在……上）
- depend on（取决于）

## 2. Protecting the Environment（保护环境）

**英文正文**

Environmental protection has become one of the most urgent issues of our time. Forests are being cut down, oceans are being polluted, and many animals are in danger of disappearing. If we had taken action earlier, the situation might not be so serious. Fortunately, more and more people have realized the importance of living in a sustainable way. Small actions, such as recycling and saving energy, can make a big difference when everyone takes part.

**中文译文**

环境保护已成为我们这个时代最紧迫的问题之一。森林被砍伐，海洋被污染，许多动物濒临灭绝。如果我们早一点采取行动，情况也许不会这么严重。幸运的是，越来越多的人已经意识到可持续生活的重要性。诸如回收利用、节约能源这样的小行动，当人人都参与时，就能带来巨大的改变。

**重点词汇与短语**

- one of the most urgent issues（最紧迫的问题之一）
- be cut down / be polluted（被砍伐 / 被污染）
- in danger of disappearing（濒临灭绝）
- in a sustainable way（以可持续的方式）
- make a big difference（带来巨大改变）

## 3. The Value of Education（教育的价值）

**英文正文**

Education is often described as the key to a better future, and for good reason. It does not simply mean passing exams; it teaches us how to think, question and understand the world. A well-educated person is more likely to find a good job and make wiser decisions. More importantly, education helps break the cycle of poverty and brings equal opportunities to everyone. That is why every society should invest heavily in education.

**中文译文**

教育常被形容为通往更美好未来的钥匙，这是有充分理由的。它不仅仅意味着通过考试，它还教会我们如何思考、质疑和理解世界。一个受过良好教育的人更可能找到好工作、做出更明智的决定。更重要的是，教育有助于打破贫困的循环，为每个人带来平等的机会。这就是为什么每个社会都应大力投资教育。

**重点词汇与短语**

- the key to a better future（通往更美好未来的钥匙）
- for good reason（有充分理由地）
- pass exams（通过考试）
- break the cycle of poverty（打破贫困的循环）
- invest in（投资于）

## 4. Working Across Cultures（跨文化合作）

**英文正文**

In today's globalized world, people from different cultures often work together. This can be both rewarding and challenging. On the one hand, diverse teams are more creative because members bring different ideas. On the other hand, cultural differences can lead to misunderstandings if we are not careful. For example, in some cultures people value being direct, while in others they prefer to be indirect. The key is to stay open-minded and respect each other's customs.

**中文译文**

在当今全球化的世界里，来自不同文化的人经常一起工作。这既让人受益，也充满挑战。一方面，多元化的团队更有创造力，因为成员带来不同的想法。另一方面，如果我们不注意，文化差异可能导致误解。例如，在一些文化中人们看重直截了当，而在另一些文化中则更喜欢委婉。关键在于保持开放的心态，尊重彼此的风俗习惯。

**重点词汇与短语**

- in today's globalized world（在当今全球化的世界里）
- on the one hand / on the other hand（一方面 / 另一方面）
- diverse teams（多元化的团队）
- lead to misunderstandings（导致误解）
- stay open-minded（保持开放的心态）

## 5. The Pros and Cons of City Life（城市生活的利弊）

**英文正文**

Living in a big city has both advantages and disadvantages. Cities offer better job opportunities, excellent schools and rich entertainment. However, city life also has its downsides. Housing is expensive, traffic is heavy, and the air is often polluted. Some people even say that cities are lonely places despite being crowded. In the end, whether city life is good for you depends on your personality and your goals. There is no single answer that fits everyone.

**中文译文**

住在大城市既有优点也有缺点。城市提供更好的工作机会、优秀的学校和丰富的娱乐。然而，城市生活也有其弊端。住房昂贵，交通拥堵，空气常常受到污染。有些人甚至说，城市虽然拥挤，却是孤独的地方。归根结底，城市生活是否适合你，取决于你的性格和目标。没有放之四海而皆准的答案。

**重点词汇与短语**

- advantages and disadvantages（优点和缺点）
- job opportunities（工作机会）
- have its downsides（有其弊端）
- despite being crowded（尽管很拥挤）
- in the end（归根结底）
