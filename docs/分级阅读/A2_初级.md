# A2 分级阅读 · 初级

> 对应 CEFR A2（约 1,000–1,500 词）。短文开始使用过去时、将来时和简单连接词，适合初级学习者背诵，积累成段表达。

## 1. My Weekend（我的周末）

**英文正文**

Last weekend I went to the park with my friends. The weather was nice, so we had a picnic there. We brought some sandwiches, fruit and drinks. We played games and took many photos. In the afternoon, we went for a walk by the lake. It was a wonderful day. I really enjoyed it.

**中文译文**

上周末我和朋友们去了公园。天气很好，所以我们在那里野餐。我们带了三明治、水果和饮料。我们玩游戏，拍了很多照片。下午，我们在湖边散步。那是美好的一天，我玩得非常开心。

**重点词汇与短语**

- go for a picnic / have a picnic（野餐）
- take photos（拍照）
- go for a walk（散步）
- by the lake（在湖边）
- enjoy（享受；玩得开心）

## 2. A Trip to the City（城市之旅）

**英文正文**

Yesterday I took a trip to the city with my family. We went there by train. First, we visited a museum. It was big and interesting. Then we had lunch at a small restaurant. The food was delicious. After that, we went shopping and bought some clothes. We came home late in the evening. We were tired but happy.

**中文译文**

昨天我和家人去城里旅行。我们坐火车去的。我们先参观了一座博物馆，它又大又有趣。然后我们在一家小餐馆吃了午饭，食物很美味。之后我们去购物，买了一些衣服。晚上我们很晚才回家。我们很累，但很开心。

**重点词汇与短语**

- take a trip（去旅行）
- by train（乘火车）
- visit a museum（参观博物馆）
- go shopping（去购物）
- tired but happy（又累又开心）

## 3. My Best Friend（我最好的朋友）

**英文正文**

My best friend is Lily. We are in the same class. She is kind and funny. She always helps me when I have problems. We both like playing badminton, so we play together every weekend. Sometimes we watch movies at her home. I am very happy to have a friend like her.

**中文译文**

我最好的朋友是莉莉。我们在同一个班。她又善良又有趣。我有困难时她总是帮助我。我们都喜欢打羽毛球，所以每个周末都一起玩。有时我们在她家看电影。能有她这样的朋友，我非常开心。

**重点词汇与短语**

- the same class（同一个班）
- kind and funny（又善良又有趣）
- have problems（遇到困难）
- both like...（都喜欢……）
- like her（像她这样）

## 4. Shopping（购物）

**英文正文**

I like shopping, but I do not like spending too much money. When I go shopping, I always make a list first. I buy what I need, not what I want. This week, I need to buy a new pair of shoes. The old ones are too small for me. I will look for a pair on sale.

**中文译文**

我喜欢购物，但我不喜欢花太多钱。购物时我总是先列一个清单。我买我需要的东西，而不是我想要的东西。这周我需要买一双新鞋，旧的对我来说太小了。我会找一双打折的。

**重点词汇与短语**

- spend money（花钱）
- make a list（列清单）
- a pair of shoes（一双鞋）
- on sale（打折；特价）
- what I need / what I want（我需要的 / 我想要的）

## 5. My Daily Routine（我的日常）

**英文正文**

I usually get up early in the morning because I have classes at eight. After breakfast, I ride my bike to school. I study hard during the day. In my free time, I often read books and listen to music. I sometimes play basketball with my classmates after school. At night, I go to bed before eleven.

**中文译文**

我通常早上起得很早，因为我八点有课。吃完早餐后，我骑自行车去学校。白天我努力学习。空闲时，我经常读书、听音乐。放学后我有时和同学打篮球。晚上我十一点前睡觉。

**重点词汇与短语**

- get up early（早起）
- ride a bike（骑自行车）
- in my free time（在我的空闲时间）
- play basketball（打篮球）
- before eleven（十一点前）
