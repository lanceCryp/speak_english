# A2 分级阅读 · 初级

> 对应 CEFR A2（约 1,000–1,500 词）。100 篇短文使用一般过去时、将来时、比较级和简单连接词，适合初级学习者背诵，积累成段表达。每篇标注核心语法点，并附语法例句。

## 一、日常生活

### 1. My Daily Routine（我的日常）

I usually get up early in the morning. After breakfast, I ride my bike to school. I study hard during the day. In my free time, I often read books and listen to music. At night, I go to bed before eleven.

我通常早上起得很早。早餐后我骑自行车去学校。白天我努力学习。空闲时我经常读书、听音乐。晚上我十一点前睡觉。

**词与短语**：get up early（早起）、ride a bike（骑自行车）、in my free time（空闲时）

**语法点睛**：**频度副词 usually / often** —— usually、often 放在实义动词前，表示动作发生的频率。

**语法示例**：
- I usually have breakfast at seven.（我通常七点吃早餐。）
- She often goes to the library after school.（她经常放学后去图书馆。）

### 2. My Weekend（我的周末）

Last weekend I went to the park with my friends. The weather was nice, so we had a picnic. We brought sandwiches, fruit and drinks. We played games and took many photos. It was a wonderful day.

上周末我和朋友们去了公园。天气很好，所以我们野餐了。我们带了三明治、水果和饮料，玩游戏、拍了很多照片。那是美好的一天。

**词与短语**：have a picnic（野餐）、take photos（拍照）、a wonderful day（美好的一天）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（不规则动词）** —— went、had、brought、took 是 go、have、bring、take 的过去式，表示过去发生的事。

**语法示例**：
- I went to school by bike yesterday.（我昨天骑车去上学。）
- We had a good time last night.（我们昨晚玩得很开心。）

### 3. A Busy Day（忙碌的一天）

Yesterday was a busy day. I got up at six. I cleaned my room in the morning. In the afternoon, I went shopping with my mother. In the evening, I did my homework. I was tired but happy.

昨天是忙碌的一天。我六点起床，早上打扫房间，下午和妈妈去购物，晚上做作业。我又累又开心。

**词与短语**：clean my room（打扫房间）、go shopping（去购物）、tired but happy（又累又开心）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（规则动词加 -ed）** —— clean → cleaned、play → played，规则动词过去式直接加 -ed。

**语法示例**：
- I cleaned my room last Sunday.（我上周日打扫了房间。）
- She played the piano yesterday.（她昨天弹了钢琴。）

### 4. My Free Time（我的空闲时间）

In my free time, I like doing many things. I often play basketball with my classmates. Sometimes I draw pictures at home. When the weather is good, I go for a walk in the park. I never feel bored.

空闲时我喜欢做很多事。我经常和同学打篮球，有时在家画画。天气好时，我去公园散步。我从不觉得无聊。

**词与短语**：do many things（做很多事）、play basketball（打篮球）、feel bored（感到无聊）

**语法点睛**：**when 引导的时间状语从句** —— when 引导从句表示"当……的时候"，说明主句动作发生的时间。

**语法示例**：
- When it rains, I stay at home.（下雨时，我就待在家里。）
- When I am free, I read books.（我有空时读书。）

### 5. My Habits（我的习惯）

I have some good habits. I get up early every day. I drink a lot of water. I wash my hands before meals. But I also have a bad habit. I go to bed too late sometimes. I should change it.

我有一些好习惯。我每天早起，喝很多水，饭前洗手。但我也有个坏习惯——有时睡得太晚。我应该改掉它。

**词与短语**：good habits（好习惯）、before meals（饭前）、a bad habit（坏习惯）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 should（应该）** —— should + 动词原形，表示"应该做某事"，用于提出建议。

**语法示例**：
- You should go to bed early.（你应该早点睡觉。）
- We should eat more vegetables.（我们应该多吃蔬菜。）

## 二、过去经历

### 6. A Trip（一次旅行）

Last month, I took a trip to the mountains with my family. We went there by car. The mountains were beautiful. We climbed and took many pictures. We came back home in the evening. I really enjoyed the trip.

上个月，我和家人去山里旅行。我们开车去的。山很美，我们爬山、拍了很多照片。晚上我们回到家。我非常喜欢这次旅行。

**词与短语**：take a trip（去旅行）、by car（开车）、enjoy the trip（享受旅行）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（不规则动词）** —— took、went、climbed、came 是 take、go、climb、come 的过去式，叙述过去的旅行。

**语法示例**：
- I took a lot of pictures on the trip.（旅行中我拍了很多照片。）
- We came back home at night.（我们晚上回到家。）

### 7. My Birthday（我的生日）

My birthday was last Sunday. My parents gave me a new bike as a gift. My friends came to my home. We ate a big cake and sang songs. I got many presents. It was my best birthday.

我的生日是上周日。父母送我一辆新自行车作礼物。朋友们来到我家，我们吃了大蛋糕、唱了歌。我收到了很多礼物。那是我最棒的生日。

**词与短语**：as a gift（作为礼物）、a big cake（大蛋糕）、many presents（很多礼物）

**语法点睛**：**give + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语** —— gave me a new bike，me 是间接宾语，a new bike 是直接宾语。

**语法示例**：
- My mother gave me a book.（妈妈给了我一本书。）
- He gave his friend a gift.（他送给朋友一件礼物。）

### 8. Last Summer（去年夏天）

Last summer, I visited my grandparents in the countryside. I stayed there for two weeks. I helped them pick fruit and feed the chickens. In the evening, we sat outside and talked. I had a great time.

去年夏天，我去乡下看望祖父母。我在那里待了两周，帮他们摘水果、喂鸡。晚上我们坐在外面聊天。我玩得很开心。

**词与短语**：in the countryside（在乡下）、pick fruit（摘水果）、have a great time（玩得开心）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时 + for + 时间段** —— stayed there for two weeks 表示"在那里待了两周"，for 后接时间段。

**语法示例**：
- I stayed in Beijing for three days.（我在北京待了三天。）
- She studied English for two hours.（她学了两小时英语。）

### 9. A School Day（一个上学日）

Yesterday was a normal school day. I had four classes in the morning and two in the afternoon. My favorite class was English. After school, I played football with my friends. Then I went home and had dinner.

昨天是普通的上学日。上午我上了四节课，下午两节。我最喜欢的课是英语。放学后我和朋友踢足球，然后回家吃晚饭。

**词与短语**：a normal day（普通的一天）、favorite class（最喜欢的课）、play football（踢足球）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（had / was）** —— had 是 have 的过去式，was 是 is/am 的过去式，描述过去的一天。

**语法示例**：
- I had three classes yesterday.（我昨天上了三节课。）
- It was a sunny day.（那是一个晴天。）

### 10. A Funny Story（一个有趣的故事）

Last week, something funny happened. I was walking home when a small dog ran after me. I was afraid, so I ran faster. Then the dog stopped and looked at me. It just wanted to play. We all laughed.

上周发生了一件有趣的事。我走回家时，一只小狗追着我跑。我害怕，就跑得更快。然后狗停下来看着我——它只是想玩。我们都笑了。

**词与短语**：something funny（有趣的事）、run after（追赶）、just wanted to（只是想要）

**语法点睛**：**过去进行时（was walking）** —— was/were + 动词-ing，表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作，常与 when 连用。

**语法示例**：
- I was reading when he came in.（他进来时我正在读书。）
- She was cooking when the phone rang.（电话响时她正在做饭。）

## 三、计划与未来

### 11. My Plan（我的计划）

I have a plan for this weekend. On Saturday morning, I will finish my homework. In the afternoon, I am going to visit my grandparents. On Sunday, I will go to the cinema with my friends. It will be a nice weekend.

我有个周末计划。周六早上完成作业，下午去看望祖父母。周日和朋友们去看电影。这会是个美好的周末。

**词与短语**：have a plan（有计划）、finish homework（完成作业）、go to the cinema（去看电影）

**语法点睛**：**一般将来时 will + 动词原形** —— will 表示将要发生的动作，后接动词原形。

**语法示例**：
- I will go to the park tomorrow.（我明天要去公园。）
- She will visit her aunt next week.（她下周要去看望姨妈。）

### 12. Next Week（下周）

Next week will be busy for me. I will have an English test on Tuesday. I need to study for it. On Friday, our class will have a party. I am going to sing a song. I feel a little nervous but excited.

下周我会很忙。周二有英语考试，我需要复习。周五我们班有聚会，我要唱一首歌。我有点紧张，但很兴奋。

**词与短语**：an English test（英语考试）、study for（为……学习）、nervous but excited（紧张但兴奋）

**语法点睛**：**be going to + 动词原形（打算做）** —— be going to 表示计划、打算做某事，比 will 更强调计划性。

**语法示例**：
- I am going to visit my friend this weekend.（我打算这个周末去看朋友。）
- They are going to have a picnic.（他们打算去野餐。）

### 13. My Future（我的未来）

In the future, I want to be a teacher. I love children and I like helping people. To reach my dream, I must study hard now. I hope my dream will come true one day.

将来我想成为一名老师。我喜欢孩子，也喜欢帮助别人。为了实现梦想，我现在必须努力学习。我希望梦想有一天能实现。

**词与短语**：in the future（将来）、reach my dream（实现梦想）、come true（实现）

**语法点睛**：**不定式表目的（to reach my dream）** —— to + 动词原形放在句首，表示"为了……"。

**语法示例**：
- To learn English well, I practice every day.（为了学好英语，我每天练习。）
- To stay healthy, I do sports.（为了保持健康，我运动。）

### 14. Going on Holiday（去度假）

This summer, I am going on holiday to the beach with my family. We will swim in the sea and build sandcastles. We will stay there for a week. I am really looking forward to it.

今年夏天，我要和家人去海边度假。我们会在海里游泳、堆沙堡，在那里待一周。我非常期待。

**词与短语**：go on holiday（去度假）、build sandcastles（堆沙堡）、look forward to（期待）

**语法点睛**：**look forward to + 名词/动名词（期待）** —— look forward to 后接名词或动名词，表示"期待做某事"。

**语法示例**：
- I am looking forward to the summer holiday.（我期待暑假。）
- She is looking forward to meeting you.（她期待见到你。）

### 15. A Party（一个聚会）

Next month, my cousin will get married. There will be a big party. I will wear my new dress. We will eat good food and dance. I think it will be a lot of fun.

下个月，我表哥要结婚了，会有一场盛大的聚会。我会穿我的新裙子。我们会吃美食、跳舞。我想那会很好玩。

**词与短语**：get married（结婚）、a big party（盛大的聚会）、a lot of fun（很好玩）

**语法点睛**：**There will be（将会有）** —— There be 句型的将来时，表示"将来会有……"。

**语法示例**：
- There will be a meeting tomorrow.（明天会有一场会议。）
- There will be many people at the party.（聚会上会有很多人。）

## 四、人物与关系

### 16. My Best Friend（我最好的朋友）

My best friend is Lily. We are in the same class. She is kind and funny. She always helps me when I have problems. We both like playing badminton, so we play together every weekend. I am happy to have a friend like her.

我最好的朋友是莉莉。我们在同一个班。她又善良又有趣，我有困难时她总是帮助我。我们都喜欢打羽毛球，所以每个周末一起玩。能有她这样的朋友我很开心。

**词与短语**：the same class（同一个班）、have problems（遇到困难）、both like（都喜欢）

**语法点睛**：**so 引导的结果从句** —— so 连接两个句子，表示"所以、因此"，引出结果。

**语法示例**：
- It was raining, so I stayed at home.（下雨了，所以我待在家里。）
- She was tired, so she went to bed early.（她累了，所以早早上床。）

### 17. My Neighbor（我的邻居）

My neighbor is an old man. His name is Mr. Chen. He is very kind. He often gives me fruit from his garden. Sometimes I help him carry things. We get along very well.

我的邻居是一位老人，姓陈。他非常和善，经常把花园里的水果给我。有时我帮他搬东西。我们相处得很好。

**词与短语**：an old man（一位老人）、from his garden（从他的花园）、get along well（相处得好）

**语法点睛**：**频度副词 often / sometimes** —— often（经常）、sometimes（有时），都放在实义动词前。

**语法示例**：
- He often helps his neighbors.（他经常帮助邻居。）
- We sometimes play chess together.（我们有时一起下棋。）

### 18. My Cousin（我的表弟）

My cousin Jack is younger than me. He is ten years old. He lives in another city, but we talk on the phone every week. During the holidays, we play games together. He is like my little brother.

我的表弟杰克比我小，他十岁。他住在另一个城市，但我们每周通电话。假期里我们一起玩游戏。他就像我的亲弟弟。

**词与短语**：younger than（比……小）、on the phone（通过电话）、like my brother（像我的弟弟）

**语法点睛**：**形容词比较级 + than（比……更……）** —— younger than me 表示"比我小"，比较级后接 than。

**语法示例**：
- My sister is taller than me.（我妹妹比我高。）
- This book is more interesting than that one.（这本书比那本更有趣。）

### 19. A New Friend（一个新朋友）

Last month, a new student came to our class. Her name is Mary. She is from another city. At first, she was shy. I helped her with her lessons. Now we are good friends.

上个月，一个新同学来到我们班，她叫玛丽，来自另一个城市。起初她很害羞。我帮她补习功课。现在我们成了好朋友。

**词与短语**：a new student（新同学）、at first（起初）、good friends（好朋友）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（叙述过去事件）** —— came、was、helped 是 come、is、help 的过去式，讲述上个月发生的事。

**语法示例**：
- A new teacher came to our school last year.（去年一位新老师来到我们学校。）
- At first, I was nervous.（起初我很紧张。）

### 20. My Idol（我的偶像）

My idol is a famous basketball player. He is tall and strong. He works very hard and never gives up. I watch his games on TV. I want to be like him one day.

我的偶像是一位著名篮球运动员。他又高又壮，非常努力、从不放弃。我在电视上看他的比赛。我希望有一天能像他一样。

**词与短语**：a famous player（著名运动员）、never give up（从不放弃）、be like him（像他一样）

**语法点睛**：**一般现在时第三人称单数（works, gives）** —— 主语 he 是第三人称单数，动词加 -s。

**语法示例**：
- He plays basketball very well.（他篮球打得很好。）
- She works hard every day.（她每天努力工作。）

## 五、购物与餐饮

### 21. Shopping（购物）

I like shopping, but I do not like spending too much money. When I go shopping, I always make a list first. I buy what I need, not what I want. This week, I need to buy a new pair of shoes.

我喜欢购物，但不喜欢花太多钱。购物时我总是先列清单，买我需要的而不是想要的。这周我需要买一双新鞋。

**词与短语**：spend money（花钱）、make a list（列清单）、a pair of shoes（一双鞋）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作宾语（spending）** —— like spending 中 spending 是动名词，作 like 的宾语，表示"花钱"。

**语法示例**：
- I don't like wasting time.（我不喜欢浪费时间。）
- She enjoys reading books.（她喜欢读书。）

### 22. At the Market（在集市）

On Sunday morning, my mother and I go to the market. The market is very busy. We buy fresh vegetables, fruit and fish. My mother can talk about the price. I help her carry the bags.

周日早上，我和妈妈去集市。集市很热闹。我们买新鲜的蔬菜、水果和鱼。妈妈会讨价还价，我帮她提袋子。

**词与短语**：in the morning（在早上）、fresh vegetables（新鲜蔬菜）、carry the bags（提袋子）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can（能力）** —— can + 动词原形，表示"能、会"，如 can talk about（会谈论）。

**语法示例**：
- My mother can cook well.（我妈妈做饭很好吃。）
- I can help you carry the bags.（我可以帮你提袋子。）

### 23. Eating Out（外出就餐）

Sometimes my family eats out. Last Friday, we went to a new restaurant. The food was delicious but a little expensive. We ordered fish, chicken and soup. We all enjoyed the meal.

有时我们家外出就餐。上周五我们去了一家新餐厅。食物很美味，但有点贵。我们点了鱼、鸡肉和汤，都很喜欢这顿饭。

**词与短语**：eat out（外出就餐）、a little expensive（有点贵）、enjoy the meal（享受这顿饭）

**语法点睛**：**转折连词 but（复现）** —— delicious but a little expensive 表示"美味但有点贵"，but 表转折。

**语法示例**：
- The food is cheap but delicious.（食物便宜但美味。）
- The room is small but comfortable.（房间小但舒适。）

### 24. My Favorite Café（我最喜欢的咖啡馆）

Near my school there is a small café. I go there with my friends after school. I like its milk tea best. The café is quiet, so we can do our homework there. It is a nice place to relax.

学校附近有一家小咖啡馆。放学后我和朋友们去那里。我最喜欢它的奶茶。咖啡馆很安静，我们可以在那里做作业。这是个放松的好地方。

**词与短语**：near my school（学校附近）、milk tea（奶茶）、a place to relax（放松的地方）

**语法点睛**：**最高级 like...best（最喜欢）** —— like something best 表示"最喜欢……"，等同于 like something the most。

**语法示例**：
- I like English best.（我最喜欢英语。）
- She likes summer best of all the seasons.（四季中她最喜欢夏天。）

### 25. Ordering Food（点餐）

Yesterday I went to a fast food restaurant. I ordered a hamburger and some fries. I also bought a cup of cola. The food was not very healthy, but it was quick and tasty.

昨天我去了快餐店。我点了一个汉堡和一些薯条，还买了一杯可乐。这些食物不太健康，但很快捷、好吃。

**词与短语**：order food（点餐）、a cup of cola（一杯可乐）、quick and tasty（快捷又好吃）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（规则 + 不规则混合）** —— ordered、bought 是 order、buy 的过去式，叙述昨天的事。

**语法示例**：
- I ordered a cup of milk tea yesterday.（我昨天点了一杯奶茶。）
- She bought a new dress last week.（她上周买了一条新裙子。）

## 六、出行与交通

### 26. By Bus（乘公交车）

I go to school by bus every day. The bus stop is near my home. I usually wait for five minutes. The bus is sometimes crowded. I listen to music on the bus. It takes me twenty minutes to get to school.

我每天坐公交车上学。公交站离我家很近，我通常等五分钟。车上有时很挤。我在车上听音乐。到学校要二十分钟。

**词与短语**：the bus stop（公交站）、crowded（拥挤的）、get to school（到学校）

**语法点睛**：**It takes + 人 + 时间 + to do（花费时间）** —— It takes me twenty minutes to... 表示"做某事花了我二十分钟"。

**语法示例**：
- It takes me ten minutes to walk to school.（我步行到学校要十分钟。）
- It takes her an hour to finish her homework.（她花一小时完成作业。）

### 27. Taking a Train（坐火车）

Last month, I took a train to visit my aunt. It was my first time on a fast train. The train was fast and comfortable. I looked out of the window and saw beautiful fields. The trip took two hours.

上个月我坐火车去看姨妈。那是我第一次坐高铁。火车又快又舒服。我望着窗外，看到了美丽的田野。行程用了两个小时。

**词与短语**：a fast train（高铁）、look out of the window（望向窗外）、beautiful fields（美丽的田野）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时 + It was my first time** —— was 表示过去状态，my first time 表示"我第一次"。

**语法示例**：
- It was my first time to see the sea.（那是我第一次看海。）
- The trip took three hours.（行程花了三小时。）

### 28. Going to the Airport（去机场）

My uncle is coming back from another country. My family will go to the airport to meet him. We will go there by car. I am very excited. I will hold a sign with his name.

我叔叔要从国外回来。我们一家要去机场接他，开车去。我非常兴奋，我会举着一个写着他名字的牌子。

**词与短语**：the airport（机场）、meet him（接他）、a sign（牌子）

**语法点睛**：**不定式表目的（to meet him）** —— go to the airport to meet him，to meet 表示"去机场"的目的。

**语法示例**：
- I go to the library to borrow books.（我去图书馆借书。）
- She came here to see me.（她来这里看我。）

### 29. Asking the Way（问路）

Yesterday I could not find the library. I asked a man for help. He was very kind. He told me to go straight and turn left at the second corner. I followed his words and found it easily.

昨天我找不到图书馆，就向一位男士求助。他很友善，告诉我直走，在第二个拐角左转。我照他说的走，很容易就找到了。

**词与短语**：ask for help（求助）、go straight（直走）、turn left（左转）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 could 的否定（could not）** —— could not（couldn't）是 can 的过去式否定，表示"过去不能"。

**语法示例**：
- I could not find my key yesterday.（我昨天找不到钥匙。）
- She could not come to the party.（她没能来参加聚会。）

### 30. A Short Trip（一次短途旅行）

On Saturday, my friends and I went on a short trip to a nearby lake. We rode our bikes there. We had a picnic by the lake and rode back in the afternoon. It was a tiring but happy day.

周六，我和朋友们去附近的湖做了一次短途旅行。我们骑自行车去的，在湖边野餐，下午骑车返回。那是又累又开心的一天。

**词与短语**：a short trip（短途旅行）、by the lake（在湖边）、ride back（骑回来）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（rode）** —— rode 是 ride 的过去式（不规则），表示"骑"。

**语法示例**：
- I rode my bike to the park yesterday.（我昨天骑车去公园。）
- We rode to the lake last Sunday.（我们上周日骑车去湖边。）

## 七、健康与运动

### 31. My Health（我的健康）

I think I am healthy. I do sports every day. I eat fruit and vegetables. I sleep eight hours every night. I do not eat too much junk food. Keeping healthy is important to me.

我觉得我很健康。我每天运动，吃水果和蔬菜，每晚睡八小时。我不吃太多垃圾食品。保持健康对我来说很重要。

**词与短语**：do sports（做运动）、junk food（垃圾食品）、keep healthy（保持健康）

**语法点睛**：**动名词作主语（Keeping healthy）** —— Keeping healthy is important，动名词短语作句子主语。

**语法示例**：
- Keeping healthy is important to everyone.（保持健康对每个人都很重要。）
- Doing sports every day is good for you.（每天运动对你有好处。）

### 32. Doing Exercise（锻炼）

Doing exercise is good for us. I run in the park every morning. Running makes me strong. My father swims twice a week. My mother does yoga at home. Exercise keeps our family healthy.

锻炼对我们有好处。我每天早上在公园跑步，跑步让我强壮。我爸爸每周游两次泳，妈妈在家做瑜伽。锻炼让我们全家保持健康。

**词与短语**：be good for（对……有好处）、twice a week（每周两次）、do yoga（做瑜伽）

**语法点睛**：**频度表达 twice a week（每周两次）** —— once/twice/three times + a week，表示频率。

**语法示例**：
- I swim twice a week.（我每周游两次泳。）
- She goes to the gym three times a week.（她每周去三次健身房。）

### 33. A Healthy Diet（健康的饮食）

A healthy diet is very important. I eat breakfast every day. I eat less sugar and less salt. I drink enough water. I also eat a lot of fruit. A good diet gives me energy.

健康的饮食非常重要。我每天吃早餐，少吃糖和盐，喝足够的水，还吃很多水果。好的饮食给我能量。

**词与短语**：a healthy diet（健康的饮食）、less sugar（更少的糖）、enough water（足够的水）

**语法点睛**：**less + 不可数名词（更少的）** —— less sugar、less salt 表示"更少的糖、更少的盐"，用于不可数名词。

**语法示例**：
- You should eat less sugar.（你应该少吃糖。）
- I drink less cola now.（我现在喝更少的可乐。）

### 34. Seeing a Doctor（看医生）

Last week I had a bad cold. I had a headache and a fever. My mother took me to see a doctor. The doctor said I should rest and drink more water. I took some medicine. Now I feel better.

上周我得了重感冒，头痛发烧。妈妈带我去看医生。医生说我要多休息、多喝水。我吃了些药，现在感觉好多了。

**词与短语**：have a cold（感冒）、see a doctor（看医生）、feel better（感觉好些）

**语法点睛**：**间接引语（said I should...）** —— The doctor said I should rest，用 said 转述医生的话，should 表示建议。

**语法示例**：
- My mother said I should study harder.（妈妈说我要更努力学习。）
- The teacher said we should read more books.（老师让我们多读书。）

### 35. Good Sleep（良好的睡眠）

Good sleep is important for us. I go to bed before ten o'clock. I do not use my phone in bed. My room is quiet and dark. When I sleep well, I feel great the next day.

良好的睡眠对我们很重要。我十点前睡觉，不在床上用手机。我的房间安静又黑暗。睡得好时，第二天我感觉很棒。

**词与短语**：good sleep（良好的睡眠）、use my phone（用手机）、feel great（感觉很棒）

**语法点睛**：**when 引导时间状语从句（复现）** —— When I sleep well, I feel great，when 从句在前，主句在后。

**语法示例**：
- When I am tired, I go to bed early.（我累的时候，就早点睡。）
- When it is sunny, we go out to play.（天气晴朗时，我们就出去玩。）

## 八、学习与工作

### 36. My Studies（我的学习）

I am a student in a middle school. I study many subjects, like Chinese, math and English. I like English best. I spend two hours on my homework every day. I want to get good grades.

我是一名中学生。我学习很多科目，如语文、数学和英语。我最喜欢英语。我每天花两小时做作业，我想取得好成绩。

**词与短语**：a middle school（中学）、like...best（最喜欢）、good grades（好成绩）

**语法点睛**：**spend + 时间 + on + 名词（花费时间）** —— spend two hours on my homework，表示"在作业上花两小时"。

**语法示例**：
- I spend one hour on reading every day.（我每天花一小时阅读。）
- She spends a lot of time on her studies.（她在学习上花很多时间。）

### 37. Learning English（学英语）

Learning English is fun but not easy. I practice speaking every day. I watch English videos. I keep a word notebook. When I meet a new word, I write it down. Practice makes perfect.

学英语很有趣但不容易。我每天练习口语，看英语视频，还准备了一个单词本。遇到新词就记下来。熟能生巧。

**词与短语**：practice speaking（练习口语）、a word notebook（单词本）、write down（记下来）

**语法点睛**：**practice + 动名词（practice speaking）** —— practice 后必须接动名词，表示"练习做某事"。

**语法示例**：
- I practice writing every day.（我每天练习写字。）
- She practices playing the piano.（她练习弹钢琴。）

### 38. My Homework（我的作业）

I usually do my homework after dinner. I do it in my room. I am careful and do not rush. If I have a question, I ask my sister. She is good at math. I finish my homework before nine.

我通常晚饭后做作业，在我的房间里。我认真仔细、不着急。有不会的就问姐姐，她数学很好。我九点前完成作业。

**词与短语**：after dinner（晚饭后）、be good at（擅长）、finish homework（完成作业）

**语法点睛**：**if 引导的条件状语从句** —— If I have a question, I ask my sister，表示"如果……就……"。

**语法示例**：
- If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.（如果明天下雨，我就待在家里。）
- If you need help, you can call me.（如果你需要帮助，可以打电话给我。）

### 39. A Part-time Job（一份兼职）

Last summer, I had a part-time job. I worked in a small bookshop. I helped put books on the shelves. I met many interesting people. I also earned some money. It was a good experience.

去年夏天，我做了一份兼职，在一家小书店工作。我帮忙把书放到书架上，认识了很多有趣的人，还赚了一些钱。这是一次很好的经历。

**词与短语**：a part-time job（兼职）、on the shelves（在书架上）、earn money（赚钱）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（worked, helped, met, earned）** —— 叙述去年夏天的兼职经历，全部用过去时。

**语法示例**：
- I worked in a café last summer.（去年夏天我在一家咖啡馆工作。）
- She earned some money during the holiday.（假期里她赚了些钱。）

### 40. My Future Job（我未来的工作）

I am thinking about my future job. I want a job that I love. I like helping people, so maybe I will be a doctor or a teacher. No matter what, I will work hard and do my best.

我在思考未来的工作。我想要一份自己热爱的工作。我喜欢帮助别人，所以也许会当医生或老师。不管怎样，我都会努力工作、尽力而为。

**词与短语**：future job（未来的工作）、no matter what（不管怎样）、do my best（尽力而为）

**语法点睛**：**定语从句（a job that I love）** —— that 引导定语从句修饰 a job，表示"我热爱的工作"。

**语法示例**：
- I want a book that is interesting.（我想要一本有趣的书。）
- She has a friend that lives in Beijing.（她有一个住在北京的朋友。）

## 九、天气与自然

### 41. The Seasons（季节）

There are four seasons in a year. Spring is warm, summer is hot, autumn is cool, and winter is cold. Each season has its own beauty. My favorite season is autumn, because it is cool and comfortable.

一年有四季。春天温暖，夏天炎热，秋天凉爽，冬天寒冷。每个季节都有它的美。我最喜欢秋天，因为它凉爽舒适。

**词与短语**：four seasons（四季）、its own beauty（它自己的美）、comfortable（舒适的）

**语法点睛**：**because 引导原因状语从句** —— because 说明原因，回答 why 的问题。

**语法示例**：
- I like spring because it is warm.（我喜欢春天，因为温暖。）
- She stays at home because it is raining.（她待在家，因为下雨了。）

### 42. A Rainy Day（一个雨天）

Yesterday was a rainy day. It rained all day. I could not go out to play. So I stayed at home, read a book and listened to the rain. The sound of rain was relaxing. I enjoyed the quiet day.

昨天是个雨天，下了一整天雨。我不能出去玩，就待在家里看书、听雨。雨声让人放松，我享受这安静的一天。

**词与短语**：all day（一整天）、stay at home（待在家）、relaxing（令人放松的）

**语法点睛**：**so 引导结果（复现）** —— 前句说不能出去玩，后句用 So 引出"所以我就待在家"。

**语法示例**：
- I was hungry, so I ate some bread.（我饿了，所以吃了点面包。）
- The movie was boring, so I left early.（电影很无聊，所以我提前走了。）

### 43. The Beach（海滩）

I love the beach. Last summer, my family went to the beach. The sand was soft and the water was clear. We swam, played in the sand and collected shells. I want to go there again.

我爱海滩。去年夏天，我们一家去了海滩。沙子柔软，海水清澈。我们游泳、玩沙、捡贝壳。我想再去那里。

**词与短语**：the sand（沙子）、collect shells（捡贝壳）、go there again（再去那里）

**语法点睛**：**并列谓语动词（swam, played, collected）** —— 用逗号和 and 连接三个过去式动词，表示连续的动作。

**语法示例**：
- We sang, danced and had a good time.（我们唱歌、跳舞、玩得很开心。）
- She washed the dishes, cleaned the room and went to bed.（她洗碗、打扫房间，然后去睡觉。）

### 44. Mountains（山）

I live near some mountains. The mountains are green in spring. Many people climb them on weekends. The air on the mountains is fresh. From the top, you can see the whole town. It is a beautiful view.

我住在几座山附近。春天山是绿色的。很多人周末去爬山。山上的空气清新。从山顶可以看到整个城镇，景色很美。

**词与短语**：climb mountains（爬山）、fresh air（清新的空气）、a beautiful view（美丽的景色）

**语法点睛**：**介词短语 From the top（从山顶）** —— from + 地点表示"从某处"，放在句首表视角。

**语法示例**：
- From the window, I can see the garden.（从窗户我能看到花园。）
- From the top, the city looks small.（从山顶看，城市显得很小。）

### 45. The Countryside（乡村）

My grandparents live in the countryside. There are green fields and small rivers. The air is fresh there. In summer, I help them in the fields. Life in the countryside is quiet and peaceful.

我的祖父母住在乡村。那里有绿色的田野和小河，空气清新。夏天我帮他们干农活。乡村的生活安静又平和。

**词与短语**：green fields（绿色田野）、small rivers（小河）、quiet and peaceful（安静平和）

**语法点睛**：**there be 句型（复现）** —— There are green fields and small rivers，表示"有绿色的田野和小河"。

**语法示例**：
- There are many trees in the countryside.（乡村里有很多树。）
- There is a river near the village.（村庄附近有一条河。）

## 十、情感与感受

### 46. A Happy Day（开心的一天）

Last Saturday was a happy day. My best friend came to my home. We played games, watched a funny movie and ate snacks together. We laughed a lot. I hope we can meet again soon.

上周六是开心的一天。我最好的朋友来我家。我们一起玩游戏、看搞笑电影、吃零食，笑了很多。我希望我们能很快再见面。

**词与短语**：a funny movie（搞笑电影）、eat snacks（吃零食）、laugh a lot（笑很多）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时 + 并列动作** —— played、watched、ate 三个过去式动词用 and 连接，叙述开心的一天。

**语法示例**：
- We talked, laughed and played games all day.（我们整天聊天、欢笑、玩游戏。）
- I read a book, drank tea and rested at home.（我在家看书、喝茶、休息。）

### 47. A Sad Day（难过的一天）

Last week I had a sad day. My pet bird flew away. I looked for it everywhere, but I could not find it. I felt very sad. My mother comforted me. I still miss my little bird.

上周我度过了难过的一天。我的宠物鸟飞走了。我到处找，但没找到。我非常难过。妈妈安慰了我。我仍然想念我的小鸟。

**词与短语**：fly away（飞走）、look for（寻找）、miss my bird（想念我的鸟）

**语法点睛**：**but 表转折 + could not（复现）** —— looked for it everywhere, but I could not find it，转折表"找遍了却找不到"。

**语法示例**：
- I looked for my key everywhere, but I could not find it.（我到处找钥匙，但找不到。）
- She tried hard, but she failed the test.（她很努力，但考试没及格。）

### 48. Feeling Tired（感到疲惫）

Sometimes I feel tired after a long day. My eyes are heavy and my legs are sore. When I feel tired, I take a hot shower and rest for a while. A good rest makes me feel new again.

有时漫长的一天过后，我感到疲惫。我的眼睛沉重，腿也酸痛。感到累时，我洗个热水澡、休息一会儿。好好休息让我重新焕发活力。

**词与短语**：a long day（漫长的一天）、take a hot shower（洗热水澡）、feel new（焕然一新）

**语法点睛**：**make + 宾语 + 动词原形（make me feel）** —— make me feel new 表示"让我感觉焕然一新"。

**语法示例**：
- Music makes me feel happy.（音乐让我感到快乐。）
- The story makes me laugh.（这个故事让我发笑。）

### 49. My Fears（我的恐惧）

Everyone has fears. When I was young, I was afraid of the dark. Now I am afraid of speaking in front of many people. But I am trying to be braver. I practice speaking every week.

每个人都有恐惧。小时候我怕黑。现在我害怕在很多人面前讲话。但我正努力变得更勇敢，每周都练习演讲。

**词与短语**：afraid of the dark（怕黑）、in front of（在……面前）、be braver（更勇敢）

**语法点睛**：**现在进行时表正在发生的状态（I am trying）** —— am trying 表示"正在努力"，强调当前持续的状态。

**语法示例**：
- I am trying to learn English well.（我正在努力学好英语。）
- She is trying to be braver.（她正努力变得更勇敢。）

### 50. Things I Love（我热爱的事物）

There are many things I love in my life. I love my family and my friends. I love sunny days and good books. I love good food and sweet music. These things make my life warm and full.

生活中有很多我热爱的事物。我爱我的家人和朋友，爱晴天和好书，爱美食和甜美的音乐。这些让我的生活温暖而充实。

**词与短语**：things I love（我热爱的事物）、sunny days（晴天）、warm and full（温暖而充实）

**语法点睛**：**make + 宾语 + 形容词（make my life warm）** —— make something + 形容词，表示"使……变得……"。

**语法示例**：
- Reading makes my life rich.（阅读让我的生活丰富。）
- Friends make my life happy.（朋友让我的生活快乐。）

## 十一、住房与社区

### 51. My Community（我的社区）

I live in a friendly community. There are many families in it. There is a small park and a supermarket. People here help each other. I like walking in my community after dinner. It is a nice place to live.

我住在一个友好的社区。这里有很多家庭，有一个小公园和一个超市。这里的人们互相帮助。我喜欢晚饭后在社区里散步。这是个宜居的好地方。

**词与短语**：a friendly community（友好的社区）、help each other（互相帮助）、a nice place to live（宜居的好地方）

**语法点睛**：**there be 句型（单复数混用）** —— There are many families（复数）、There is a small park（单数）。

**语法示例**：
- There is a school in my community.（我的社区里有一所学校。）
- There are many trees here.（这里有很多树。）

### 52. My Neighborhood（我的街区）

My neighborhood is quiet and clean. It is near the city center. There are many shops and restaurants around. A bus stop is just five minutes away. I know many people here. I love my neighborhood.

我的街区安静又干净，靠近市中心。周围有很多商店和餐馆，公交站只要五分钟路程。我认识这里的很多人。我爱我的街区。

**词与短语**：the city center（市中心）、five minutes away（五分钟路程）、know many people（认识很多人）

**语法点睛**：**距离表达（five minutes away）** —— 表示"距离多远"用时间 + away，如 five minutes away（五分钟路程）。

**语法示例**：
- My school is ten minutes away from my home.（学校离我家十分钟路程。）
- The park is just two minutes away.（公园只要两分钟路程。）

### 53. Moving House（搬家）

Last year, my family moved to a new house. The new house is bigger than the old one. It has three bedrooms and a garden. It took us two days to move everything. Now I like my new house very much.

去年，我们一家搬到了新房子。新房子比旧房子大，有三个卧室和一个花园。我们花了两天搬家。现在我非常喜欢我的新房子。

**词与短语**：move to a new house（搬到新房子）、three bedrooms（三个卧室）、a garden（一个花园）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 bigger than（比……更大）** —— bigger 是 big 的比较级，bigger than the old one 表示"比旧的更大"。

**语法示例**：
- My new room is bigger than the old one.（我的新房间比旧的大。）
- This city is bigger than my hometown.（这座城市比我的家乡大。）

### 54. My New Home（我的新家）

My new home is on the eighth floor. It is bright and spacious. From the window, I can see a big park. My room is not very big, but it is cozy. I love my new home.

我的新家在八楼，明亮又宽敞。从窗户望去，我能看到一个很大的公园。我的房间不是很大，但很舒适。我爱我的新家。

**词与短语**：on the eighth floor（在八楼）、bright and spacious（明亮宽敞）、cozy（舒适的）

**语法点睛**：**序数词（eighth）** —— 表示楼层用序数词，eighth 是 eight 的序数词，表示"第八"。

**语法示例**：
- My office is on the fifth floor.（我的办公室在五楼。）
- She lives on the tenth floor.（她住在十楼。）

### 55. Good Neighbors（好邻居）

Good neighbors are important. My neighbors are very kind. When my family is away, they help water our plants. Sometimes we share food with each other. We should be kind to our neighbors too. Good neighbors make life better.

好邻居很重要。我的邻居非常友善。我们不在家时，他们帮忙给植物浇水。有时我们互相分享食物。我们也应该对邻居友善。好邻居让生活更美好。

**词与短语**：water the plants（给植物浇水）、share food（分享食物）、be kind to（对……友善）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 should（复现）** —— We should be kind to our neighbors，should + 动词原形表示"应该"。

**语法示例**：
- We should be kind to others.（我们应该对他人友善。）
- You should say hello to your neighbors.（你应该向邻居问好。）

## 十二、假期与旅行

### 56. My Holiday（我的假期）

Last winter holiday, I went to Harbin with my parents. It was very cold and snowy. We saw beautiful ice sculptures. We also went skating on the ice. It was my first time to see so much snow. I had a wonderful holiday.

去年寒假，我和父母去了哈尔滨。那里非常冷，还下雪。我们看到了美丽的冰雕，还在冰上滑冰。那是我第一次见到这么多雪。我度过了一个美好的假期。

**词与短语**：winter holiday（寒假）、ice sculptures（冰雕）、go skating（滑冰）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时（叙述假期）** —— went、saw、had 是 go、see、have 的过去式，讲述过去的假期。

**语法示例**：
- I went to the beach last summer.（去年夏天我去了海滩。）
- We saw many beautiful things.（我们看到了很多美丽的事物。）

### 57. Planning a Trip（计划旅行）

Next month, I am planning a trip to Xi'an. I am going to visit the old city wall. I will try the local food. I will take many photos. I am really excited about this trip. I hope it will be sunny.

下个月，我计划去西安旅行。我要去参观古城墙，尝尝当地美食，拍很多照片。我对这次旅行非常兴奋，希望天气晴朗。

**词与短语**：plan a trip（计划旅行）、the old city wall（古城墙）、local food（当地美食）

**语法点睛**：**be going to + will 并用** —— 计划用 be going to，预测/希望用 will，都表示将来。

**语法示例**：
- I am going to visit my grandparents.（我要去看望祖父母。）
- I hope it will be a nice day.（我希望会是个好天气。）

### 58. At the Hotel（在酒店）

When we travel, we stay at a hotel. Last trip, our hotel room was clean and comfortable. We could see the sea from the window. The staff were friendly. I liked the breakfast there. Staying at a hotel is fun.

我们旅行时住在酒店。上次旅行，我们的酒店房间干净又舒适，从窗户能看到大海。工作人员很友好，我喜欢那里的早餐。住酒店很有趣。

**词与短语**：stay at a hotel（住酒店）、the staff（工作人员）、see the sea（看海）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 could（过去能力，复现）** —— We could see the sea，could 表示过去"能够"。

**语法示例**：
- From my room, I could see the mountains.（从我的房间能看到山。）
- We could hear the sea at night.（晚上我们能听到海声。）

### 59. Traveling by Plane（坐飞机旅行）

Last year, I traveled by plane for the first time. I was a little nervous at first. The plane took off and flew high above the clouds. The view was amazing. The flight took two hours. Now I like flying.

去年，我第一次坐飞机旅行。起初我有点紧张。飞机起飞后，飞到了云层之上。景色太美了。飞行用了两个小时。现在我喜欢坐飞机了。

**词与短语**：by plane（坐飞机）、take off（起飞）、above the clouds（云层之上）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时 + at first（起初）** —— at first 表示"起初"，常与后来（now/later）形成对比。

**语法示例**：
- At first, I was afraid, but later I enjoyed it.（起初我害怕，但后来我很喜欢。）
- At first, it was hard, but now it is easy.（起初很难，现在很简单。）

### 60. My Dream Trip（我梦想的旅行）

I have a dream trip. I would like to travel around the world. I want to see famous places in different countries. I want to try different food and meet new friends. I hope my dream will come true.

我有一个梦想的旅行。我想环游世界，看看不同国家的名胜，尝尝不同的美食，结识新朋友。我希望梦想能实现。

**词与短语**：a dream trip（梦想的旅行）、around the world（环游世界）、famous places（名胜）

**语法点睛**：**would like to do（想要做）** —— would like to + 动词原形，表示"想要做"，比 want 更礼貌。

**语法示例**：
- I would like to visit France.（我想去法国。）
- She would like to have a cup of tea.（她想喝杯茶。）

## 十三、通讯与科技

### 61. My Phone（我的手机）

I have a smart phone. It is very useful. I can call my friends and send messages. I can also take photos and listen to music. But I should not use my phone too much. I use it in a healthy way.

我有一部智能手机，非常有用。我能给朋友打电话、发信息，还能拍照、听音乐。但我不应该用太多手机，我要健康地使用它。

**词与短语**：a smart phone（智能手机）、send messages（发信息）、in a healthy way（以健康的方式）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can + 并列动词（复现强化）** —— can call、send、take、listen，连续列举"能做"的事。

**语法示例**：
- I can sing and dance.（我会唱歌和跳舞。）
- My phone can take photos and play music.（我的手机能拍照、放音乐。）

### 62. Using the Internet（使用互联网）

I use the Internet every day. I search for information for my homework. I watch English videos online. I also talk with my friends online. The Internet is helpful, but I must be careful. I should not share my private information.

我每天使用互联网。我上网搜索作业资料，在线看英语视频，还在网上和朋友聊天。互联网很有帮助，但我必须小心，不应该泄露个人信息。

**词与短语**：search for information（搜索信息）、online（在线）、private information（个人信息）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 must（必须，复现）** —— must + 动词原形，语气比 should 更强，表示"必须"。

**语法示例**：
- You must be careful online.（上网时你必须小心。）
- We must protect our eyes.（我们必须保护眼睛。）

### 63. Social Media（社交媒体）

Many people use social media. I use it to share my life with friends. I post photos and short videos. I also like reading my friends' posts. But I do not spend too much time on it. Social media should not take all my time.

很多人使用社交媒体。我用它和朋友分享生活，发布照片和短视频。我也喜欢看朋友的动态。但我不会花太多时间在上面。社交媒体不应该占据我所有的时间。

**词与短语**：social media（社交媒体）、share my life（分享生活）、post photos（发布照片）

**语法点睛**：**不定式表目的（to share）** —— use it to share my life，to share 表示使用的目的。

**语法示例**：
- I use my phone to take photos.（我用手机拍照。）
- She uses the Internet to study.（她用互联网学习。）

### 64. Online Learning（在线学习）

Online learning is popular now. I take online English classes every weekend. I can study at home. The teacher talks with me through video. Online learning is convenient. I can learn anywhere and anytime.

在线学习现在很流行。我每个周末上在线英语课，可以在家学习。老师通过视频和我交流。在线学习很方便，我可以随时随地学习。

**词与短语**：online learning（在线学习）、take classes（上课）、through video（通过视频）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can 表可能（复现）** —— I can study at home / learn anywhere，can 表示"能够、可以"。

**语法示例**：
- I can study online at home.（我可以在家在线学习。）
- You can learn English anywhere.（你可以在任何地方学英语。）

### 65. Staying Safe Online（网络安全）

It is important to stay safe online. I should not tell strangers my real name or address. I should not open strange links. I must ask my parents when I am not sure. Being safe online is very important.

网络安全很重要。我不应该告诉陌生人我的真实姓名和地址，不应该打开奇怪的链接。不确定时要问父母。上网安全非常重要。

**词与短语**：stay safe online（安全上网）、strange links（奇怪的链接）、my real name（我的真实姓名）

**语法点睛**：**should not + 动词原形（不应该）** —— should not（shouldn't）表示"不应该做某事"。

**语法示例**：
- You should not open strange links.（你不应该打开奇怪的链接。）
- We should not be late for school.（我们不应该上学迟到。）

## 十四、衣服与时尚

### 66. My Clothes（我的衣服）

I have many clothes in my wardrobe. I have T-shirts, dresses, trousers and coats. I wear a T-shirt and shorts in summer. In winter, I wear a warm coat. My clothes are clean and comfortable. I like them.

我的衣柜里有很多衣服，有 T 恤、连衣裙、裤子和外套。夏天我穿 T 恤和短裤，冬天穿暖和的外套。我的衣服干净又舒适，我喜欢它们。

**词与短语**：a wardrobe（衣柜）、trousers（裤子）、comfortable（舒适的）

**语法点睛**：**名词复数与不可数（trousers 常以复数出现）** —— trousers、shorts 通常以复数形式出现，用 a pair of 计量。

**语法示例**：
- I bought a pair of trousers.（我买了一条裤子。）
- She wears shorts in summer.（她夏天穿短裤。）

### 67. Buying New Clothes（买新衣服）

Last weekend, I went shopping for new clothes. I tried on several jackets. The blue one was nice, but it was too small. The black one was bigger and it fit me well. I bought the black jacket. It was a good choice.

上周末，我去买新衣服。我试穿了几件夹克。蓝色的很好看，但太小了。黑色的更大，而且合身。我买了黑色夹克，这是个好选择。

**词与短语**：try on（试穿）、fit me well（很合身）、a good choice（好选择）

**语法点睛**：**比较级（bigger）** —— The black one was bigger，bigger 是 big 的比较级，表示"更大"。

**语法示例**：
- This shirt is bigger than that one.（这件衬衫比那件大。）
- I need a bigger size.（我需要更大的尺码。）

### 68. My Favorite Outfit（我最喜欢的穿搭）

My favorite outfit is a white shirt and blue jeans. It is simple but nice. I wear it when I go out with friends. It is comfortable and easy to match. I feel confident in this outfit.

我最喜欢的穿搭是白衬衫配蓝牛仔裤，简单又好看。和朋友出去时我就穿它。它舒适又百搭，穿上它我很自信。

**词与短语**：favorite outfit（最喜欢的穿搭）、blue jeans（蓝色牛仔裤）、easy to match（百搭）

**语法点睛**：**简单句 + 并列形容词（simple but nice）** —— simple but nice 表示"简单但好看"，but 表转折。

**语法示例**：
- The dress is simple but beautiful.（这条裙子简单但漂亮。）
- My room is small but cozy.（我的房间小但舒适。）

### 69. Dressing for Weather（按天气穿衣）

We should dress for the weather. When it is cold, I wear a warm coat and a scarf. When it is hot, I wear light clothes. When it rains, I take an umbrella. Dressing right keeps us comfortable.

我们应该根据天气穿衣。天冷时，我穿暖和的外套、围围巾。天热时，我穿轻薄的衣服。下雨时，我带上雨伞。穿得合适让我们保持舒适。

**词与短语**：dress for the weather（根据天气穿衣）、a scarf（围巾）、light clothes（轻薄的衣服）

**语法点睛**：**when 引导的排比从句（复现）** —— 连续用三个 When... 引导时间状语从句，说明不同天气下的穿着。

**语法示例**：
- When it is cold, I wear gloves.（天冷时，我戴手套。）
- When it is sunny, I wear a hat.（天晴时，我戴帽子。）

### 70. Fashion（时尚）

Fashion changes quickly. Some people always follow the latest fashion. I think comfort is more important than fashion. I like clothes that are both nice and comfortable. You do not need to follow every trend.

时尚变化很快。有些人总是追赶最新潮流。我觉得舒适比时尚更重要。我喜欢既好看又舒适的衣服。你不必追赶每一个潮流。

**词与短语**：the latest fashion（最新潮流）、follow the trend（追赶潮流）、comfort（舒适）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more important than** —— more important than 表示"比……更重要"，多音节形容词用 more + 原级。

**语法示例**：
- Health is more important than money.（健康比金钱更重要。）
- This book is more interesting than that one.（这本书比那本更有趣。）

## 十五、食物与烹饪

### 71. Cooking（烹饪）

I like cooking with my mother. We cook dinner together every weekend. I can cook some simple dishes now. I can make fried rice and tomato soup. Cooking is fun and useful. I feel proud when my family likes my food.

我喜欢和妈妈一起做饭。我们每个周末一起做晚餐。现在我会做一些简单的菜了，会做炒饭和西红柿汤。做饭有趣又有用。家人喜欢我做的菜时，我很自豪。

**词与短语**：cook dinner（做晚餐）、simple dishes（简单的菜）、feel proud（感到自豪）

**语法点睛**：**when 引导时间状语从句（复现）** —— I feel proud when my family likes my food，when 从句说明"什么时候"自豪。

**语法示例**：
- I feel happy when I cook for my family.（为家人做饭时我很开心。）
- She feels proud when she helps others.（帮助别人时她很自豪。）

### 72. My Favorite Food（我最喜欢的食物）

My favorite food is dumplings. My mother makes the best dumplings in the world. We eat dumplings on weekends and during festivals. I like them with vinegar. Dumplings make me think of home and family.

我最喜欢的食物是饺子。我妈妈包的饺子是世界上最好吃的。我们周末和过节时吃饺子。我喜欢蘸醋吃。饺子让我想起家和亲人。

**词与短语**：favorite food（最喜欢的食物）、make dumplings（包饺子）、think of（想起）

**语法点睛**：**最高级 the best（复现）** —— the best dumplings in the world，best 是 good 的最高级，表示"最好的"。

**语法示例**：
- My mother makes the best cakes.（我妈妈做的蛋糕最好吃。）
- This is the best restaurant in town.（这是城里最好的餐厅。）

### 73. Making a Meal（做一顿饭）

Let me tell you how to make fried rice. First, prepare the rice, eggs and vegetables. Next, put some oil in the pan. Then, fry the eggs and rice together. Finally, add a little salt. The fried rice is ready. It is simple and delicious.

让我告诉你怎样做炒饭。首先，准备米饭、鸡蛋和蔬菜。然后，往锅里放点油。接着，把鸡蛋和米饭一起炒。最后，加一点盐。炒饭就做好了，简单又美味。

**词与短语**：fried rice（炒饭）、put some oil（放点油）、a little salt（一点盐）

**语法点睛**：**顺序副词 First / Next / Then / Finally** —— 用这些词描述做事的步骤顺序。

**语法示例**：
- First, wash the rice. Then, cook it.（先洗米，然后煮饭。）
- Finally, add some water and wait.（最后，加点水等待。）

### 74. Eating Habits（饮食习惯）

I am trying to build healthy eating habits. I eat three meals on time. I eat more vegetables than before. I eat less fast food now. I also drink less sweet drinks. Good habits keep me healthy.

我正在养成健康的饮食习惯。我按时吃三餐，比以前吃更多蔬菜，现在吃更少快餐，也喝更少的甜饮料。好习惯让我保持健康。

**词与短语**：eating habits（饮食习惯）、three meals（三餐）、fast food（快餐）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more...than / less（复现）** —— more vegetables than before（比以前更多蔬菜）、less fast food（更少快餐）。

**语法示例**：
- I eat more fruit than before.（我比以前吃更多水果。）
- She drinks less coffee now.（她现在喝更少的咖啡。）

### 75. Food from Different Places（各地美食）

China has many different kinds of food. In the north, people like noodles. In the south, people eat more rice. Different places have different tastes. I want to try food from all over China. It must be a great experience.

中国有很多不同的美食。北方人喜欢面条，南方人吃更多米饭。不同的地方有不同的口味。我想尝遍全中国各地的美食，那一定是很棒的体验。

**词与短语**：different kinds of（不同种类的）、different tastes（不同的口味）、all over China（全中国）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more（更多，复现）** —— In the south, people eat more rice，more 表示"更多"。

**语法示例**：
- I want to eat more vegetables.（我想吃更多蔬菜。）
- People in the north eat more noodles.（北方人吃更多面条。）

## 十六、娱乐与媒体

### 76. Movies（电影）

I love watching movies. I watch a movie every weekend. My favorite kind is cartoon movies. They are funny and full of imagination. Last week, I watched a new cartoon movie. It was wonderful. Movies bring me joy.

我爱看电影，每个周末看一部。我最喜欢动画片，它们有趣又充满想象力。上周我看了一部新动画片，非常精彩。电影给我带来快乐。

**词与短语**：watch a movie（看电影）、full of imagination（充满想象力）、bring me joy（给我带来快乐）

**语法点睛**：**最高级/种类表达（My favorite kind）** —— favorite 表示"最喜欢的"，kind 表示"种类"。

**语法示例**：
- My favorite kind of music is pop.（我最喜欢的音乐类型是流行乐。）
- What kind of books do you like?（你喜欢哪类书？）

### 77. Music and Songs（音乐和歌曲）

Music is a big part of my life. I listen to music every day. I like pop songs and light music. Music can make me happy or calm. I also like singing with my friends. Music brings people together.

音乐是我生活的重要部分。我每天听音乐，喜欢流行歌曲和轻音乐。音乐能让我开心或平静。我也喜欢和朋友一起唱歌。音乐把人们联系在一起。

**词与短语**：a big part of my life（我生活的重要部分）、light music（轻音乐）、bring people together（把人们联系在一起）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can 表可能（make...happy）** —— Music can make me happy，can 表示"能够、可能"。

**语法示例**：
- Music can make me calm.（音乐能让我平静。）
- A good book can make me happy.（一本好书能让我开心。）

### 78. TV Programs（电视节目）

I like watching TV programs. I usually watch cartoons after school. My parents like watching the news. On weekends, we watch a movie together. But I should not watch TV for too long. I should protect my eyes.

我喜欢看电视节目。放学后我通常看动画片。父母喜欢看新闻。周末我们一起看电影。但我不应该看太久电视，要保护眼睛。

**词与短语**：TV programs（电视节目）、watch the news（看新闻）、for too long（太久）

**语法点睛**：**频度副词 usually（复现）** —— usually 放在实义动词 watch 前，表示"通常"。

**语法示例**：
- I usually do my homework first.（我通常先做作业。）
- She usually goes to bed at ten.（她通常十点睡觉。）

### 79. Reading for Fun（快乐阅读）

Reading is one of my hobbies. I read story books and science books. I read before bed every night. Reading helps me learn new things. It also makes me calm. I think reading is the best hobby.

阅读是我的爱好之一。我读故事书和科学书，每晚睡前阅读。阅读帮我学到新东西，也让我平静。我觉得阅读是最好的爱好。

**词与短语**：one of my hobbies（我的爱好之一）、science books（科学书）、learn new things（学新东西）

**语法点睛**：**one of + 复数名词（……之一）** —— one of my hobbies 表示"我的爱好之一"，of 后接复数。

**语法示例**：
- English is one of my favorite subjects.（英语是我最喜欢的科目之一。）
- This is one of the best books.（这是最好的书之一。）

### 80. Games and Sports（游戏和运动）

I like both games and sports. Playing games is fun, and doing sports is healthy. I think doing sports is more useful than playing games. So I spend more time on sports. I play basketball and run every day.

游戏和运动我都喜欢。玩游戏有趣，做运动健康。我觉得做运动比玩游戏更有用，所以我在运动上花更多时间。我每天打篮球、跑步。

**词与短语**：both...and...（两者都）、more useful than（比……更有用）、spend time on（在……上花时间）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 more useful than（复现）** —— more useful than 表示"比……更有用"，用于多音节形容词。

**语法示例**：
- Sports are more useful than games.（运动比游戏更有用。）
- This book is more useful than that one.（这本书比那本更有用。）

## 十七、规则与义务

### 81. School Rules（校规）

Our school has many rules. We must wear our school uniform. We must arrive on time. We must not run in the hallways. We should be polite to teachers. Rules make our school a better place.

我们学校有很多规则。我们必须穿校服，必须按时到校，不能在走廊里奔跑，应该对老师有礼貌。规则让学校变得更好。

**词与短语**：school uniform（校服）、arrive on time（按时到校）、be polite to（对……有礼貌）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 must / must not** —— must 表示"必须"，must not（mustn't）表示"禁止"。

**语法示例**：
- We must listen to the teacher.（我们必须听老师讲。）
- You must not talk loudly in class.（上课时你不能大声说话。）

### 82. Traffic Rules（交通规则）

Traffic rules keep us safe. We must cross the road at the crosswalk. We must look left and right before crossing. We must not run across the road. When the light is red, we must stop. Safety comes first.

交通规则保障我们的安全。我们必须走人行横道过马路，过马路前要左右看看，不能跑着横穿马路。红灯亮时必须停下。安全第一。

**词与短语**：traffic rules（交通规则）、the crosswalk（人行横道）、cross the road（过马路）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 must 表义务（复现强化）** —— 连续用 must 表达交通规则中的义务与禁止。

**语法示例**：
- You must wear a helmet when riding.（骑车时你必须戴头盔。）
- We must not cross when the light is red.（红灯时我们不能过马路。）

### 83. Home Rules（家规）

My family has some rules too. I have to do my homework before dinner. I have to go to bed before ten. I can watch TV only on weekends. I think these rules are good for me. They help me form good habits.

我家也有一些规矩。晚饭前我必须做完作业，十点前必须睡觉，只能在周末看电视。我觉得这些规矩对我有好处，能帮我养成好习惯。

**词与短语**：home rules（家规）、form good habits（养成好习惯）、before ten（十点前）

**语法点睛**：**have to（不得不）** —— have to + 动词原形，表示客观要求的"不得不、必须"，比 must 更强调外部要求。

**语法示例**：
- I have to get up early on school days.（上学日我不得不早起。）
- She has to clean her room every week.（她每周不得不打扫房间。）

### 84. Following Rules（遵守规则）

Why should we follow rules? Rules protect us and keep things fair. When everyone follows the rules, life is easier. I try my best to follow the rules at school and at home. I think following rules is a good habit.

我们为什么要遵守规则？规则保护我们，让事情公平。当每个人都遵守规则时，生活会更轻松。我尽力遵守学校和家里的规则。我认为遵守规则是个好习惯。

**词与短语**：follow the rules（遵守规则）、keep things fair（让事情公平）、try my best（尽力）

**语法点睛**：**when 引导时间状语从句（复现）** —— When everyone follows the rules, life is easier，when 从句在前。

**语法示例**：
- When we follow the rules, we are safe.（遵守规则时，我们才安全。）
- When everyone works hard, we succeed.（人人努力时，我们才能成功。）

### 85. Public Manners（公共礼仪）

Good manners are important in public. We should wait in line. We should not talk loudly in the library. We should give our seat to old people on the bus. We should say "please" and "thank you". Good manners make the world kinder.

公共场合的礼仪很重要。我们应该排队等候，在图书馆不大声说话，在公交车上给老人让座，常说"请"和"谢谢"。良好的礼仪让世界更友善。

**词与短语**：public manners（公共礼仪）、wait in line（排队等候）、give our seat to（给……让座）

**语法点睛**：**should / should not（复现强化）** —— 连续用 should 和 should not 表达公共场合应有的礼仪。

**语法示例**：
- We should wait in line quietly.（我们应该安静排队。）
- You should not talk loudly in public.（你不应该在公共场合大声说话。）

## 十八、请求与帮助

### 86. Asking for Help（求助）

When I need help, I ask politely. I say, "Excuse me, could you help me, please?" I explain my problem clearly. After someone helps me, I say thank you. Being polite is important when we ask for help.

需要帮助时，我会礼貌地求助。我说："打扰一下，请问你能帮我吗？"我清楚地说明问题。别人帮我之后，我会道谢。求助时礼貌很重要。

**词与短语**：ask politely（礼貌地求助）、explain my problem（说明我的问题）、ask for help（求助）

**语法点睛**：**Could you...?（礼貌请求）** —— Could you help me, please? 用 could 比 can 更礼貌，表示请求。

**语法示例**：
- Could you open the window, please?（请你打开窗户好吗？）
- Could you tell me the way to the station?（你能告诉我去车站的路吗？）

### 87. Offering Help（主动帮忙）

I like helping others. When I see someone in trouble, I offer to help. I say, "Can I help you?" or "Let me help you." Helping others makes me happy. I believe kindness makes the world better.

我喜欢帮助别人。看到别人遇到困难时，我主动帮忙。我说："需要帮忙吗？"或"让我来帮你吧。"帮助别人让我快乐。我相信善良让世界更美好。

**词与短语**：in trouble（遇到困难）、offer to help（主动帮忙）、kindness（善良）

**语法点睛**：**Can I help you?（提供帮助）** —— Can I...? 用来主动提出帮助，是常用的礼貌表达。

**语法示例**：
- Can I help you carry the bags?（需要我帮你提袋子吗？）
- Can I open the door for you?（需要我帮你开门吗？）

### 88. Asking for Permission（请求许可）

Before doing something, I often ask for permission. I say, "May I come in?" or "Can I use your pen?" At school, I ask the teacher before leaving. Asking for permission shows respect. It is a good habit.

做事情之前，我常常先请求许可。我说："我可以进来吗？"或"我可以用你的笔吗？"在学校，离开前我会先问老师。请求许可体现尊重，是个好习惯。

**词与短语**：ask for permission（请求许可）、show respect（体现尊重）、a good habit（好习惯）

**语法点睛**：**May I...?（请求许可）** —— May I come in? 用 may 请求许可，比 can 更正式、更礼貌。

**语法示例**：
- May I borrow your book?（我可以借你的书吗？）
- May I ask a question?（我可以问个问题吗？）

### 89. Giving Directions（指路）

Yesterday a visitor asked me the way to the museum. I said, "Go straight and turn right at the second crossing." He thanked me and smiled. I was happy to help. Giving directions is a useful skill.

昨天一位游客向我问去博物馆的路。我说："直走，在第二个十字路口右转。"他向我道谢并微笑。我很高兴能帮忙。指路是一项有用的技能。

**词与短语**：ask the way（问路）、go straight（直走）、the second crossing（第二个十字路口）

**语法点睛**：**祈使句（Go straight / Turn right）** —— 祈使句用动词原形开头，表示命令或指引。

**语法示例**：
- Turn left at the corner.（在拐角处左转。）
- Walk along this street for five minutes.（沿着这条街走五分钟。）

### 90. Saying Thank You（道谢）

Saying thank you is very important. When someone helps me, I always say thank you. I also write thank-you notes sometimes. A simple "thank you" can make people happy. I should be thankful for the people around me.

道谢非常重要。别人帮助我时，我总是说谢谢，有时还写感谢便条。一句简单的"谢谢"能让人开心。我应该对身边的人心怀感激。

**词与短语**：say thank you（道谢）、thank-you notes（感谢便条）、be thankful for（对……心怀感激）

**语法点睛**：**情态动词 can（复现）** —— A simple "thank you" can make people happy，can 表示"能够、可能"。

**语法示例**：
- A kind word can make people happy.（一句暖心的话能让人开心。）
- Your help can make a big difference.（你的帮助能带来很大不同。）

## 十九、比较与选择

### 91. Making Choices（做选择）

Every day we make choices. What should we eat? What should we wear? Some choices are easy, and some are hard. When I make a choice, I think carefully. I compare the good and bad sides. Then I choose the best one.

我们每天都在做选择。吃什么？穿什么？有些选择容易，有些很难。做选择时，我认真思考，比较好的一面和坏的一面，然后选择最好的。

**词与短语**：make choices（做选择）、compare（比较）、the good and bad sides（好的一面和坏的一面）

**语法点睛**：**特殊疑问句（What should we...?）** —— What should we eat? 用疑问词 what 开头，询问"什么"。

**语法示例**：
- What should I wear today?（我今天该穿什么？）
- Which one should I choose?（我该选哪一个？）

### 92. Comparing Things（比较事物）

Let me compare two cities. Beijing is bigger than my hometown. But my hometown is quieter than Beijing. Beijing has more people and more cars. My hometown has fresher air. Each city has its own good points.

让我比较两座城市。北京比我的家乡大，但我的家乡比北京安静。北京有更多人和更多车，我的家乡空气更清新。每座城市都有自己的优点。

**词与短语**：compare（比较）、more people（更多人）、fresher air（更清新的空气）

**语法点睛**：**比较级（bigger / quieter / more / fresher）** —— 比较两个事物时用比较级 + than，或 more + 名词。

**语法示例**：
- This city is bigger than that one.（这座城市比那座大。）
- The air here is fresher than in the city.（这里的空气比城里清新。）

### 93. Which One Is Better?（哪个更好？）

Which is better, living in the city or the countryside? The city is convenient, but it is noisy. The countryside is quiet, but it is not convenient. I think it depends on what you like. For me, the countryside is better.

住在城市还是乡村更好？城市方便但吵闹，乡村安静但不方便。我觉得这取决于你喜欢什么。对我来说，乡村更好。

**词与短语**：convenient（方便的）、noisy（吵闹的）、depend on（取决于）

**语法点睛**：**比较级 + 选择问句** —— Which is better...? 用比较级提问，比较两个选项。

**语法示例**：
- Which is better, tea or coffee?（茶和咖啡哪个更好？）
- Which is cheaper, the bus or the subway?（公交和地铁哪个更便宜？）

### 94. My Favorite Season（我最喜欢的季节）

My favorite season is winter. Some people think winter is too cold. But I think winter is the most beautiful season. When it snows, everything turns white. I can make a snowman and have snowball fights. Winter brings me a lot of fun.

我最喜欢的季节是冬天。有些人觉得冬天太冷，但我觉得冬天是最美的季节。下雪时，一切都变白了。我可以堆雪人、打雪仗。冬天给我带来很多乐趣。

**词与短语**：favorite season（最喜欢的季节）、make a snowman（堆雪人）、snowball fights（打雪仗）

**语法点睛**：**最高级 the most beautiful** —— the most + 形容词，多音节形容词的最高级形式，表示"最……"。

**语法示例**：
- This is the most beautiful park I have seen.（这是我见过的最美的公园。）
- Spring is the most comfortable season.（春天是最舒适的季节。）

### 95. Big or Small?（大还是小？）

Which do you like better, a big city or a small town? A big city is exciting, but it is crowded. A small town is peaceful, but it is a little boring. I like small towns better because I like a quiet life.

你更喜欢大城市还是小城镇？大城市令人兴奋，但很拥挤。小城镇宁静，但有点无聊。我更喜欢小城镇，因为我喜欢安静的生活。

**词与短语**：a big city（大城市）、crowded（拥挤的）、a quiet life（安静的生活）

**语法点睛**：**like...better（更喜欢）** —— like A better than B 表示"比起 B 更喜欢 A"。

**语法示例**：
- I like tea better than coffee.（比起咖啡，我更喜欢茶。）
- She likes reading better than watching TV.（比起看电视，她更喜欢读书。）

## 二十、节日与传统

### 96. Spring Festival（春节）

Spring Festival is the most important festival in China. It usually comes in January or February. Before the festival, we clean our house. On New Year's Eve, we have a big family dinner. Children get red packets. It is a happy time for everyone.

春节是中国最重要的节日，通常在一月或二月。节前我们打扫房子。除夕夜，我们吃一顿丰盛的家庭晚餐。孩子们收到红包。这是每个人都开心的时刻。

**词与短语**：the most important festival（最重要的节日）、New Year's Eve（除夕）、red packets（红包）

**语法点睛**：**最高级 the most important（复现）** —— the most + 形容词，表示"最重要的"。

**语法示例**：
- This is the most important day of my life.（这是我人生中最重要的一天。）
- Family is the most important thing to me.（家庭对我来说是最重要的。）

### 97. Mid-Autumn Festival（中秋节）

Mid-Autumn Festival is in autumn. On that day, the moon is full and bright. Families get together and eat mooncakes. We sit outside and enjoy the beautiful moon. The festival means family reunion. I love this warm festival.

中秋节在秋天。那天月亮又圆又亮。家人们团聚在一起吃月饼。我们坐在外面赏月。这个节日意味着家人团圆。我爱这个温暖的节日。

**词与短语**：the moon is full（月圆）、eat mooncakes（吃月饼）、family reunion（家人团圆）

**语法点睛**：**一般现在时描述节日传统** —— 描述节日习俗时用一般现在时，表示习惯性、规律性的活动。

**语法示例**：
- We eat mooncakes on Mid-Autumn Festival.（我们在中秋节吃月饼。）
- Families get together on this day.（这一天家人们团聚。）

### 98. Dragon Boat Festival（端午节）

Dragon Boat Festival is on the fifth day of the fifth lunar month. We eat zongzi and watch dragon boat races. The races are exciting. I like eating sweet zongzi best. This festival remembers a great poet, Qu Yuan.

端午节在农历五月初五。我们吃粽子、看龙舟赛。比赛很激动人心。我最喜欢吃甜粽子。这个节日是为了纪念伟大的诗人屈原。

**词与短语**：dragon boat races（龙舟赛）、eat zongzi（吃粽子）、lunar month（农历月份）

**语法点睛**：**序数词 + 日期（the fifth day）** —— 表达"初五"用 the fifth day，序数词表日期。

**语法示例**：
- The festival is on the first day of the lunar year.（这个节日在农历新年的第一天。）
- Today is the fifth day of the month.（今天是这个月的第五天。）

### 99. National Day（国庆节）

National Day is on October first. Last year, my family visited Beijing. We saw the flag raising. It was very moving. This year, we plan to visit the Great Wall. I am looking forward to it. I love my country.

国庆节是十月一日。去年，我们一家去了北京，看了升旗仪式，非常感人。今年，我们计划去长城，我很期待。我爱我的祖国。

**词与短语**：National Day（国庆节）、the flag raising（升旗仪式）、the Great Wall（长城）

**语法点睛**：**一般过去时 + 一般将来时对比** —— last year 用过去时（visited, saw），this year 用将来时（plan to）。

**语法示例**：
- Last year, I visited the Great Wall.（去年我参观了长城。）
- This year, we will go to Beijing.（今年我们要去北京。）

### 100. Festival Food（节日美食）

Every festival has its special food. We eat dumplings on Spring Festival. We eat mooncakes on Mid-Autumn Festival. We eat zongzi on Dragon Boat Festival. Festival food is more than food. It carries our culture and memory. I love festival food.

每个节日都有它的特色美食。春节吃饺子，中秋节吃月饼，端午节吃粽子。节日美食不只是食物，它承载着我们的文化和记忆。我爱节日美食。

**词与短语**：special food（特色美食）、more than（不只是）、carry culture（承载文化）

**语法点睛**：**比较结构 more than（不只是）** —— more than 表示"不只是、不仅仅是"，强调更深的意义。

**语法示例**：
- A friend is more than a person you know.（朋友不只是你认识的一个人。）
- Music is more than just sound.（音乐不只是声音。）
