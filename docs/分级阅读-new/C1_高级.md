# C1 分级阅读-new · 高级（全覆盖版）

> 对应 CEFR C1（约 8,000 词）。本套以「四维全覆盖」为目标：14 个核心语法点 + 高频动词短语 + 习语 + 固定搭配 + 20 个话题，全部织入 16 篇短文。每篇标注语法点、分类短语、语法例句（≥3 句），适合逐句跟读、反复背诵。

## 一、高级倒装

### 1. Never Have I Seen（我从未见过）· 高级倒装 Never/Hardly

Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset. Hardly had I arrived home when it began to rain. Rarely do we stop to appreciate the small things. Seldom does he complain about his work. Little did I know that this decision would change my life. Not until last year did I realise the value of time.

我从未见过如此美丽的日落。我刚到家就开始下雨了。我们很少停下来欣赏小事。他很少抱怨工作。我当时几乎不知道这个决定会改变我的人生。直到去年我才意识到时间的价值。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：change one's life（改变人生）、realise the value（认识到价值）
- 词汇：sunset（日落）、appreciate（欣赏）、complain（抱怨）、rarely（很少）

**语法点睛**
**高级倒装 Never/Hardly/Rarely/Seldom/Little/Not until**——否定或半否定副词置于句首时，主句要用部分倒装（助动词提到主语前）。Hardly had I...when... 表「刚……就……」。

**语法示例**
- Never have I met such a kind person.（我从未遇到过如此善良的人。）
- Hardly had we left when it started snowing.（我们刚离开就开始下雪了。）
- Not until yesterday did she call me.（直到昨天她才给我打电话。）

### 2. Only Then Did I Understand（只有那时我才明白）· 倒装 Only/Not only

Only then did I understand the true meaning of success. Only by working together can we solve this problem. Not only does exercise improve health, but it also reduces stress. No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang. Under no circumstances should you give up. Only when we act will things change.

只有那时我才明白成功的真正含义。只有共同努力，我们才能解决这个问题。锻炼不仅能改善健康，还能减轻压力。我刚坐下电话就响了。无论如何你都不应该放弃。只有当我们行动时，事情才会改变。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：solve a problem（解决问题）、reduce stress（减轻压力）、give up（放弃）
- 词汇：meaning（含义）、circumstances（情况）、act（行动）

**语法点睛**
**倒装 Only/Not only/No sooner/Under no circumstances**——Only + 状语、Not only、Under no circumstances 等置于句首时，主句用部分倒装。

**语法示例**
- Only by practising can you improve.（只有练习你才能进步。）
- Not only did he win, but he also set a record.（他不仅赢了，还创了纪录。）
- Under no circumstances should you lie.（无论如何你都不该撒谎。）

## 二、强调结构

### 3. What the World Needs（世界所需要的）· 强调句 cleft What...is

What the world needs now is understanding. What matters is not how much you earn, but how much you give. What I admire most is her determination. What surprised everyone was his sudden resignation. What we must remember is that change takes time. What makes a society strong is trust.

世界现在需要的是理解。重要的不是你挣多少，而是你给予多少。我最钦佩的是她的决心。让所有人惊讶的是他突然辞职。我们必须记住的是，改变需要时间。让社会强大的是信任。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：admire someone（钦佩某人）、take time（需要时间）
- 词汇：determination（决心）、resignation（辞职）、trust（信任）

**语法点睛**
**强调句 cleft（What...is）**——用 What 引导的名词性从句作主语，把要强调的内容放句尾，形成「What + 从句 + is/was + 强调部分」。

**语法示例**
- What I need is a good night's sleep.（我需要的是好好睡一觉。）
- What impressed me was her honesty.（打动我的是她的诚实。）
- What we should focus on is quality.（我们应关注的是质量。）

### 4. It Is Not Until... That（直到……才）· 强调句 It is...that（高级）

It was not until last year that she started her own business. It is because of teamwork that we succeeded. It is the small details that make the difference. It was her kindness that touched everyone. It is through failure that we learn the most. It is not what you say, but what you do, that matters.

直到去年她才开始创业。我们成功正是因为团队合作。正是那些小细节带来了不同。是她的善良打动了每个人。我们学得最多的是通过失败。重要的不是你说什么，而是你做什么。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：start a business（创业）、make the difference（带来不同）
- 词汇：teamwork（团队合作）、failure（失败）、kindness（善良）

**语法点睛**
**强调句 It is...that（高级）**——强调时间、原因、方式等状语：It is not until...that（直到……才）、It is because...that（正是因为）、It is through...that（正是通过）。

**语法示例**
- It was not until midnight that he returned.（直到半夜他才回来。）
- It is because of you that I am here.（我在这里正是因为有你。）
- It is through practice that we improve.（我们正是通过练习进步的。）

## 三、虚拟语气（高级）

### 5. Were It Not for...（要不是……）· 虚拟语气（高级）

Were it not for your help, I would not have succeeded. Had I known the risks, I would have acted differently. Were she to win the award, she would be the youngest ever. Should you need any assistance, please contact us. But for your support, the project would have failed. If it were not for technology, our lives would be very different.

要不是你的帮助，我不会成功。如果我当时知道风险，我会采取不同行动。如果她获奖，她将成为有史以来最年轻的。如果你需要任何帮助，请联系我们。要不是你的支持，项目早就失败了。如果没有科技，我们的生活将大不相同。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：win an award（获奖）、take risks（冒险）
- 词汇：assistance（帮助）、support（支持）、differently（不同地）

**语法点睛**
**虚拟语气（高级）**——省略 if 的倒装：Were it not for（要不是）、Had I known（如果我当时知道）、Should you need（如果你需要）；But for 也可表「要不是」。

**语法示例**
- Were it not for the rain, we would go out.（要不是下雨，我们就出门了。）
- Had I been there, I would have helped.（如果我当时在，我会帮忙。）
- But for your advice, I would have failed.（要不是你的建议，我就失败了。）

### 6. Had It Not Been for...（要不是当时……）· 虚拟语气（高级倒装）

Had it not been for the timely warning, the damage would have been far worse. Had the government acted sooner, the crisis could have been avoided. Were it not for economic growth, poverty would be far more widespread. Had we invested earlier, we would have gained far more. But for his courage, many lives would have been lost.

要不是及时的警告，损失会严重得多。如果政府早点行动，危机本可以避免。要不是经济增长，贫困会普遍得多。如果我们早点投资，收益会大得多。要不是他的勇气，许多人都会丧生。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：avoid a crisis（避免危机）、invest early（早点投资）、lose lives（丧生）
- 词汇：timely（及时）、widespread（普遍）、courage（勇气）

**语法点睛**
**虚拟语气（高级倒装）**——Had it not been for...（要不是当时……）、Were it not for...（要不是现在……），均省略 if 并用倒装，用于正式、书面语境。

**语法示例**
- Had it not been for the storm, we would have arrived on time.（要不是暴风雨，我们就准时到了。）
- Were it not for your kindness, I would be lost.（要不是你的善良，我会迷失。）
- But for the delay, we would have won.（要不是延误，我们就赢了。）

## 四、条件句与分词

### 7. A Mixed Conditional（混合条件）· 混合条件句（高级）

If I had taken that job ten years ago, I would be in a very different position now. If she were more confident, she would have applied for the position. If we had planned ahead, we would not be struggling today. If he were not so talented, he would not have achieved so much. If I had known then what I know now, I would have chosen differently.

如果我十年前接受了那份工作，我现在会处于完全不同的位置。如果她更自信，她当时就会申请那个职位。如果我们提前计划，今天就不会这么艰难。如果他没有这么有才华，就不会取得这么大成就。如果我当时知道现在知道的这些，我会做出不同的选择。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：apply for a position（申请职位）、plan ahead（提前计划）、achieve much（取得成就）
- 词汇：confident（自信）、talented（有才华）、position（位置）

**语法点睛**
**混合条件句（高级）**——条件与结果时间错位：过去条件 + 现在结果（had done → would do）；现在条件 + 过去结果（did → would have done）。

**语法示例**
- If I had saved more, I would be richer now.（如果我多存点钱，现在会更富。）
- If she were honest, she would have told us.（如果她诚实，她当时就会告诉我们。）
- If I had caught that flight, I would be in Paris now.（如果我赶上那班飞机，现在就在巴黎了。）

### 8. I Wish I Had Known（我多希望早知道）· wish/if only（高级）

I wish I had known the truth from the beginning. If only I had listened to my parents' advice. She wishes she had not wasted so much time. I wish you would stop worrying about the future. He wished he had never made that promise. If only we could turn back the clock.

我多希望从一开始就知道真相。要是我当初听了父母的建议就好了。她多希望自己没有浪费那么多时间。我希望你别再为未来担忧。他多希望自己从没许下那个承诺。要是我们能回到过去就好了。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：know the truth（知道真相）、listen to advice（听建议）、waste time（浪费时间）、make a promise（许下承诺）
- 词汇：beginning（开始）、future（未来）、worry（担忧）

**语法点睛**
**wish/if only（高级）**——对过去的遗憾用 wish + had done / if only + had done；对现在的愿望用 wish + did；对将来用 wish + would。

**语法示例**
- I wish I had studied medicine.（我多希望当初学医。）
- If only I had known you were coming.（要是我早知道你要来就好了。）
- I wish you would trust me.（我希望你能信任我。）

## 五、分词结构

### 9. The Sun Having Set（太阳落山后）· 分词独立结构

The sun having set, we made our way home. All things considered, the trip was a great success. Weather permitting, we will go hiking tomorrow. The meeting being over, everyone left the room. His work finished, he went out for a walk. The guests having arrived, the dinner began.

太阳落山后，我们踏上了回家的路。全面考虑，这次旅行非常成功。如果天气允许，我们明天去远足。会议结束后，大家都离开了房间。工作完成后，他出去散步了。客人到齐后，晚宴开始了。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：make one's way（前进）、go hiking（去远足）、go for a walk（去散步）
- 词汇：permit（允许）、consider（考虑）、guest（客人）

**语法点睛**
**分词独立结构（absolute construction）**——「名词 + 分词」构成独立主格，作状语表时间、条件、原因等，其逻辑主语不同于主句主语。

**语法示例**
- Weather permitting, we will hold the picnic.（天气允许的话，我们就办野餐。）
- All things considered, we did well.（全面考虑，我们做得不错。）
- The rain having stopped, we continued our journey.（雨停了，我们继续旅程。）

### 10. Given the Circumstances（考虑到情况）· 分词短语（高级）

Given the circumstances, the result was remarkable. Considering the cost, the quality is excellent. Judging from his expression, he was not pleased. Taking everything into account, we should proceed. Provided with enough data, the team can work faster. Compared with last year, sales have doubled.

考虑到当时的情况，这个结果很了不起。考虑到成本，这个质量很出色。从他的表情判断，他并不高兴。把一切都考虑进去，我们应该继续。如果提供足够的数据，团队能更快工作。与去年相比，销售额翻了一番。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：take into account（考虑进去）、proceed（继续）
- 词汇：circumstances（情况）、remarkable（了不起）、expression（表情）、double（翻倍）

**语法点睛**
**分词短语（高级）**——Given/Considering/Judging from/Taking...into account 等固定分词短语作状语，表示「考虑到、从……判断」，常用于正式语体。

**语法示例**
- Given his age, he is very strong.（考虑到他的年龄，他很强壮。）
- Judging from the sky, it will rain.（从天色判断，要下雨了。）
- Taking all factors into account, the plan is sound.（考虑所有因素，计划是合理的。）

## 六、名词化与语体

### 11. The Pursuit of Happiness（追求幸福）· 名词化 nominalization

The pursuit of happiness is a universal goal. The introduction of new technology has transformed the industry. There has been a significant improvement in the quality of life. The reduction of pollution requires joint effort. The government announced the implementation of new policies. His refusal to compromise surprised everyone.

对幸福的追求是一个普遍的目标。新技术的引入已经改变了这个行业。生活质量有了显著改善。减少污染需要共同努力。政府宣布实施新政策。他拒绝妥协让所有人惊讶。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：improve the quality of life（改善生活质量）、implement policies（实施政策）
- 词汇：pursuit（追求）、universal（普遍）、improvement（改善）、implementation（实施）

**语法点睛**
**名词化 nominalization**——把动词或形容词变成名词，使表达更正式、抽象：pursue→pursuit、improve→improvement、reduce→reduction、implement→implementation。

**语法示例**
- The development of science changes society.（科学的发展改变社会。）
- There was a noticeable decline in sales.（销售额有明显的下降。）
- His decision to resign shocked everyone.（他辞职的决定让所有人震惊。）

### 12. Formal vs Informal（正式与非正式）· 语体/语域切换

In informal English, we say "a lot of"; in formal writing, we prefer "a considerable amount of". We can write "I think" in an email, but in a report we use "it is argued that". Colloquial expressions like "kids" become "children" in formal contexts. The phrase "put off" becomes "postpone" in academic writing. Informal "give up" becomes "abandon" in formal style.

在非正式英语中我们说 a lot of，在正式写作中我们更倾向用 a considerable amount of。邮件里可以写 I think，但报告里我们用 it is argued that。kids 这样的口语表达在正式语境中变成 children。短语 put off 在学术写作中变成 postpone。非正式的 give up 在正式文体中变成 abandon。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：academic writing（学术写作）
- 词汇：informal（非正式）、formal（正式）、colloquial（口语的）、postpone（推迟）、abandon（放弃）

**语法点睛**
**语体/语域切换**——同一意思在不同语境用不同语体：口语/中性词 vs 正式/学术词（kids→children、put off→postpone、give up→abandon、a lot of→a considerable amount of）。

**语法示例**
- Informal: I want to buy a car. Formal: I wish to purchase a vehicle.（口语：我想买车。正式：我欲购车。）
- Informal: get better. Formal: improve.（口语：变好。正式：改善。）
- Informal: kids. Formal: children.（口语：小孩。正式：儿童。）

## 七、省略与语篇

### 13. Omission and Substitution（省略与替代）· 省略与替代 ellipsis

She can speak French, and her brother can too. I don't like coffee, but my wife does. He was tired, but she wasn't. If possible, please reply by Friday. When in Rome, do as the Romans do. We should leave if necessary.

她会说法语，她哥哥也会。我不喜欢咖啡，但我妻子喜欢。他累了，但她没有。如果可能，请周五前回复。入乡随俗。如有必要，我们应该离开。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：reply by Friday（周五前回复）
- 词汇：substitution（替代）、ellipsis（省略）、necessary（必要）

**语法点睛**
**省略与替代 ellipsis/substitution**——用助动词（do/can/was）替代前面提到的内容避免重复；省略从句主语和 be 动词（if possible = if it is possible）。

**语法示例**
- I like tea, and she does too.（我喜欢茶，她也喜欢。）
- He can swim, but I can't.（他会游泳，但我不会。）
- If necessary, we will wait.（如有必要，我们会等。）

### 14. Furthermore, Moreover（此外）· 语篇衔接词（高级）

The plan is costly; furthermore, it is impractical. The policy has its benefits; nevertheless, it has drawbacks. The evidence is strong; therefore, we can trust it. In contrast, the second approach is far simpler. On the one hand, it saves time; on the other hand, it costs more. Consequently, the project was delayed.

这个计划成本高；此外，它也不切实际。这项政策有好处；然而，它也有缺点。证据很充分；因此，我们可以相信它。相比之下，第二种方法简单得多。一方面，它节省时间；另一方面，它成本更高。因此，项目被推迟了。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：save time（节省时间）、delay a project（推迟项目）
- 词汇：furthermore（此外）、nevertheless（然而）、therefore（因此）、consequently（因此）、in contrast（相比之下）

**语法点睛**
**语篇衔接词（高级）**——Furthermore（递进）、Nevertheless（转折）、Therefore/Consequently（因果）、In contrast（对比）、On the one hand...on the other hand（两面）。

**语法示例**
- The task is difficult; moreover, it is urgent.（任务艰巨，而且紧急。）
- The plan is risky; nevertheless, we should try.（计划有风险，然而我们应该尝试。）
- He was ill; consequently, he missed the exam.（他病了，因此错过了考试。）

## 八、平行与被动

### 15. Not Only... but Also（平行结构）· 平行结构

She is not only intelligent but also hard-working. The company aims not only to grow but also to give back to society. We should focus on both quality and efficiency. He is interested in reading, writing, and travelling. The policy seeks to reduce costs, to improve quality, and to expand access. To err is human; to forgive, divine.

她不仅聪明，而且勤奋。公司的目标不仅是发展，还要回馈社会。我们应该既关注质量也关注效率。他喜欢阅读、写作和旅行。这项政策旨在降低成本、提高质量、扩大准入。犯错是人，宽恕是神。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：give back to society（回馈社会）、reduce costs（降低成本）
- 词汇：intelligent（聪明）、efficiency（效率）、forgive（宽恕）

**语法点睛**
**平行结构**——并列成分语法形式一致：not only...but also 后接相同结构；并列动词用相同形式（to do...to do...to do）；并列名词短语保持对称。

**语法示例**
- She likes singing, dancing, and painting.（她喜欢唱歌、跳舞和画画。）
- The plan is both practical and affordable.（计划既实用又负担得起。）
- We aim to learn, to grow, and to contribute.（我们旨在学习、成长和贡献。）

### 16. The Report Has Been Published（报告已发布）· 高级被动

The report is said to have been published last week. The building is believed to have been built in the 18th century. The policy is expected to be implemented next year. It is reported that the agreement has been reached. The data is thought to have been analysed carefully. New measures are being taken to address the issue.

据说报告上周已经发布了。人们相信这座建筑建于 18 世纪。这项政策预计明年实施。据报道，协议已经达成。人们认为数据已经被仔细分析。正在采取新措施来解决这个问题。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：publish a report（发布报告）、reach an agreement（达成协议）、take measures（采取措施）
- 词汇：implement（实施）、analyse（分析）、address（解决）

**语法点睛**
**高级被动（have/get done + reporting passives）**——「be said/believed/thought to have been done」表示「据说已被……」；「be expected to be done」表示「预计被……」，是 reporting passive 与完成/不定式被动的结合。

**语法示例**
- The building is said to have been designed by a famous architect.（据说这栋楼由著名建筑师设计。）
- The law is expected to be passed soon.（这项法律预计很快通过。）
- It is believed that the treasure was buried here.（人们相信宝藏埋在这里。）
