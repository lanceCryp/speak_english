## 一、条件句系统

### 1. If I Had Studied Harder（如果我当时更努力学习）· 第三条件句

If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam. If she had left earlier, she would not have missed the train. If we had known the truth, we would have acted differently. He would have come if he had been invited. If it had not rained, the match would have continued.

如果我当时更努力学习，我就通过考试了。如果她早点出发，就不会错过火车。如果我们当时知道真相，我们就会采取不同的行动。如果他被邀请了，他就会来。如果当时没下雨，比赛就会继续。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：pass the exam（通过考试）、miss the train（错过火车）、know the truth（知道真相）
- 词汇：invite（邀请）、continue（继续）、differently（不同地）

**语法点睛**
**第三条件句**——if + 过去完成时（had done），主句 would have done，表示与过去事实相反的假设（已无法改变）。

**语法示例**
- If I had seen you, I would have said hello.（如果我当时看见你，我会打招呼。）
- She would have won if she had tried harder.（如果她当时更努力，她就赢了。）
- If we had left on time, we would not have been late.（如果我们准时出发，就不会迟到。）

### 2. If I Hadn't Missed the Train（如果我没错过火车）· 第三条件句（否定与疑问）

If I had not missed the train, I would have arrived on time. Had I known the answer, I would have told you. What would you have done if you had been in my place? If she had listened to my advice, she would not have lost so much money. Would you have helped him if he had asked?

如果我没错过火车，我就能准时到达了。如果我当时知道答案，我就告诉你了。如果你当时处在我这个位置，你会怎么做？如果她当时听了我的建议，就不会损失那么多钱。如果他当时请求，你会帮他吗？

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：miss the train（错过火车）、arrive on time（准时到达）、listen to advice（听建议）
- 词汇：in one's place（处在某人的位置）、advice（建议）

**语法点睛**
**第三条件句（否定与疑问）**——否定用 had not + done；正式文体可省略 if 倒装（Had I known = If I had known）；疑问句将 would/could 提前。

**语法示例**
- Had I known earlier, I would have come.（如果我早知道，我就会来。）
- If he had not helped me, I would have failed.（如果他没帮我，我就失败了。）
- What would you have said?（你当时会怎么说？）

### 3. A Mixed Situation（混合情况）· 混合条件句

If I had saved more money last year, I would be richer now. If she had taken the job, she would be living in Shanghai today. If I were more careful, I would not have made that mistake. He would not be so tired now if he had slept well last night. If we had planned better, we would not be in trouble now.

如果我去年多存点钱，我现在就会更富有。如果她当时接受了那份工作，她现在就住在上海了。如果我再细心一点，就不会犯那个错误。如果他昨晚睡得好，现在就不会这么累。如果我们当时计划得更好，现在就不会有麻烦。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：save money（存钱）、take a job（接受工作）、make a mistake（犯错）、be in trouble（有麻烦）
- 词汇：careful（细心）、plan（计划）

**语法点睛**
**混合条件句**——条件与结果跨越不同时间：过去条件 + 现在结果（had done → would do），或现在条件 + 过去结果（did → would have done）。

**语法示例**
- If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.（如果我当时学医，我现在就是医生了。）
- If she were honest, she would have told us.（如果她诚实，她当时就会告诉我们。）
- If I had caught the bus, I would not be late now.（如果我赶上了公交，现在就不会迟到。）

### 4. I Wish I Could Fly（我希望我能飞）· wish / if only

I wish I could speak three languages. I wish I had studied harder at school. She wishes she lived near the sea. I wish you would stop worrying. If only I had known the truth earlier. We wish we had not missed the concert.

我希望我会说三种语言。我多希望上学时更努力学习。她希望自己住在海边。我希望你别再担心了。要是我早点知道真相就好了。我们希望没有错过那场音乐会。

**词与短语**
- 动词短语：worry about（担心）
- 固定搭配：speak languages（说语言）、miss the concert（错过音乐会）、know the truth（知道真相）
- 词汇：wish（希望）、near（靠近）

**语法点睛**
**wish / if only**——表示对现状的不满或对过去的遗憾：wish + 过去时（对现在）；wish + 过去完成时（对过去）；wish + would（对将来的愿望）。

**语法示例**
- I wish I had more free time.（我希望我有更多空闲时间。）
- If only I had listened to you.（要是我当初听了你的话就好了。）
- I wish it would stop raining.（我希望雨能停。）

### 5. Unless You Try（除非你尝试）· unless / provided / as long as / in case

You will never succeed unless you try. Provided that you work hard, you will improve. I will come as long as I am free. Take an umbrella in case it rains. We will go ahead unless there are objections. Supposing you lost your job, what would you do?

除非你尝试，否则永远不会成功。只要你努力，你就会进步。只要我有空，我就会来。带把伞以防下雨。除非有人反对，否则我们就继续。假设你失业了，你会怎么办？

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：take an umbrella（带伞）、lose one's job（失业）
- 词汇：unless（除非）、provided that（只要）、as long as（只要）、in case（以防）、supposing（假设）

**语法点睛**
**条件替代式 unless/provided/as long as/in case/supposing**——unless = if...not（除非）；provided/as long as 表「只要」；in case 表「以防」；supposing 表「假设」。

**语法示例**
- You will not pass unless you study.（除非你学习，否则不会通过。）
- I will lend you the book provided you return it.（只要你归还，我就把书借给你。）
- Take your phone in case you get lost.（带上手机以防迷路。）

## 二、被动语态进阶

### 6. The Bridge Is Being Built（桥正在被建）· 被动语态（进行/完成被动）

A new bridge is being built across the river. The problem has been solved by the engineers. The house was being painted when I arrived. This road has been repaired recently. The results are being discussed at the meeting. A new hospital has been opened in our town.

一座新桥正在河上修建。这个问题已被工程师们解决。我到达时房子正在粉刷。这条路最近被修过了。结果正在会议上讨论。我们镇上开了一家新医院。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：build a bridge（建桥）、solve a problem（解决问题）
- 词汇：engineer（工程师）、repair（修理）、discuss（讨论）、recently（最近）

**语法点睛**
**被动语态（进行/完成被动）**——现在进行被动 is/are being + done；过去进行被动 was/were being + done；现在完成被动 has/have been + done。

**语法示例**
- The road is being repaired.（这条路正在维修。）
- The work has been finished.（工作已经完成了。）
- The room was being cleaned when I came in.（我进来时房间正在打扫。）

### 7. It Is Said That...（据说……）· reporting passives

It is said that the company will hire more workers. It is believed that the economy will improve. It is reported that the storm caused great damage. It is thought that the painting is worth millions. The actor is said to be very generous. It is known that exercise improves health.

据说公司要招聘更多员工。人们相信经济会好转。据报道，暴风雨造成了巨大损失。人们认为这幅画价值数百万。据说这位演员非常慷慨。众所周知，锻炼能改善健康。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：hire workers（招聘员工）、cause damage（造成损失）
- 词汇：economy（经济）、storm（暴风雨）、generous（慷慨）、worth（价值）

**语法点睛**
**reporting passives（It is said/believed/reported that...）**——用于转述普遍观点或报道，避免指明信息来源。也可用「主语 + be said to do」结构。

**语法示例**
- It is said that he is very rich.（据说他非常富有。）
- It is believed that the earth is round.（人们相信地球是圆的。）
- She is said to speak five languages.（据说她会说五种语言。）

### 8. I Had My Car Repaired（我让人修了车）· have/get something done

I had my car repaired last week. She is getting her hair cut tomorrow. We had our house painted last summer. He needs to get his phone fixed. I am having a new computer installed. They had the documents checked by a lawyer.

我上周让人修了车。她明天要去剪头发。我们去年夏天让人粉刷了房子。他需要让人修手机。我正在让人安装一台新电脑。他们让律师检查了文件。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：repair a car（修车）、cut hair（剪发）、paint a house（粉刷房子）
- 词汇：install（安装）、document（文件）、lawyer（律师）

**语法点睛**
**have/get something done**——表示「让别人为自己做某事」或「遭遇某事」。结构：have/get + 宾语 + 过去分词。

**语法示例**
- I had my watch repaired.（我让人修了表。）
- She got her teeth checked.（她让人检查了牙齿。）
- We are having the garden cleaned.（我们正让人清理花园。）

## 三、转述与推测

### 9. He Said He Had Seen It（他说他见过）· 间接引语（完整 backshift）

He said that he had seen the film. She told me she would be late. They said they were leaving the next day. He asked if I had finished the report. She told me she had been waiting for an hour. He asked me what I was doing.

他说他看过那部电影。她告诉我她会迟到。他们说他们第二天离开。他问我是否完成了报告。她告诉我她已经等了一个小时。他问我在做什么。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：see a film（看电影）、finish the report（完成报告）
- 词汇：report（报告）、the next day（第二天）、late（迟）

**语法点睛**
**间接引语（完整 backshift）**——转述时，时态整体后退：现在→过去、过去→过去完成、将来 will→would、现在完成→过去完成；时间词也要变（tomorrow→the next day）。

**语法示例**
- She said she had already eaten.（她说她已经吃过了。）
- He asked if I could help.（他问我能否帮忙。）
- They said they would arrive soon.（他们说很快就到。）

### 10. She Must Have Gone Home（她一定回家了）· 情态动词 + have done

She must have gone home early. He might have forgotten our meeting. They can't have finished the work already. You should have told me the truth. She may have taken the wrong bus. I could have helped you if you had asked.

她一定早早回家了。他可能忘了我们的会面。他们不可能已经完成了工作。你本应该告诉我真相。她可能坐错了公交车。如果你开口，我本可以帮你。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：go home early（早回家）、tell the truth（说真相）、take the wrong bus（坐错车）
- 词汇：meeting（会议）、forget（忘记）、already（已经）

**语法点睛**
**情态动词 + have done（推测过去）**——must have done 表「一定做过」；might/may have done 表「可能做过」；can't have done 表「不可能做过」；should have done 表「本该做而未做」。

**语法示例**
- He must have left already.（他一定已经离开了。）
- She might have missed the bus.（她可能错过了公交。）
- You should have called me.（你本应该给我打电话。）

## 四、从句与分词

### 11. My Brother, Who Lives Abroad（我住在国外的哥哥）· 非限制性定语从句

My brother, who lives in Canada, is coming home next month. The new museum, which opened last year, is very popular. My teacher, whose advice I value, encouraged me to apply. Beijing, where I was born, is a huge city. The project, which took two years, was a great success.

我哥哥住在加拿大，他下个月要回家了。新博物馆去年开放，非常受欢迎。我的老师鼓励我去申请，我非常珍视他的建议。北京是我出生的地方，是一座大城市。这个项目花了两年时间，非常成功。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：come home（回家）、apply for（申请）
- 词汇：abroad（在国外）、value（珍视）、encourage（鼓励）、success（成功）

**语法点睛**
**非限制性定语从句（which/who/whose/where）**——提供额外信息，用逗号隔开，去掉后句子仍完整。不能用 that 引导非限制性定语从句。

**语法示例**
- My car, which is ten years old, still runs well.（我的车十年了，仍运转良好。）
- Her sister, who is a doctor, lives in London.（她姐姐是医生，住在伦敦。）
- The book, whose author is famous, became a bestseller.（这本书作者很有名，成了畅销书。）

### 12. Walking in the Rain（在雨中行走）· 分词短语作状语

Walking in the rain, I thought about my future. Feeling tired, she went to bed early. Having finished my work, I went out for a walk. Seen from the hill, the city looks beautiful. Written in simple English, the book is easy to read. Not knowing what to say, he remained silent.

在雨中行走时，我想着我的未来。因为感到疲惫，她早早睡了。做完工作后，我出去散步了。从山上看，这座城市很美。这本书用简单英语写成，很容易读。因为不知道该说什么，他保持沉默。

**词与短语**
- 动词短语：go out（出门）、go for a walk（去散步）
- 固定搭配：go to bed early（早睡）、remain silent（保持沉默）
- 词汇：future（未来）、silent（沉默）、simple（简单）

**语法点睛**
**分词短语作状语**——现在分词（doing）表主动/同时进行；过去分词（done）表被动；having done 表「已完成且先于主句」。

**语法示例**
- Hearing the news, she burst into tears.（听到消息，她哭了。）
- Built in 1800, the house is very old.（这座房子建于 1800 年，很古老。）
- Having finished dinner, we went for a walk.（吃完晚饭，我们出去散步。）

## 五、倒装与强调

### 13. Not Only... But Also（不仅……而且）· 倒装 Not only

Not only did he pass the exam, but he also got the highest score. Not only is she talented, but she also works very hard. Not only has the city grown, but it has also become greener. Not only do I like the food, but I also enjoy the culture.

他不仅通过了考试，还得了最高分。她不仅有才华，而且非常努力。这座城市不仅变大了，还变得更绿了。我不仅喜欢这里的食物，还享受这里的文化。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：pass the exam（通过考试）、get the highest score（得最高分）
- 词汇：talented（有才华的）、culture（文化）、score（分数）

**语法点睛**
**倒装（Not only...but also）**——Not only 置于句首时，其后要用部分倒装（助动词提到主语前）：Not only did he... / Not only is she...

**语法示例**
- Not only did she win, but she also broke the record.（她不仅赢了，还打破了纪录。）
- Not only is he smart, but he is also kind.（他不仅聪明，而且善良。）
- Not only have we met, but we have become friends.（我们不仅见过，还成了朋友。）

### 14. It Was He Who Broke It（是他打破了它）· 强调句 It is...that

It was Tom who broke the window. It was last year that we first met. It is her advice that I always follow. It was in this café that they fell in love. It is not the money that matters, but the friendship. It was because of the rain that we stayed home.

是汤姆打破了窗户。我们初次见面是去年。我始终遵循的是她的建议。他们就是在这家咖啡馆相爱的。重要的不是钱，而是友谊。正是因为下雨，我们才待在家里。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：break the window（打破窗户）、fall in love（相爱）、stay home（待在家）
- 词汇：café（咖啡馆）、friendship（友谊）、matter（重要）

**语法点睛**
**强调句（It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who）**——用于强调句子某一成分。强调人用 who/that，其他用 that。

**语法示例**
- It was she who suggested the idea.（是她提出了这个想法。）
- It is on Sunday that we usually meet.（我们通常是周日见面。）
- It was the teacher that helped me most.（帮助我最多的是老师。）

### 15. What Matters Most（最重要的是）· 强调句 What...is

What matters most is your health. What I need now is a good rest. What surprised me was his calmness. What she wants is a simple life. What we should focus on is quality. What made him succeed was his patience.

最重要的是你的健康。我现在需要的是好好休息。让我惊讶的是他的冷静。她想要的是简单的生活。我们应该关注的是质量。让他成功的是他的耐心。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：have a rest（休息）、focus on（关注）
- 词汇：surprise（惊讶）、calmness（冷静）、patience（耐心）、succeed（成功）

**语法点睛**
**强调句（What...is/was）**——用 What 引导的名词性从句作主语，把要强调的内容放到句尾，即「What + 从句 + is/was + 强调内容」。

**语法示例**
- What I like most is reading.（我最喜欢的是阅读。）
- What we need is more time.（我们需要的是更多时间。）
- What impressed me was her honesty.（打动我的是她的诚实。）

## 六、虚拟与强调

### 16. If I Were to Win（如果我赢了）· 虚拟语气（基础）

If I were to win the lottery, I would travel the world. If the government were to invest more, education would improve. If you were to ask me, I would say yes. Were I in your position, I would accept the offer. If it were not for your help, we would have failed.

如果我中了彩票，我会环游世界。如果政府投资更多，教育就会改善。如果你问我，我会说好。如果我处在你的位置，我会接受这个提议。如果不是你的帮助，我们早就失败了。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：win the lottery（中彩票）、accept the offer（接受提议）、travel the world（环游世界）
- 词汇：invest（投资）、position（位置）、government（政府）

**语法点睛**
**虚拟语气（基础）**——If I were to do 表示对将来不太可能的假设；Were I... 是省略 if 的倒装；If it were not for... 表示「要不是」。

**语法示例**
- If I were you, I would wait.（如果我是你，我会等待。）
- Were she here, she would help us.（如果她在这里，她会帮我们。）
- If it were not for the rain, we would go out.（要不是下雨，我们就出门了。）

### 17. I Do Believe You（我确实相信你）· 强调 do/did

I do believe you are right. She does work very hard. He did tell me the truth. We do appreciate your help. It does make a difference. I did try my best, but it was not enough.

我确实相信你是对的。她确实非常努力。他确实告诉了我真相。我们确实感激你的帮助。这确实有影响。我确实尽力了，但还是不够。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：tell the truth（说真相）、make a difference（产生影响）、try one's best（尽力）
- 词汇：appreciate（感激）、enough（足够）、believe（相信）

**语法点睛**
**强调 do/did/does**——在肯定句中，用助动词 do/does/did + 动词原形，加强语气，表示「确实、的确」。

**语法示例**
- I do want to help you.（我确实想帮你。）
- She did call me last night.（她昨晚确实给我打了电话。）
- He does like coffee.（他确实喜欢咖啡。）

## 七、语篇与用词

### 18. First of All（首先）· 语篇标记

First of all, we need to understand the problem. Moreover, the cost is rising. However, there is another side to the issue. On the whole, the plan is a good one. In conclusion, we should act now. To sum up, quality is more important than quantity.

首先，我们需要理解这个问题。此外，成本在上升。然而，这个问题还有另一面。总体而言，这个计划是好的。总之，我们现在应该行动。概括来说，质量比数量更重要。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：understand the problem（理解问题）、act now（立即行动）
- 词汇：moreover（此外）、however（然而）、in conclusion（总之）、quantity（数量）、quality（质量）

**语法点睛**
**语篇标记**——用于组织篇章、衔接逻辑的词：First of all（首先）、Moreover（此外）、However（然而）、On the whole（总体）、In conclusion（总之）、To sum up（概括）。

**语法示例**
- First of all, let me introduce myself.（首先，让我自我介绍。）
- The plan is risky; however, it may work.（计划有风险，然而可能有效。）
- In conclusion, I agree with you.（总之，我同意你。）

### 19. Stop Worrying（别担心了）· 动名词 vs 不定式（stop/remember/forget）

I stopped worrying about things I cannot change. She stopped to buy some bread. Remember to lock the door when you leave. I remember meeting him at the party. I forgot to call my mother. I will never forget seeing the sea for the first time.

我不再为无法改变的事情担心了。她停下来去买面包。离开时记得锁门。我记得在聚会上见过他。我忘了给妈妈打电话。我永远不会忘记第一次看见大海。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：lock the door（锁门）、meet someone（遇见某人）
- 词汇：remember（记得）、forget（忘记）、change（改变）

**语法点睛**
**stop/remember/forget + doing vs to do**——stop doing（停止做）、stop to do（停下来去做）；remember/forget doing（记得/忘记做过）、remember/forget to do（记得/忘记去做）。

**语法示例**
- I stopped smoking last year.（我去年戒烟了。）
- Remember to bring your book.（记得带书。）
- I remember visiting this place before.（我记得以前来过这里。）

### 20. A Well-Known Writer（一位知名作家）· 复合形容词

She is a well-known writer around the world. He is a hard-working student. This is a ten-page report. We stayed in a five-star hotel. She gave a thought-provoking speech. I bought a second-hand car. He is a good-looking young man.

她是全世界知名的一位作家。他是一个勤奋的学生。这是一份十页的报告。我们住在一家五星级酒店。她做了一场发人深省的演讲。我买了一辆二手车。他是个英俊的年轻人。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：give a speech（发表演讲）、buy a car（买车）
- 词汇：well-known（知名的）、hard-working（勤奋的）、thought-provoking（发人深省的）、second-hand（二手的）

**语法点睛**
**复合形容词**——由两个或多个词用连字符连接，作定语修饰名词：well-known、hard-working、ten-page（数词+单数名词）、five-star、second-hand。

**语法示例**
- She is a well-known scientist.（她是一位知名科学家。）
- This is a six-year-old child.（这是一个六岁的孩子。）
- He is a hard-working student.（他是一个勤奋的学生。）

## 八、综合进阶

### 21. The More We Learn（我们学得越多）· 比较结构（进阶）

The more we learn, the more we realize how little we know. The harder you work, the better the result will be. The sooner we start, the sooner we will finish. It is getting more and more expensive to live in the city. This problem is no less serious than the last one.

我们学得越多，就越意识到自己知道得很少。你越努力，结果就越好。我们开始得越早，就结束得越早。在城市生活越来越贵。这个问题和上一个同样严重。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：realize the truth（认识到真相）、get more expensive（变贵）
- 词汇：realize（意识到）、result（结果）、serious（严重）、expensive（贵）

**语法点睛**
**比较结构（进阶）**——「the + 比较级, the + 比较级」表「越……越……」；「比较级 and 比较级」表「越来越」；no less...than 表「同样」。

**语法示例**
- The more you practise, the better you become.（你练习越多，就越好。）
- It is becoming harder and harder to find time.（时间越来越难挤出来。）
- She is no less capable than her brother.（她和哥哥一样能干。）

### 22. Society in Change（变化中的社会）· 被动 + 分词综合

Society is being changed by technology. New ideas are constantly being introduced. Having grown up with the internet, young people think differently. Faced with new challenges, we must adapt quickly. The old ways, replaced by new ones, are disappearing. It is believed that this trend will continue.

社会正在被科技改变。新观念不断被引入。伴随着互联网长大，年轻人的思维方式不同。面对新挑战，我们必须迅速适应。旧的方式正被新的取代，逐渐消失。人们相信这一趋势将持续下去。

**词与短语**
- 固定搭配：think differently（不同地思考）、face challenges（面对挑战）、adapt quickly（迅速适应）
- 词汇：constantly（不断地）、trend（趋势）、disappear（消失）、adapt（适应）

**语法点睛**
**被动 + 分词综合**——将进行被动（is being changed）、having done（已完成）、过去分词短语（faced with、replaced by）、reporting passive（It is believed）综合运用，形成 B2 级的复杂表达。

**语法示例**
- The problem is being solved by experts.（问题正由专家解决。）
- Faced with difficulties, she never gave up.（面对困难，她从不放弃。）
- Having finished the project, they celebrated.（完成项目后，他们庆祝了一番。）
